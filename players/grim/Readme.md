# Grim: Document Guide

This directory contains reference material and campaign-planning notes related to Grim (Amara Mistweaver).

## Story Snapshot (DM-Facing)

> This snapshot combines established character facts with campaign hooks. Treat planned revelations as DM notes, not as information Grim or the other players already know.

- **Main goal:** Follow the path into Barovia and find her son Milo, keeping faith that he can be brought home.
- **Most distinguishing feature:** Grim is a cautious, quick-witted veteran adventurer who carries a flesh pouch with a steady heartbeat and is accompanied by a Living Shadow.
- **Story and NPC hooks:** The pouch and shadow make her bargain hard to hide, while her old adventuring nickname, silver tongue, and Will's Clockwork Amulet can prompt questions or recognition. Her unresolved fight with a hag coven is a possible connection to Barovia's hags; the link between that history and Milo is DM-planned. See [Backstory](backstory.md) and [DM Plans](dm_plans.md).
