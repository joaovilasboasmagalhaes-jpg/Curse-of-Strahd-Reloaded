# Character

The adventuring life had been good to Grim - not many could say they pocketed most of the gold and settled down early. She had been lucky to find such a stable crew, keen to take it slow and steady, and most importantly, safely. They had their misfortunes, they had lost some people along the years, but all in all it had been a decent success.

Settling down had come very naturally to her, tending to her own home in the outskirts of a nice town she had quickly grown fonder of growing things instead of butchering them. Of course, her outings and meetups with the old lads always brought back memories, their hard battles still being worthy of a song, but what she wanted now was to have a family - to be a parent.

What she wanted is precisely what she got - Will had always made that extra effort with her, and she fell for the smith quickly. Not too long after, at least in her Half-Elf eyes, their child had arrived. Milo had gotten lucky - his face was just like his mother's, but his mind was growing to be cunning like his father.

It had been nearly 30 years of this peaceful life for Grim, but things never stay quiet in a world like this. After a storm raged unstoppably for weeks on end, a mist had slowly seeped into the town, growing like mold and thickening until one couldn't see further than their own eyes.

The screaming began when she had been away from home. Her doors were smashed to kindling, far away she could see Will's body being held, his neck snapped at a wrong angle, behind him Milo slowly getting swept deeper and deeper into the mist. The hulking figure holding Will, just a blur of a shadow in her eyes.

She ran, but the mists became more and more solid, and then her ankles were being held by her very own shadow, it slowly creeping from under her, enveloping her legs tighter and tighter.

[This is her Dark Gift manifesting - Living Shadow.]

The thing extended a hand and placed the flesh pouch into Grim's trembling fingers. Inside, a heartbeat. Slow. Steady. Alive. Then it spoke:

> "The child lives."
> 

The heartbeat continued.

> "Beyond the Mists."
> 

It looked toward the fog.

> "You cannot reach him."
> 

A pause.

> "But I can show you the path."
> 

The shadow around Grim's feet tightened.

> "Carry his heart."
"Follow the path."
"Do not turn away from him."
> 

The heartbeat echoed inside the pouch.

> "So long as you seek him, the bond remains."
"Abandon him, and he will be lost to you."
> 

The creature pointed into the mist.

> "Go to the wanderers with the painted wagons."
"They know the road."
> 

Then the shadow rose and wrapped around her own.

> "Accept this, and the path opens."
>

[A deal was made here with the hulking shadow - she was given A pouch made of flesh, with a sinew drawstring. Inside, the beating heart of her child, the only proof he is still alive. This deal also gives her directions - to meet the Vistani, who will then take her into the mists of Barovia.]

Details about Grim:

- Her real name is Amara Mistweaver - Grim is her nickname from her adventuring days with her old crew. She's about 90 to 100 years old.
- She'd been known for being overly cautious - most of the times her crew backed off from something had been after she had given them a chewing. Scaredy Grim, fortunately or not, had become a staple in some of their songs. In her eyes, what others sometimes saw as cowardice, she saw as simply not being stupid.
- Quick wit and a silver tongue helped her fork over some deals and weasel her and her crew out of some shady situations. The lads always sang their praises over the time she convinced a Bull Troll that he was, in fact, intruding in his own home, and left to find a new cave to live in.
- After settling down with Will, she'd grown more confident somehow, the simple lifestyle had made her somewhat bolder. Sometimes she'd brood over the past, thinking that perhaps she could have taken a bit more risk on some jobs - maybe she'd have avoided some deaths, maybe she could have kept more friends.
- The worst fight she's ever had was on a swamp against a group of hags - some of the crew died, and they had to run away. She never found out what happened to their bodies and it haunts her to this day.
- Will died at 48 - they'd been together for 23 years. He'd been a Smith, and know her for a while longer, at the end of her adventuring days her crew usually came to the shop he worked at to sort things out. He'd gifted her a Clockwork Amulet, which she still kept in perfect shape with her at all times.
- Her father left her early on, he saw a Half-Elf as not pure enough and his time with her mother as a mistake. Her mom raised her into the cunning, witty woman she is. She has fond memories of her mother, and has never bothered to meet her father again.
- Milo is her first born and just about 13 years of age. Grim and Will had been hoping for more kids once he'd gotten a bit older, perhaps when he reached adulthood. They called him a half Half-Elf, as his ears were barely any pointier than a human's.
