## Observed Play

- **Session 2, Death House treasure:** Grim used her shadow to retrieve an item from the chest while keeping herself behind a barricade ([Session 2](../../sessions/Session%202/log.md), cult master's chamber).
- **Session 3, ritual decision:** Grim argued for the ritual as a possible way out and later tried to kill the creature on the altar in case that changed the outcome; she stopped when the fight became too dangerous ([Session 3](../../sessions/Session%203/log.md), ritual chamber).
- **Session 4, Village of Barovia:** Grim helped residents practically, asked about recent events, and later challenged Donavich's faith after Elga had encouraged him ([Session 4](../../sessions/Session%204/log.md), town square and church).
- **Session 8, Tser Pool:** Grim asked about the drowned town, drew a conclusion about her son's kidnapping, and discussed desperate possible choices, including seeking Strahd's help ([Session 8](../../sessions/Session%208/log.md), private reading and party discussion).

## Working Hypotheses

- **Hypothesis:** Grim's player may enjoy pursuing personal leads through direct questions and follow-up with NPCs.
  - **Confidence:** Medium; unconfirmed.
  - **Evidence:** Grim sought information from residents in Session 4, questioned Rahadin in Session 6 and Doru in Session 7, and used her Session 8 reading to investigate the drowned town ([Session 4](../../sessions/Session%204/log.md); [Session 6](../../sessions/Session%206/log.md); [Session 7](../../sessions/Session%207/log.md); [Session 8](../../sessions/Session%208/log.md)).
  - **Alternatives / counterevidence:** These questions closely serve Grim's urgent character goal; no log says the player enjoyed the conversations as a play style.
  - **Preparation implication / check:** Put optional, actionable follow-up questions within reach of important NPCs and note whether the player pursues them when they are not tied to Grim's immediate objective.
- **Hypothesis:** The player may be interested in consequential choices with strategic and moral tradeoffs.
  - **Confidence:** Low; unconfirmed.
  - **Evidence:** Grim advocated trying the ritual in Session 3 and considered dangerous bargains in Session 8 ([Session 3](../../sessions/Session%203/log.md); [Session 8](../../sessions/Session%208/log.md)).
  - **Alternatives / counterevidence:** The choices were motivated by Grim's stated mission and the group's immediate need to escape; they do not establish a general preference for morally dark content.
  - **Preparation implication / check:** When appropriate, provide more than one viable path with visible consequences; keep any such choice optional and ask whether that kind of dilemma works for the player.
