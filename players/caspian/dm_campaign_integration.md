# Caspian: Campaign Integration

> [!warning] Mutable DM document
> This document contains campaign-facing consequences, act-by-act DM hooks, and implementation notes for integrating Caspian into *Curse of Strahd: Reloaded*. For mutable DM lore on Ashcairn, the Order of the Blades, and Ravka Ashclaw, see [DM Plans](dm_plans.md).

## Campaign Throughline

Caspian's personal story enters the campaign in stages, running parallel to Ivan's:

1. Early wolf encounters read as *wrong* to Caspian — too disciplined, too coordinated for ordinary infected lycanthropes — without revealing why yet.
2. Rumors in Vallaki connect recent werewolf aggression to a change in pack leadership, sharpening Caspian's hunt.
3. Arc L reveals that the pack responsible for Ashcairn's destruction is the same one holding Ivan's family, and that Ravka Ashclaw personally led the raid.
4. Arc L and Arc Q create a direct, ongoing tension between Caspian (who wants the pack destroyed) and Ivan (who wants his family saved).
5. Arc L also reveals that some of Ashcairn's taken children survived, newly cursed — turning Caspian's fight with Ravka into a rescue against a ticking clock, not only a reckoning.
6. Arc Q gives Caspian his reckoning with Ravka alongside Ivan's confrontation with Kiril.
7. Arc S and the climax pay off Caspian's Shadowfell nature as something distinct from the werewolf plot entirely.

## Act I: Early Foreshadowing

### Arc A: Escape From Death House

- Death House sits on unusually thin, shadow-soaked ground. Give Caspian a brief, unexplained sensory beat here (a pressure behind the eyes, a taste of ash) — the first quiet hint of his [Shadowfell Resonance](dm_plans.md), well before anyone can connect it to anything.

### Arc C: Into the Valley

- Arturi's werewolf manuscript describes ordinary infected lycanthropes. Caspian can notice the account doesn't match what happened at Ashcairn — the attackers moved with purpose and discipline, not mindless savagery. Keep this as an unresolved observation; do not explain the Wolfir/inherited-lycanthropy distinction yet (that revelation belongs to Ivan, per [Ivan's Campaign Integration](../ivan/dm_campaign_integration.md)).
- Any wolves or Strahd's servants who react oddly to Ivan (per Ivan's Act I notes) should not simultaneously react to Caspian. Keep the two threads visibly separate until Arc L.

## Act II: Rumors and Connections

### Arc E: The Missing Vistana

- When Kasimir and Szoldar describe the pack's recent shift toward more aggressive, centralized behavior under new leadership, Caspian should seize on this immediately as the first real lead on Ashcairn's destruction — in contrast to Ivan, who receives the same information with dread rather than eagerness.
- Do not let this scene name Kiril or reveal the Petrovich/Venatricho history. It should read only as "the pack has a new, harsher leader."

## Act III: The Werewolf Climax

### Arc L: The Den of Wolves

This arc is the first major revelation for both Caspian and Ivan, and should be prepped as a single scene with two payoffs.

- Caspian recognizes the den's territory, scent-markers, or captured plunder as matching what was taken from Ashcairn.
- **Ravka Ashclaw** is present in the den (see [DM Plans](dm_plans.md)) and can be identified — by Caspian's own tracking, by a captive's testimony, or by her own boasting — as the raid's leader. She should not be shy about it; she is proud of what she did.
- The den also holds Ashcairn's taken children, newly cursed with lycanthropy but not yet past their first transformation (see [DM Plans](dm_plans.md)). Finding them alive reframes the encounter: defeating Ravka before the next full moon is the surest way to lift their curse, giving Caspian a rescue to complete, not only a kill to make.
- This creates the arc's central party tension: Caspian wants the pack (Ravka above all) destroyed outright, while Ivan is simultaneously discovering that this pack holds his father Skennis, his brother Emil, and the remnants of his own clan. Some of the werewolves Caspian wants dead are the very people Ivan is trying to save.
  - Let the party navigate this rather than resolving it for them. Ravka herself is not sympathetic and can be a clean target for Caspian without forcing a fight against Ivan's family — but incidental pack members present in the den are a genuine point of friction.
- Ravka should not die accidentally offscreen; reserve her for a personal confrontation with Caspian, ideally while Ivan is occupied with Skennis or Emil elsewhere in the den, so both payoffs can land without competing for the table's attention.

### Arc Q: A Shining Beacon

- If Ravka survived Arc L, this is her last stand alongside Kiril. She should fight to prove herself to him one final time, giving Caspian the reckoning Arc L may have denied him.
- Kiril's confrontation remains primarily Ivan's scene (per [Ivan's Campaign Integration](../ivan/dm_campaign_integration.md)); Caspian's role here is closing his own account with Ravka, not competing for Kiril's narrative focus.
- If any Ashcairn children were rescued but not yet cured in Arc L, resolve whether the party found a way to lift their curse before the next full moon — this can land here or slightly earlier, but should not be left open indefinitely.

## Act IV: Secrets of the Ancient

### Arc S: A Sword of Sunlight (The Amber Temple)

- The vestiges sense that Caspian already carries a shard of the Shadowfell in his blood and single him out with a more personal, more insistent temptation than the rest of the party (see [Shadowfell Resonance](dm_plans.md)). This is unconnected to Ravka or Ashcairn — it is about who Caspian is, not what happened to him.

## Climax & Epilogue

### The Grand Conjunction

- Strahd's plan to tear the Mists open manipulates the same planar seam Caspian has lived beside his whole life. He should be the character who first notices something is wrong with the fabric of the Mists as the ritual nears completion, giving him a meaningful, non-combat way to help the party understand or disrupt it.

## Implementation Checklist

- [ ] Keep all definitive Ashcairn/Ravka history in [DM Plans](dm_plans.md).
- [ ] Keep Caspian's and Ivan's early wolf-related foreshadowing threads separate through Act I and Act II.
- [ ] Prepare Kasimir/Szoldar's rumor scene in Arc E to land differently for Caspian (eager) than for Ivan (dreadful).
- [ ] Prepare Ravka's identification and confrontation in Arc L as a scene that runs alongside, not instead of, Ivan's family reveal.
- [ ] Decide whether incidental pack members create an active conflict between Caspian and Ivan during Arc L, or remain a background tension the players resolve themselves.
- [ ] Prepare the rescued Ashcairn children as a ticking-clock objective in Arc L, and decide their fate (cured, lost, or left open) no later than Arc Q.
- [ ] Prepare Ravka's return (or death recap) for Arc Q if she survives the den.
- [ ] Prepare Caspian's personalized Amber Temple temptation in Arc S.
- [ ] Prepare Caspian's Shadowfell-resonance beat for the Grand Conjunction climax.
