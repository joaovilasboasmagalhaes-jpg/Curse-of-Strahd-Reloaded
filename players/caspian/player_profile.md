# Player Profile: 08 / Caspian

DM-only. Draft based on Caspian's questionnaire. The player's name is not recorded here; unanswered preferences are left open rather than inferred.

## Player

- **What they enjoy at the table** (combat, roleplay, puzzles, exploration, politics, humor, drama): Comfortable with horror and terror. Open to broad themes when handled seriously and relevant to the plot. Prefers a serious campaign with dedicated moments for relaxing and lighter interaction.
- **What they dislike or avoid:** No specific themes were named as off-limits in the individual questionnaire. Their broad answer remains subject to the table's agreed boundaries, including no described sexual violence.
- **How they play** (decisive, cautious, chatty, quiet, in-character, tactical): Not established. The questionnaire describes Caspian's willpower and judgment, not the player's table style.
- **Spotlight preference** (likes center stage, or prefers small personal moments): Not established.

Session 0 notes, from the [questionnaire](../../sessions/Session%200/session_0_questionnaire.csv): The player prefers mostly easy fights, some hard encounters, and rare TPKs. The table-wide agreement is more demanding (few easy fights, most fights hard, with TPK a real risk), so this is a recorded preference difference, not a change to the campaign contract. They prefer turns to be ready and brief, with one or two sentences of strategic value; they noted that even two minutes may be too long unless the previous player's action changes the situation. They also agreed that PvP requires in-character consent from both players, matching the table rule.

## Character

- **What the player has said they want from the story:** Caspian's backstory should matter in play. The player would like his story to connect to the mystery of the party members' differing dates, if that mystery is used. They also want a genuinely hopeful outcome in which Caspian helps free Barovia from Strahd and can return to rebuild his home.
- **What the player has reacted to strongly at the table** (cite `Session N`): No clear reactions are documented in the questionnaire. Do not infer a preference from the session logs without a specific example.
- **Things they asked about or followed up on:** The player asked for clarification about Ashcairn, suggesting the name or its relevance was not clear to them. They also raised the party's differing dates and want that mystery explained if introduced.
- **Hopes for the ending:** Caspian helps the group free Barovia from Strahd's threat, then returns to rebuild his home. The player calls this a good ending.
- **Lines not to cross:** No individual content prohibition was named. The player accepts broad themes when handled seriously and kept relevant to the plot, within the table's agreed boundaries. PvP requires both involved players' in-character consent. The player also expects any introduced mystery, including the differing dates, to receive an explanation; they would like Caspian's story connected to it, though they are unsure how.

## Notes

- The player describes Caspian's willpower as a strength and his lack of real-world experience as a weakness. They suggest he may be afraid of werewolves and, more deeply, of failing to kill Strahd and regain sovereignty over his home; the deeper fear is tentative, not confirmed.
- The player says Caspian listens to experienced people when their reasoning makes sense, and may reconsider beliefs after experiencing the consequences of his actions. Treat this as character guidance, not a confirmed preference for how the player likes consequences handled.
- The player wants Caspian to protect the dignity of his birthplace and restore safety for its people. They connect killing Strahd with regaining his people's trust and returning to rule by birthright.
- The answers do not establish which scene types the player enjoys, how much spotlight they prefer, or what they want left undefined.
- Session 0 includes a numeric answer for campaign focus, but the scale is not defined in the questionnaire, so it is not interpreted here. The group's agreed focus is balanced between combat and roleplay, with a slight lean toward guided story and room for exploration and decisions.

## Working Hypotheses

- **Hypothesis:** The player may prefer time to review layered clues before committing to an interpretation or question.
  - **Confidence:** Low; unconfirmed.
  - **Evidence:** Caspian's question was deferred until the player could review Madam Eva's sign in Session 8; the existing questionnaire-derived profile also records a desire for mysteries the campaign introduces to receive explanations.
  - **Alternatives / counterevidence:** The log says he had trouble following the reading, so the delay may reflect comprehension or memory rather than a general preference. His stated wish for mysteries to be explained is explicit story feedback, not proof of this hypothesis.
