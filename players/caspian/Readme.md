# Caspian: Document Guide

This directory contains reference material and campaign-planning notes related to Caspian.

## Story Snapshot (DM-Facing)

> This snapshot combines established character facts with campaign hooks. Treat planned revelations as DM notes, not as information Caspian or the other players already know.

- **Main goal:** Find the werewolves who destroyed his home in Ashcairn, learn the fate of the children taken in the raid, and bring the attackers to justice.
- **Most distinguishing feature:** Caspian is a Shadar-kai trained at the Order of the Blades, combining swordsmanship with battle magic.
- **Story and NPC hooks:** His family's deaths and the taken children give the werewolf trail personal stakes. He remembers an old academy text mentioning Strahd and a colorful people, and may notice that the attackers behaved with unusual discipline. Ashcairn's Shadowfell connection and a faint resonance are optional DM-planned hooks. See [Backstory](backstory.md), [DM Plans](dm_plans.md), and [Campaign Integration](dm_campaign_integration.md).
