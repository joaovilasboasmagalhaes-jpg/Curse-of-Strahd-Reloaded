# Caspian's Background: Ashcairn and the Order

### Ashcairn, your home

Ashcairn is a small, secluded shadar-kai village near Daggerford, far from Barovia. Its lasting grey half-light and ash-colored stone were ordinary parts of home to you.

Ashcairn's people are wary and self-reliant. They hunt, quarry shadow-glass from the surrounding land, and trade sparingly with Daggerford. It is a village, not a city, and its people have had to rely on one another to keep it safe.

### The Order of the Blades

You were sent away from Ashcairn to study at the Order of the Blades, an academy for shadar-kai magi. Its tradition combines swordsmanship with battle magic. In its old archive, you once encountered a fragmentary account of a conqueror named Strahd and a strange, colorful people associated with him. The text was obscure, and you do not remember enough to identify those people or know what their connection to Strahd was.

### What you know about the attack

You were away studying when Ashcairn was attacked. By the time you returned, your family's home had been destroyed and your relatives were dead. The village had survived, but only barely; guards from a nearby town had driven the attackers away before the whole settlement was lost.

Survivors told you the attackers were werewolves. Some children had been taken; others had been killed. They also said the attackers spoke of serving “Devil Strahd.”

You followed the raiders' old trail toward a nearby town, but arrived after they had gone. There, you saw a colorful caravan that reminded you of the people in the academy text. One traveler watched you for a moment and smiled before the caravan headed into the forest.
