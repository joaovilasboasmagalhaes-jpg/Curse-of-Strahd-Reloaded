# Caspian: Character Check-In

These questions are a chance to revisit Caspian and what you would like from his story as the campaign develops. Short answers are fine, and you can skip anything.

1. **What matters most to Caspian?** What person, place, belief, or ideal would he risk a lot to protect?
2. **What are Caspian's greatest strength and greatest flaw?** How do they show up in the way he acts?
3. **What does Caspian fear most, and what does he do when that fear is tested?**
4. **What does Caspian want right now?** What does he believe will happen if he gets it?
5. **What does Caspian believe happened at Ashcairn?** Which parts does he know, and which parts is he assuming?
6. **What could make Caspian reconsider something he feels certain about?**
7. **What would you like to see Caspian face or discover during the campaign?** Is there anything about his past or identity you would rather leave undefined?
8. **What would feel like a satisfying resolution for Caspian?** Is there a particular kind of change, achievement, or ending you hope for?
9. **Is there anything you'd like me to handle carefully or avoid in his story?**
