# Game of Stories: Caspian

> **Canon backstory.** This is the recorded version prepared for the Session 9 Game of Stories. The telling at the table may have differed.

When he was a child, Caspian used to be the annoyance of the guards at Fortress Volkgorod (His family's estate in Umbralax). Not that there were any forbidden places for the to-be lord, but surely there were more appropriate places for a small boy to be than the War Room filled with iron plates, steel maces and all the pointy things that could hurt him.
And yet, due to his arcanistic affinity, there Caspian went, teleporting through walls into state affair meetings, jumping 10 foot high up in the air through ceiling windows assisted by gusts of destructive wind that would wreck entire harvest seasons, and even one time made himself invisble for a fortnight. His mother was deeply worried about this last one.
One day, it was my turn to bear witness to his mischievous ways. I was in the library, when all of the sudden, I hear the whistlings of a wind draft by my ears. Thought maybe one of the Sages had left a window open, but I was about to be caught by surprise by the gravitational wave that came after it, making all objects in the room be violently shaken and remain afloat (myself included). IT TOOK FOUR MAGI ORDER TAUGHT SORCERERS TO COUNTERSPELL THE RASCAL. Obviously our library was in disarray after the event.
As a way to correct him, I made it his punishment that he would be the one to clean everything up, and with a catch: He would write down the name of each book he picked up and read it through and provide me with a report on it. The list was over 300 books long and went over a myriad of topics : from the fine arts, to cooking, to the war on the Middle Provinces.
Over the course of the following years, we always saw Caspian with a book in hand wherever we went. Reports piled up on my desk and, at the start they were the ramblings of a boy that was upset at his punishment. But they soon began to become more and more thorough. He was particularly delicate with World History topics, I had reasons to believe it was his favourite subject to read and write on. Once, when in doubt of a topic I knew he had read upon, even called him to my Inner Circle meeting to assist me.
Seeking and sharing knowledge stopped being the hassle he once dreaded and began to be his passion. He corrected the swordmasters on the theoretical postures of the fencing arts, cooks on the spice usage, and historians on their world reports. But theory alone was starting to bore young Caspian. So when he asked to go to the Academy, to supplement research with field expertise, I was conflicted but knew this was his first step into his journey to adulthood and lordship.
Now, completing his first year at the academy, I am happy to celebrate his summer return with this feast that honors the path that led him here. Caspian, as your father, I am so proud…
*The speech stops here and has a blood soaked ending to the pages. It was meant for the feast his father was organizing in Caspian's honor, for completing a first year as one of the Academy's
finest. It was never finished, presumably due to the attack on Umbralax, and was found in his fathers pockets in the wreckage*
