When word reaches you that your home village has been attacked, you leave the academy immediately. The journey takes longer than it should. Roads are blocked, travelers avoid the region, and every day spent on the road gives you more time to imagine what you might find when you arrive.

The truth is worse.

Several homes were destroyed during the attack, including your family's. You find your relatives dead inside, their bodies already beginning to decay. Whatever happened here is long over. The village survived, but barely. Guards from a nearby town arrived in time to drive the attackers away before the entire settlement was lost.

The survivors speak of werewolves. Some children were taken. Others were killed. The attackers spoke of serving “Devil Strahd,” though most dismiss it as madness or superstition. You only remember the name faintly from an old academy text mentioning a conqueror named Strahd. In this text there was also references to a strange group of colorful people associated with him, though you can’t recall the details clearly.

You remain in the village for a time, searching for answers. You pick up the trail left by the werewolves and follow it into the wilderness. The trail is old and difficult to track, but it leads toward a nearby town.

By the time you arrive, the werewolves are long gone.

Instead, you see something familiar.

A colorful caravan has just stopped along the road. The travelers remind you of the people described in the ancient text.

One of them looks up at you as they prepare to leave. For a moment, their attention lingers—calm, deliberate—before they offer a small smile, like they were expecting you.

Then the caravan starts moving again, heading into the nearby forest.

If the trail you’re following has gone cold, this may be the only direction left.
