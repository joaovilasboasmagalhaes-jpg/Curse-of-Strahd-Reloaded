# Vampire Details

Agni is a hybrid vampire, a Dhampir. He did not go the full process of becoming a vampire, but has been affected enough to walk the same path. He is also a bloodhunter of the Undying Thirst, which also gives him vampiric abilities. Some abilities from this class overlap with the racial features, so a fusion has been done between the two. Furthermore, it will be interesting to make a thirst/hunger system regarding his vampiric nature, something to make him feed regularly.

This page will serve as a guide to the details of Agni’s vampiric nature.

The following features that Agni gets access to are replaced by the ones in this guide:

- [Race] Vampiric Bite

## Climbing Speed

You gain a climbing speed equal to your walking speed. At 5th level, you can move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and upside down along ceilings, while leaving your hands free.

## Vampiric Nature

Whenever you use Hunter's Bane to analyze an Undead, you can add one roll of your Rite Die to the check.

## Fangs

The ancient disease of your Order has irreparably changed your physiology. Your teeth become elongated fangs which you can use for unarmed strikes. You have advantage on the attack if you are grappling the creature. On a hit, your Fangs deal necrotic damage equal to your Rite Die + your Strength or Dexterity modifier. 

## Vampiric Drain

You can drain life from your foes. When you hit a creature with a Fang attack, you can immediately use your Bonus Action to drain their life force. You can make this decision after rolling for damage but before removing your fangs. You regain hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt by your Fangs. If your hit point maximum has been reduced, it also increases by the same amount, up to its normal maximum.

You can drain foes this way a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier (a minimum of once), and you regain all uses when you finish a long rest.

## Blood Sense

Your vampiric instincts awaken, allowing you to perceive the lifeblood of nearby creatures.

While Blood Sense is active, you can sense the presence of all **living creatures** within 30 feet of you, even if they are behind total cover or invisible. You know the exact location of each creature, whether it is **bloodied**, and which living creature within range has the **lowest current hit points**. This sense does not reveal a creature's appearance or identity.

### Predatory Focus

While Blood Sense is active, if at least one **bloodied** creature is within range, you have disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures that aren't bloodied.

### Blood Frenzy

The scent of freshly spilled blood threatens to overwhelm your self-control.

Whenever a living creature that you can sense with Blood Sense becomes **bloodied** for the first time, you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw.

**Save DC = 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Constitution modifier.**

On a failed save, you must immediately use your reaction, if available, to move up to half your speed toward that creature by the shortest safe route. If the creature is within your reach at the end of this movement, you must make one melee weapon attack against it.

# Thirst

Your vampiric nature grants you an insatiable thirst for blood. You no longer require food or water to survive and instead you gain a pool of Thirst Points (TP). You have a total of TP equal to your Blood Hunter level × 6.

Every day at dawn, you lose a number of Thirst Points equal to your Blood Hunter level.

## Stages

As you lose or gain thirst points, you go through different stages which can boon or bane you.

| Thirst Points per level | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| 1-2 | **Thirsty** |
| 3-4 | **Composed** |
| 5-6 | **Sated** |

As an example, a level 5 Blood Hunter would have a maximum of 30 Thirst Points and would be Thirsty with 1-10 TP, Composed with 11-20 TP and Sated with 21-30 TP.

### Thirsty

Your hunger dominates your instincts.

- **Blood Sense** is always active and cannot be suppressed.
- **Predatory Focus** applies.
- **Blood Frenzy** applies.
- You have **disadvantage on Dexterity and Charisma ability checks.**

### Composed

You and the Beast exist in balance.

- As a **bonus action**, you activate Blood Sense for **1 minute**.
- **Predatory Focus** applies while Blood Sense is active.
- **Blood Frenzy** applies while Blood Sense is active.

### Sated

Your hunger is quiet, and you command your vampiric instincts with complete control.

- As an **action**, you activate Blood Sense for **10 minutes**.
- **Predatory Focus** does not apply.
- **Blood Frenzy** does not apply.
- You have **advantage on Dexterity and Charisma ability checks.**

## Vampiric Vitality

Your stolen lifeforce sustains your body beyond mortal limits.

As a bonus action, you can expend up to a number of Thirst Points equal to your Blood Hunter level. You regain 1 hit point for each Thirst Point expended in this way.

## Feeding

To regain Thirst Points, you must feed on a creature. To feed on a creature they must be at 0 hit points and within 1 minute of dying. The feeding process takes one uninterrupted minute. A creature that has been fed on this way decays quickly as it loses its blood and becomes a dry husk.

<aside>
💡

**Optional Rule:**

You may also feed on a willing creature. A willing creature being charmed does not interfere for the purposes of this ability. Whenever you feed this way, you gain half of the regular TP you gain from feeding (rounded up) and reduce the creature’s max hit points by an amount equal to the Thirst Points you gain until they finish a Long Rest.

</aside>

A creature can only be fed upon if it is **no more than one size category smaller** than you. Smaller creatures simply do not contain enough blood or vitality to meaningfully satisfy your hunger.

The value you get from each creature you drain depends on the creature type:

| Creature Type | TP Gained |
| --- | --- |
| Construct | 0 |
| Undead | 0 |
| Beast | 3 |
| Plant | 3 |
| Ooze | 4 |
| Monstrosity | 4 |
| Humanoid | 12 |
| Giant | 16 |
| Elemental | 16 |
| Aberration | 20 |
| Fey | 20 |
| Fiend | 20 |
| Dragon | 24 |
| Celestial | 30 |

Furthermore, the following creature types can’t sate you past **Composed** stage: Beast, Plant, Ooze, Monstrosity.
