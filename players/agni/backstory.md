# Agni Vidar

Widow’s Peak had always been a small little village, at least ever since I got here some 20 years ago. The actual inhabitants of the village were few, around 22 if I remember correctly, counting myself. Of course we had some travelers pass by from time to time, and some of them stayed for longer periods of time, with a very rare few actually staying here to live, while some of our older neighbors passed on to greener pastures or younger folks went out looking for new adventures or simply to find a more lively place to build their future. As for me, as soon as I found this place, I knew it was home.
Here I met the love of my life, Mavis, with whom I built a wonderful life. She was born here, like our son Milosh. I quickly found a purpose here. I have always been a decent swordsman, used to play around a lot when I was a kid and just had some natural talent for it, never really did any training. But now, I found something worth protecting, and that’s what I did every single day for the past 20 years. Until yesterday.

A couple months ago, we had someone new arrive at Widow’s Peak, Vasili, a very old man claiming to have been born here a long time ago, and that was now back to finish his life’s journey back where it started. He was quite jovial for his age and to the surprise of a lot of us, he opened up a small tavern out of a shack next to his house. It was a nice change of pace, having a place where the people could gather, relax and socialize at the end of the day. I frequented it almost every day, even if not just to say hi and have a quick chat, but most of the times to drink some of the wine Vasili brought with him. Even young Milosh, who came with me from time to time, was able to drink a special, only very slightly alcoholic drink Vasili made. Mavis was never much for drinking and was usually tired as she helped tend our farm animals, so she never drank anything, would only occasionally show up to chat with the other villagers.

And then came yesterday. I woke up in the middle of the night, somehow feeling hungry. To my surprise, Milosh was also up and also feeling hungry. I went to fetch some dry meat to eat from the tavern and told him to wait a bit for me to get back with the food. As I stepped outside, I noticed a few more lights than usual were lit inside peoples home and Vasili was outside the tavern, almost like he was waiting. I approached, asking if everything was alright and that’s when I saw it. Red glowing eyes. Sharp pointy fangs. He was no longer an old man. He was young, and he was strong and tall and he just grinned at me. I ran back home. I ran as fast as I could, knowing it was too late for me, hoping it wasn’t too late for Milosh. I’d heard the stories about vampires, but who would ever think they would come for you. As I got home, I dropped to my knees. Mavis was on our living room floor, with a pool of blood around her neck. Milosh was asleep, cuddled in her arms, with blood staining his entire face. And the only thing I could fucking think about was how tasty her blood looked. And so I drank it, until I was satisfied, and then I cried myself to sleep.

And now here I am. Its day now, and it seems like we aren’t the only ones to have gone through this. I’m leaving Milosh with our neighbors. I’m going after Vasili, and I’m going to find a way to cure my son. 

## Background

This section covers your character's life before arriving in Barovia.

- What was your character doing before arriving in Barovia?

Agni was living a peaceful life in Widow’s Peak. He was it’s protector, keeping dangerous wildlife at bay and dealing with any petty thieves or bandits that might stroll into the village.

- What event had the greatest impact on your character's life?

His arrival at Widow’s Peak and the day before he left, the two turning points of his life

- What is your character most proud of?

His son Milosh and the life he lived with him and Mavis

- What is your character's greatest regret?

Becoming weak, blind and dulled with so many years of peaceful life

## Personality

This section covers who your character is and how they see the world.

- What is your character's greatest strength?

His drive. He does not stop at anything to achieve his goals. He is beyond redemption

- What is your character's greatest flaw?

He lacks self-worth. He only lives to save his son from this curse. That’s his only purpose and he has become a shell of his former self because of it

- What does your character fear the most?

He won’t ever forget Vasili’s eyes on that fateful night. He is scared of him, of his unreachable power, but that doesn’t mean he won’t try. Not trying means his son will always have to live like this, and that can’t happen

- What is one line your character refuses to cross?

He has his own stash of blood that he kept from Mavis’ body, that he has been using to keep his hunger at bay. He won’t drink anyone else’s blood, at least never without their permission.

## Motivation

This section covers what drives your character.

- What does your character want more than anything else?

To save his son

- Why does your character keep going when things seem hopeless?

Because the future is never hopeless, if not me, then no one else will give that to Milosh

## Physical Description

Agni is a swordsman at heart, wielding his longsword and nothing else into battle. The sword is worn from use over the years. He wears simple clothes, a beige shirt, a dark leather coat with some steel reinforcements in one of the shoulders and one of the bracers (opposite side).

He is 35-40 years old. His brown hair is starting to grey a bit and he wears a messy looking mullet that continues into a short but also messy looking beard. His brown eyes have started to turn into this deep burgundy color. His face is worn and tired, but has a fierceness to it, a glimmer of hope always present. His left hand is gloved, but his right hand isn’t, and you can see some scars on it, some of them from cuts, others from bites.
