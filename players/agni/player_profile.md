## Working Hypotheses

- **Hypothesis:** Agni's player may appreciate dilemmas that let the party respond compassionately to people changed or endangered against their will.
  - **Confidence:** Low; unconfirmed.
  - **Evidence:** In Session 3, Agni opposed the ritual partly because he saw himself in the creature; in Session 5, he spent a healing potion to save Bildrath ([Session 3](../../sessions/Session%203/log.md); [Session 5](../../sessions/Session%205/log.md)).
  - **Alternatives / counterevidence:** These choices fit Agni's character and immediate circumstances; the logs do not record whether the player enjoyed either scene.
  - **Preparation implication / check:** Offer an optional compassionate alternative when it fits the scene, and ask whether this kind of dilemma is enjoyable beyond Agni's personal story.
