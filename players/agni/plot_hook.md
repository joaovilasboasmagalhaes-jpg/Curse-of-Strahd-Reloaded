You complete the blood ritual. The result is immediate and unsettling. The connection to Vasili doesn’t behave like distance or direction anymore—it behaves like presence. Like something being pulled just beyond sight. You act quickly and get on the move.

The trail leads you to the edge of a forest outside a neighboring settlement. The air grows dense as you approach, heavy with a mist that doesn’t belong to any natural weather. It gathers between the trees in slow, deliberate motion, swallowing depth and sound until even the forest edge feels distant.

The connection stops at the treeline but you don’t see him. It’s as if something inside is cutting it off or absorbing it. It doesn’t matter, Vasili is inside.

You stand there, focused, almost locked in place by the lingering pull of the magic. You never saw it coming. A caravan appears next to you, with colorful figures riding it. They turn to you.
