# Agni Vidar: Campaign Integration

> [!warning] Mutable DM document
> This document contains campaign-facing consequences, act-by-act DM hooks, scene adjustments, and implementation notes for integrating Agni Vidar into *Curse of Strahd: Reloaded*. For mutable DM lore on Lord Cassian von Holtz and the 3-tiered cure, see [DM Plans](dm_plans.md).

---

## Campaign Throughline

Agni's personal story progresses across the campaign in five distinct stages:

1. **Act I: The Scent of the Bloodline & The Father's Mirror.** Agni arrives in Barovia following the soft blood connection to Cassian. In the Village of Barovia, encountering Donavich and Doru confronts Agni with his deepest fear regarding his son, while establishing the tragedy of vampiric hunger.
2. **Act II: Tracing the Alchemist in Vallaki.** Whispers of the name "Vasili von Holtz" lead Agni and Sister Elga to discover Cassian's auxiliary laboratory in Vallaki, uncovering the first research notes needed to brew the *Blood-Stasis Philter*.
3. **Act III: Confronting the Maker in Ravenloft.** During the Dinner with Strahd and the Ravenloft Heist, Agni confronts Lord Cassian von Holtz directly, overcoming his maker, claiming his journals, and securing his heart-blood for the cure.
4. **Act IV: The Primordial Catalyst.** In the Amber Temple, Agni uncovers the ancient metaphysical origin of vampirism at the sarcophagus of the Vampyr, completing the formula for the *Panacea of the Sunlit Vein*.
5. **The Climax & Epilogue:** Slaying Strahd severs the root of the vampiric curse, allowing the panacea to permanently cure Milosh and freeing Agni to choose his own legacy.

## Act I: Into the Mists

### Arc B: Welcome to Barovia

#### 1. The Undercroft of the Church (Doru's Mirror)
* **Context:** Father Donavich keeps his starving, vampire-spawn son Doru locked in the church undercroft ([Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia.md](Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia.md)).
* **Agni's Experience:**
  * When approaching the church, Agni's `Blood Sense` detects Doru’s presence beneath the floorboards before anyone else notices.
  * The sound of Doru screaming in agony (*"Father, please! The hunger burns! Just a drop!"*) strikes directly at Agni's emotional wound regarding young Milosh back home.
  * When speaking with Donavich, the old priest's desperate rationalizations (*"He is still my boy... I cannot let him die, but I cannot let him kill"*) hold up a horrifying mirror to Agni's own dilemma.
* **Roleplay Opportunity:** Agni can use his unique understanding of the blood hunger to communicate with Doru. If Agni offers alchemical advice or helps calm Doru without bloodshed, it establishes that vampiric thirst can be managed with discipline rather than mindless slaughter.

#### Using Cassian's Pull in Act I
* The soft connection should first appear as a brief sensory disturbance rather than a command: the smell of Vasili's wine in an empty room, a memory of Mavis's blood, or the sensation that someone is listening through Agni's hunger.
* If the party later encounters a trace of Cassian, use **Cassian's Pull** from [DM Plans](dm_plans.md). Cassian can use it once per long rest. Agni can resist and lose Thirst Points, or lean into the connection for a powerful but dangerous ten-minute advantage while allowing Cassian to sense him in return.
* Do not use the mechanic to force Agni to attack Doru, the party, or an innocent person. Its first purpose is to establish the rivalry and show that Agni can turn the connection into a weapon.

#### 2. The Bloodline Signature
* When Rahadin arrives for his proclamation in the village square, Agni detects a subtle undercurrent of distilled vampire ichor and grave-magic clinging to the castle’s retinue. The subtle magical signature matches the tainted vintage from Widow's Peak.

#### Madam Eva's Reading
* When Madam Eva reads the Tarokka cards for the party at the Tser Pool Encampment:
  * She looks into Agni's burgundy eyes and murmurs: *"The father who drank the sorrow of his own house. You chase a shadow who wore an old man's face, but you walk in the footsteps of an ancient plague. The blood you carry is both your shackle and your weapon. To save the child who sleeps beyond the fog, you must tear the heart from the alchemist and pierce the root of the mountain."*
  * She confirms that the man named "Vasili" who visited Widow's Peak returned to Castle Ravenloft when the master of the castle awakened.

## Act II: The Shadowed Town

### Arc D & Arc F: Vallaki Intrigue

#### 1. The Name "Vasili von Holtz"
* As the party navigates Vallaki, they hear the name "Vasili von Holtz" mentioned with respect by townsfolk, merchants, and Lady Fiona Wachter.
* **The Investigation:** Agni initially suspects "Vasili" is walking the streets of Vallaki. However, careful questioning or investigating old trade manifests reveals that "Vasili von Holtz" is a historic family name that noble agents of Castle Ravenloft use when conducting business with mortals.
* A ledger in Lady Wachter's library or Henrik van der Voort's shop notes shipments of special herbal preservatives, alchemical glassware, and fortified wine consigned to a "Lord Cassian von Holtz" of Ravenloft.
* **The Timing Clue:** The ledgers and Cassian's notes predate Strahd's awakening by several weeks. They show that Widow's Peak was the last stage of a much older experiment, with a planned observation period and a final activation date later than the night of the massacre. The notes refer to the awakening as an interruption that triggered the formula early, not as the reason Cassian began the trial.
* **The Failed Cohorts:** Cassian's notes also refer to earlier villages where the same process failed. The full chronology belongs in the future research-notes document, but the party can learn the important conclusion here: Agni's village was the latest attempt, and Cassian believed he had finally found the correct subjects and dosage.
* **The Replaced Plan:** Cassian had prepared a smaller activation ritual for Widow's Peak. Strahd's awakening replaced that ritual with a much larger blood-pulse, producing the village-wide catastrophe before Cassian was ready. This explains why Cassian treats Agni and Milosh as valuable evidence while also regarding the massacre as a ruined experiment.

#### 2. Crafting the Blood-Stasis Philter (Inter-Party Collaboration with Sister Elga)
* **Discovering the Notes:** In Vallaki, the party discovers a hidden strongbox belonging to Cassian (e.g., hidden in the attic of Wachterhaus or recovered from a vampire safehouse). It contains partial research notes on blood stabilization.
* **The Brewing Scene:**
  * Sister Elga and Agni spend downtime collaborating. Agni provides insights into the exact physical symptoms of the Dhampir thirst, while Sister Elga uses her Alchemist’s Supplies and knowledge of medicinal herbs to refine the formula.
  * The party gathers **Morning Dew** at dawn in the Svalich Woods, **Cat's Tongue** from the Wachterhaus garden or the fields outside Vallaki, and **Olisuba Leaf** from the medicinal grasses Van Richten searches in Arc E. The party can earn Van Richten's cooperation by helping him in that scene instead of simply taking the ingredient.
  * Cassian's notes identify the recipe as a simplified adaptation of the Potion of Vitality from [Potion Brewing and Ingredient Gathering](../sister_elga/potion_brewing.md). The recipe also requires one vial of Agni's blood and one stabilized vampire-ichor ampoule recovered from Cassian's laboratory.
  * Elga spends 8 hours brewing the philter. She makes a common-recipe check against DC 8 using her proficiency bonus and Potion Making Level modifier; until she earns Potion Making experience, that modifier is +0. Agni can Help with the check by describing his symptoms and recognizing when the mixture is affecting the blood correctly.
  * On a success, the party gets three doses. On a failure, the herb ingredients can be recovered under the guide's partial-loss rules, but the gold and stabilized ichor are spent. This creates a delay and a reason to protect the laboratory catalyst without making Elga an expert.
* **What the Philter Does:** Each dose stabilizes Milosh for seven days, prevents further deterioration, suppresses his immediate urge for humanoid blood, and lets animal blood sustain him temporarily. It does not cure him. If Agni drinks a dose, it instead gives him advantage on Blood Frenzy saves and prevents involuntary Blood Sense activation for 24 hours.
  * **Sending it to Milosh:** The party cannot send the philter by ordinary magic or escort it out themselves. After earning the trust of Stanimir's neutral caravan at Tser Pool, they can ask Stanimir, Radu, or Elisabeta to carry a sealed batch through the Mists to Widow's Peak. The Vistani may carry the medicine and letters, but Agni and the other non-Vistani characters cannot travel with them through the boundary. The courier returns with the caretakers' report and a message from Milosh. Arrigal and Luvash's camp may offer the same service, but they serve Strahd and may betray the delivery.
  * **If the party asks to leave:** Stanimir explains that his freedom is not theirs. He can cross the Mists because Strahd's ancient debt protects Vistani travelers; the protection does not extend to outsiders in his wagon. He can demonstrate this by asking a non-Vistani volunteer to step into the fog while he remains beside them. The fog turns the volunteer around before they can cross, while Stanimir can pass through and return. The scene should make the rule clear without punishing the players for asking.
  * **The First Message:** Agni should write Milosh a short letter before the courier leaves. The courier reads it aloud at Widow's Peak, and Milosh's reply should include one personal detail that proves he remembers Agni, such as asking about Agni's sword, Mavis's favorite song, or whether the party has found Vasili.
  * **Ongoing Contact:** Each successful delivery can carry another philter dose and one letter in each direction. A report should answer three questions: whether Milosh is stable, what he has eaten or tried to eat, and what he remembers about Cassian's experiment. This gives Agni meaningful contact without weakening the Mists.
  * **Emergency Contact:** If the party obtains a casting of *sending*, Agni can attempt a brief 25-word message to Milosh. Apply the normal 5 percent chance of failure for communication across the planar boundary. A successful reply is immediate but short; it cannot deliver medicine, inspect Milosh, or replace the courier route.
  * **Delivery Risk:** If the party has angered Strahd or Cassian, the courier may report that a bat, wolf, or Vistani agent followed the route. The philter can arrive late, damaged, or with a warning that Cassian now knows Milosh's location. Do not remove the delivery automatically; make the danger a consequence the party can respond to.

## Act III: The Broken Land

### Arc O: Dinner with the Devil

#### Meeting the Court
* During the dinner at Castle Ravenloft ([Act III - The Broken Land/Arc O - Dinner with the Devil.md](Act III - The Broken Land/Arc O - Dinner with the Devil.md)), Strahd introduces his court. Among the consorts and advisors stands **Lord Cassian von Holtz**.
* **Cassian's Reaction to Agni:**
  * Cassian is neither frightened nor remorseful. He looks at Agni with the cold pride of a scientist admiring a masterwork.
  * *"Remarkable,"* Cassian observes calmly over wine. *"Most common minds would have rotted into mindless ghouls when the contagion matured that night. But your will, Agni... and the exquisite tragedy of your boy... the way your bloodline held the threshold without dying. You were the crowning proof of my work."*
  * Cassian attempts to pull on the soft blood connection during the conversation. Agni feels his hunger sharpen and memories of Widow's Peak intrude, but the pull cannot compel him to kneel, attack, or obey. Cassian is visibly unsettled if Agni resists or turns the sensation back into a means of locating him.
  * If Agni resists successfully, record a **Defiance** mark. Cassian recognizes the resistance as a personal insult, not merely a failed experiment, and becomes more likely to make a mistake later in the castle.
  * If Agni asks why the awakening happened when it did, Cassian admits that he expected several more weeks of observation. Strahd's return sent the blood-pulse that activated the formula prematurely, destroying the clean trial and forcing Cassian to improvise. Cassian considers the resulting deaths unfortunate waste, while he considers Milosh and Agni proof that the unfinished process still worked.
* **Strahd's Amusement:** Strahd finds the exchange entertaining, noting that survival of the fittest is the law of Barovia: *"If you wish to settle a debt with my physician, master Vidar, prove your worth in my valley first. In Castle Ravenloft, we do not duel over appetizers."*

### Arc P: Ravenloft Heist & The Apothecary of the Crypts

#### The Personal Boss Fight: Lord Cassian von Holtz
* **Location:** The Alchemical Laboratory / Apothecary in the dungeons of Castle Ravenloft (near area K76 / K78).
* **The Encounter:** While exploring or infiltrating the castle, Agni leads the party into Cassian's private workshop.
* **Cassian's Tactics:** Cassian fights as an enhanced **Elder Vampire Spawn / Alchemical Savant**, throwing vials of caustic vampire blood, utilizing spider climb, and attempting to harvest fresh samples from Agni during combat. At an important moment, he uses **Cassian's Pull** to provoke a hunger spike or intrusive memory. Agni chooses how to respond: resisting costs Thirst Points, while leaning into the connection makes Cassian impossible to hide from but gives Agni a serious temporary advantage against him.
* **The Spoils of Victory:**
  1. **Cassian's Complete Alchemical Grimoire:** Details the full history of the Widow's Peak experiment and confirms that the final missing ingredient for a complete cure requires ancient planar alchemy from the Amber Temple.
  2. **The Heart-Blood Catalyst:** A vial of Cassian's crystallized heart-blood, preserved to serve as the physical base for the ultimate panacea.

## Act IV: Secrets of the Ancient

### Arc S: A Sword of Sunlight (The Amber Temple)

#### The Sarcophagus of the Vampyr
* In the lower vaults of the Amber Temple ([Act IV - Secrets of the Ancient/Arc S - A Sword of Sunlight.md](Act IV - Secrets of the Ancient/Arc S - A Sword of Sunlight.md)), the party reaches the shattered amber sarcophagus of the **Vampyr**.
* **The Ancient Inscription:**
  * Ancient runes carved into the amber base describe the cosmic nature of the first blood pact.
  * Deciphering the runes reveals the missing spiritual formula: how to transmute corrupted vampiric blood back into mortal vitality using holy water, amber dust, and the maker's heart-blood.
* **Completing the Panacea:** With Sister Elga's aid and the power of the Amber Temple, Agni brews the completed **Panacea of the Sunlit Vein**.

## Climax & Epilogue

### Severing the Root
* In the final battle against Strahd von Zarovich, Agni fights knowing that Strahd is the root of the entire bloodline. Every blow struck against the Darklord weakens the metaphysical grip of the curse on all who bear it.
* When Strahd falls and the Mists part:
  1. The cosmic link tethering Milosh and Agni to Ravenloft dissolves.
  2. Because Agni successfully maintained his line and never fed on living humanoid blood, the curse naturally purges from his system once Strahd is defeated and the *Blood-Stasis Philter* stabilizes him. (He may also choose to retain his disciplined Dhampir abilities under full self-control as an eternal guardian).
 3. Agni returns to Widow's Peak with the party, now able to cross the former boundary freely. He sees Milosh in person for the first time since the night of the awakening and explains the cure himself.
 4. The *Panacea of the Sunlit Vein* (incorporating the Amber Temple catalyst) is administered to Milosh, successfully breaking his permanent sealed curse, cleansing his body, and restoring him to full, normal mortal life. Milosh's recovery is a scene of trust and choice, not an automatic effect: he must accept the medicine from Agni after hearing what happened in Barovia.
