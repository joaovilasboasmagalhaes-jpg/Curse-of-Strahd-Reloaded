# Agni Vidar: Campaign Integration

> [!warning] Mutable DM document
> This document contains campaign-facing consequences, act-by-act DM hooks, scene adjustments, and implementation notes for integrating Agni Vidar into *Curse of Strahd: Reloaded*. For mutable DM lore on Doctor Osric Valemont and the 3-tiered cure, see [DM Plans](dm_plans.md).

---

## Campaign Throughline

Agni's personal story progresses across the campaign in five distinct stages:

1. **Act I: The Scent of the Bloodline & The Father's Mirror.** Agni arrives in Barovia following the soft blood connection to Osric. In the Village of Barovia (played in Sessions 4-7), Doru and Donavich confront Agni with his deepest fear regarding his son, while establishing the tragedy of vampiric hunger. This stage is complete; later scenes can call back to it.
2. **Act II: Tracing the Alchemist in Vallaki.** Whispers of the Count's foreign physician lead Agni and Sister Elga to discover Osric's auxiliary laboratory in Vallaki, uncovering the first research notes needed to brew the *Blood-Stasis Philter*.
3. **Act III: Confronting the Maker in Ravenloft.** During the Dinner with Strahd and the Ravenloft Heist, Agni confronts Doctor Osric Valemont directly, overcoming his maker, claiming his journals, and securing his heart-blood for the cure.
4. **Act IV: The Primordial Catalyst.** In the Amber Temple, Agni uncovers the ancient metaphysical origin of vampirism at the sarcophagus of the Vampyr, completing the formula for the *Panacea of the Sunlit Vein*.
5. **The Climax & Epilogue:** Slaying Strahd severs the root of the vampiric curse, allowing the panacea to permanently cure Milosh and freeing Agni to choose his own legacy.

## Act I: Into the Mists

### Arc B: Welcome to Barovia (played)

#### 1. The Undercroft of the Church (Doru's Mirror), Session 7
* **What happened ([Session 7](../../sessions/Session%207/log.md)):** Donavich told the party about Doru, a vampire spawn locked in the Undercroft. The party talked with Doru and tested his hunger with blood. Doru found Agni's blood "different and less appealing"; Grim felt the touch of death when she bandaged Agni's hand. Doru passed the test with Grim's blood, shared a hug with Donavich in tears, and Donavich plans to try animal blood. Ivan threatened to kill Doru if anything ever happens in the town.
* **What it did for Agni:** It showed him his worst fear in a stranger's family: a father unable to stop his son from being a starving monster, and a son who held back with help. Do not retcon any extra senses, such as a Blood Sense detection; Blood Sense only senses living creatures, and Session 4 records it finding nothing new.
* **Callbacks:** Doru's "different and less appealing" verdict hints that Agni is not a full vampire. Grim noticed the death-touch, which is a loose thread for a later conversation. In Session 7 Doru also told Grim only vaguely of Strahd's heart; that is her thread, not Agni's.

#### Using Osric's Pull in Act I
* The soft connection should first appear as a brief sensory disturbance rather than a command: the smell of Vasili's wine in an empty room, a memory of Mavis's blood, or the sensation that someone is listening through Agni's hunger.
* If the party later encounters a trace of Osric, use **Osric's Pull** from [DM Plans](dm_plans.md). Osric can use it once per long rest. Agni can resist and lose Thirst Points, or lean into the connection for a powerful but dangerous ten-minute advantage while allowing Osric to sense him in return.
* Do not use the mechanic to force Agni to attack the party or an innocent person. Its first purpose is to establish the rivalry and show that Agni can turn the connection into a weapon.

#### 2. Rahadin's Sniff (played, Session 6)
* **What happened ([Session 6](../../sessions/Session%206/log.md)):** Rahadin gave his proclamation, then turned to the party and sniffed the air when looking at Ivan and Agni. Nothing in the log says Agni sensed anything himself.
* **Optional later use:** Once Agni meets Osric's notes or Osric himself, he can realize that what Rahadin sniffed was the same tainted vintage from Widow's Peak. Treat this as a later realization, not as a detection that happened at the time.

## Act II: The Shadowed Town

### Arc D & Arc F: Vallaki Intrigue

#### 1. The Count's Physician
* As the party navigates Vallaki, merchants and tradespeople who deal with the castle mention that the Count keeps a foreign physician, a Doctor Valemont, who orders glassware, preservatives, and fortified wine. They do not know his cover name "Vasili", which was used only at Widow's Peak. Strahd's own alias "Aurel Veshtor" appears only in the Lovina and Abbey history (the History and Arc K) and has no link to Osric.
* **The Investigation:** Agni initially suspects "Vasili" is walking the streets of Vallaki. Careful questioning or old trade manifests lead him to the physician instead.
* A ledger in Lady Wachter's library or a merchant's books notes shipments of special herbal preservatives, alchemical glassware, and fortified wine consigned to "Doctor Osric Valemont" of Ravenloft. (Henrik van der Voort's shop is Volenta's base, so use it only if the party has reason to search it.)
* **The Timing Clue:** Osric's notes show that Widow's Peak was the last stage of a much older experiment, with a planned observation period and a final activation date later than the night of the massacre. The notes refer to the awakening as an interruption that triggered the formula early, not as the reason Osric began the trial. Dates in the notes are on Osric's own clock; do not compare them with Strahd's waking on the Barovian clock.
* **The Failed Cohorts:** Osric's notes also refer to earlier villages where the same process failed. The full chronology belongs in the future research-notes document, but the party can learn the important conclusion here: Agni's village was the latest attempt, and Osric believed he had finally found the correct subjects and dosage.
* **The Replaced Plan:** Osric had prepared a smaller activation ritual for Widow's Peak. Strahd's awakening replaced that ritual with a much larger blood-pulse, producing the village-wide catastrophe before Osric was ready. This explains why Osric treats Agni and Milosh as valuable evidence while also regarding the massacre as a ruined experiment.
* **The Heirloom:** In the same strongbox lies an inert keepsake (a family heirloom or piece of his physician's kit) that does nothing in Barovia. Osric's notes, and later Osric himself, reveal that it is a mist talisman for the world he came from, which is the same world as Widow's Peak. Mist Wanderer characters would recognize it on touch. It does not work while Strahd is awake and gives the party no way out. Strahd's borders are closed.
* **Origin Clue:** The notes also show that Osric was born in Widow's Peak and that the house beside the tavern was his family's, so Agni may recall a half-remembered local story about the family that left.

#### 2. Crafting the Blood-Stasis Philter (Inter-Party Collaboration with Sister Elga)
* **Discovering the Notes:** In Vallaki, the party discovers a hidden strongbox belonging to Osric (e.g., hidden in the attic of Wachterhaus or recovered from a vampire safehouse). It contains partial research notes on blood stabilization. **DM-only warning: Elga's Urn glows (Leo Dilisnya entombed beneath Wachterhaus, plus any spawn at a safehouse); tell the player.**
* **The Brewing Scene:**
  * Sister Elga and Agni spend downtime collaborating. Agni provides insights into the exact physical symptoms of the Dhampir thirst, while Sister Elga uses her Alchemist’s Supplies and knowledge of medicinal herbs to refine the formula.
  * The party gathers **Morning Dew** at dawn in the Svalich Woods, **Cat's Tongue** from the Wachterhaus garden or the fields outside Vallaki, and **Olisuba Leaf** from the medicinal grasses Van Richten searches in Arc E. The party can earn Van Richten's cooperation by helping him in that scene instead of simply taking the ingredient.
  * Osric's notes identify the recipe as a simplified adaptation of the Potion of Vitality from [Potion Brewing and Ingredient Gathering](../sister_elga/potion_brewing.md). The recipe also requires one vial of Agni's blood and one stabilized vampire-ichor ampoule recovered from Osric's laboratory.
  * Elga spends 8 hours brewing the philter. She makes a common-recipe check against DC 8 using her proficiency bonus and Potion Making Level modifier; until she earns Potion Making experience, that modifier is +0. Agni can Help with the check by describing his symptoms and recognizing when the mixture is affecting the blood correctly.
  * On a success, the party gets three doses. On a failure, the herb ingredients can be recovered under the guide's partial-loss rules, but the gold and stabilized ichor are spent. This creates a delay and a reason to protect the laboratory catalyst without making Elga an expert.
* **What the Philter Does:** Each dose suppresses Agni's immediate urge for humanoid blood: advantage on Blood Frenzy saves and no involuntary Blood Sense activation for 24 hours. For Milosh, one dose would stabilize him for seven days, prevent deterioration, and let animal blood sustain him temporarily. It does not cure him.
  * **No delivery:** Nothing crosses the Mists. Milosh cannot be reached by courier, *sending* or any other route, and no doses leave Barovia. The batch stays with Agni until the Mists part, when he can give Milosh a dose in person. Do not use the unseen outside to pressure Agni with a ticking clock.
  * **If the party asks to leave:** They cannot. The Mists turn non-Vistani creatures around. Vistani cross freely, but this story does not use them as a courier. Stanimir can explain, and demonstrate, why his freedom is not theirs: the fog turns a non-Vistani volunteer around while he walks through and back.

## Act III: The Broken Land

### Arc O: Dinner with the Devil

#### Meeting the Court
* During the dinner at Castle Ravenloft ([Act III - The Broken Land/Arc O - Dinner with the Devil.md](Act III - The Broken Land/Arc O - Dinner with the Devil.md)), Strahd introduces his court. Among the consorts and advisors stands **Doctor Osric Valemont**, the Count's physician. Osric hid his experiments from Strahd (see [DM Plans](dm_plans.md)). He has chosen this dinner to present the Widow's Peak result to Strahd, and that is how Strahd learns of it. **DM-only warning: Elga's Urn glows (Strahd's court is undead); tell the player.**
* **Osric's Reaction to Agni:**
  * Osric is neither frightened nor remorseful. He looks at Agni with the cold pride of a scientist admiring a masterwork.
  * *"Remarkable,"* Osric observes calmly over wine. *"Most common minds would have rotted into mindless ghouls when the contagion matured that night. But your will, Agni... and the exquisite tragedy of your boy... the way your bloodline held the threshold without dying. You were the crowning proof of my work."*
  * Osric attempts to pull on the soft blood connection during the conversation. Agni feels his hunger sharpen and memories of Widow's Peak intrude, but the pull cannot compel him to kneel, attack, or obey. Osric is visibly unsettled if Agni resists or turns the sensation back into a means of locating him.
  * If Agni resists successfully, record a **Defiance** mark. Osric recognizes the resistance as a personal insult, not merely a failed experiment, and becomes more likely to make a mistake later in the castle.
  * If Agni asks why the awakening happened when it did, Osric admits that he expected several more weeks of observation. Strahd's return sent the blood-pulse that activated the formula prematurely, destroying the clean trial and forcing Osric to improvise. Osric considers the resulting deaths unfortunate waste, while he considers Milosh and Agni proof that the unfinished process still worked.
  * If Agni asks how he reached Widow's Peak, Osric answers with a half-truth: he was born there, and the Mists once let him go home. He does not mention the heirloom unless pushed.
* **Strahd's Amusement:** Strahd, who only now learns of the experiment, finds the exchange entertaining, noting that survival of the fittest is the law of Barovia: *"If you wish to settle a debt with my physician, master Vidar, prove your worth in my valley first. In Castle Ravenloft, we do not duel over appetizers."*

### Arc P: Ravenloft Heist & The Apothecary of the Crypts

#### The Personal Boss Fight: Doctor Osric Valemont
* **Location:** A new laboratory and apothecary chamber added off the dungeon hall (P11a, K73) in Castle Ravenloft. Rooms K75e (Henrik's cell), K76/K77 (the torture chamber with zombies and Anton) and K78 (the brazier room) are already used in [Arc P](Act III - The Broken Land/Arc P - Ravenloft Heist.md), so do not place it there.
* **The Encounter:** While exploring or infiltrating the castle, Agni leads the party into Osric's private workshop. **DM-only warning: Elga's Urn glows throughout the castle; tell the player.**
* **Osric's Tactics:** Osric fights as an enhanced vampire spawn alchemist. Build his stat block with the combat skill before use; no numbers are fixed yet. He throws vials of caustic vampire blood, uses spider climb, and attempts to harvest fresh samples from Agni during combat. At an important moment, he uses **Osric's Pull** to provoke a hunger spike or intrusive memory. Agni chooses how to respond: resisting costs Thirst Points, while leaning into the connection makes Osric impossible to hide from but gives Agni a serious temporary advantage against him.
* **Encounter design (hard, not a static HP race):** The player likes difficult battles and is stubborn, so the fight must be tough and give Agni choices. Give it an objective besides killing Osric: his grimoire and heart-blood are being destroyed or carried off (visible progress, for example a burning stack of notes or a retreat route that closes). Give it something to react to: reagent vials and a collapsing laboratory, mid-fight phase changes, and retreat or reinforcements from the castle. State the expected length in rounds when building it, per the combat skill.
* **Stubbornness:** If Agni pushes the fight past a retreat point, let the cost fall on the party, not as a railroad. The grimoire burns, or Osric escapes with the heart-blood.
* **The Spoils of Victory:**
  1. **Osric's Complete Alchemical Grimoire:** Details the full history of the Widow's Peak experiment and confirms that the final missing ingredient for a complete cure requires ancient planar alchemy from the Amber Temple.
  2. **The Heart-Blood Catalyst:** A vial of Osric's crystallized heart-blood, preserved to serve as the physical base for the ultimate panacea.

## Act IV: Secrets of the Ancient

### Arc S: A Sword of Sunlight (The Amber Temple)

#### The Sarcophagus of the Vampyr
* In the lower vaults of the Amber Temple ([Act IV - Secrets of the Ancient/Arc S - A Sword of Sunlight.md](Act IV - Secrets of the Ancient/Arc S - A Sword of Sunlight.md)), the party reaches the shattered amber sarcophagus of the **Vampyr**.
* **The Ancient Inscription:**
  * Ancient runes carved into the amber base describe the cosmic nature of the first blood pact.
  * Deciphering the runes reveals the missing spiritual formula: how to transmute corrupted vampiric blood back into mortal vitality using holy water, amber dust, and the maker's heart-blood.
* **Completing the Panacea:** With Sister Elga's aid and the power of the Amber Temple, Agni brews the completed **Panacea of the Sunlit Vein**.

## Climax & Epilogue

### Severing the Root
* In the final battle against Strahd von Zarovich, Agni fights knowing that Strahd is the root of the entire bloodline. Every blow struck against the Darklord weakens the metaphysical grip of the curse on all who bear it.
* When Strahd falls and the Mists part:
  1. The cosmic link tethering Milosh and Agni to Ravenloft dissolves.
  2. Because Agni successfully maintained his line and never fed on living humanoid blood, the curse naturally purges from his system once Strahd is defeated and the *Blood-Stasis Philter* stabilizes him. (He may also choose to retain his disciplined Dhampir abilities under full self-control as an eternal guardian).
 3. Agni returns to Widow's Peak with the party, now able to cross the former boundary freely. He sees Milosh in person for the first time since the night of the awakening and explains the cure himself.
 4. The *Panacea of the Sunlit Vein* (incorporating the Amber Temple catalyst) is administered to Milosh, successfully breaking his permanent sealed curse, cleansing his body, and restoring him to full, normal mortal life. Milosh's recovery is a scene of trust and choice, not an automatic effect: he must accept the medicine from Agni after hearing what happened in Barovia.
