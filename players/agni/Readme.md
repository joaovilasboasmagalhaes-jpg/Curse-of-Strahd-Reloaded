# Agni: Document Guide

This directory contains reference material and campaign-planning notes related to Agni Vidar.

## Story Snapshot (DM-Facing)

> This snapshot combines established character facts with campaign hooks. Treat planned revelations as DM notes, not as information Agni or the other players already know.

- **Main goal:** Find Vasili and save his son Milosh from the vampiric curse that has taken hold of them both.
- **Most distinguishing feature:** Agni is a Dhampir swordsman struggling with blood hunger and a powerful Blood Sense. He is determined not to feed on anyone without consent.
- **Story and NPC hooks:** His burgundy eyes, scarred hand, and reactions to blood or undead can prompt questions. Doru and Father Donavich mirror Agni's fear for Milosh; Vasili's identity and the search for a cure are his central leads. See [Backstory](backstory.md), [Vampire Details](vampire_details.md), and [Campaign Integration](dm_campaign_integration.md).
