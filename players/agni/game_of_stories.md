# Game of Stories: Agni

> **Canon backstory.** This is the recorded version prepared for the Session 9 Game of Stories. The telling at the table may have differed.

Milosh has always been an adventurous kid, and he used to explore the forest outskirts a lot when he was younger. One day, however, he didn’t come back home after dark. This happened when he was just 9 years old, me and Mavis were worried sick so I immediately set out to search for him. I searched aimlessly for around 2 hours before I finally found a trace of him, one of his shoes, stuck under the root of a tree. It was a start, and from there I was able to track where he headed. Thankfully, there didn’t seem to be any blood or any signs that he was hurt around, but still, he was a kid alone at night, or worse, someone or something had him.

After following the trail for around an hour, I saw a light through the trees and people talking. As I approached, making sure to keep myself hidden, I saw what seemed to be three men, talking eating and drinking, and Milosh, seemingly unharmed , also eating and talking to them. Thinking back on this, I should’ve waited a bit and listened to what they were talking about, to understand the situation, but I had been searching for my son for hours, so upon seeing him, I just ran at him to hug him and make sure he was alright, which he seemed to be. The other men, upon seeing me run up to Milosh, drew their weapons, somewhat clumsily, has they seemed to be a bit drunk. They demanded to know who I was, which I told them, and they said my son told them about Widow’s Peak, and they were very excited to head there and see what they could find. Their intent was clear, they were going to use Milosh to try to attack our village and take whatever they could. As they finished saying this, I drew my sword, pushed Milosh behind me, and just rushed at the closest of them. He didn’t expect this sudden attack, and I was able to cut across his dominant arm, making him drop his mace. As I turned to my left, one of them was rushing at me, spear in hand. I was able to dodge a deadly blow, but I got stabbed in the shoulder, staggering me. I could hear the last of them rushing up behind me, so I grabbed the spear, still stuck to my shoulder, and spun around, making the two men crash into each other. I then broke the spear handle with my sword, took it out of my shoulder and slammed into the chest of the one that stabbed me with it. Out of nowhere, I was hit across the back with a mace. The man I initially disarmed was able to get back up and find enough strength in his other arm to hit me back. I was able to get back up fast enough to finish him off with a quick slash across the neck, but when I looked back, the last man had Milosh in his grasp, holding him with his harm across his neck, and pointing his sword at me. I started to go to put my sword down, but to my surprise, and to the bandit, Milosh hit him right in the balls with his elbow and I immediately took the opportunity to dash at him and pierce him with my sword right through the middle of the man’s chest.

Milosh was shook after having seen the carnage that unfolded, and I was starting to feel my wounds as the adrenaline wore off, but we were safe, and so was Widow’s Peak. After that, Milosh started training a bit, trying to learn how to defend himself better, but he never again wondered too far into the forest.
