# Plot Hook

You search through the ruined remains of the monastery for the Tome of Records. It is one of the few things that might have survived. If it is gone, then so is everything you were meant to preserve.

The place is barely recognizable. Hallways are broken and open into nothing. Some sections are gone entirely, as if they were never there. Others are collapsed and warped beyond recognition, half-buried in debris and ash.

Then you find it.

Not the Tome itself, but a carved rune set into the floor beneath the rubble. It should not be here. It is not part of any Ven doctrine you were taught. It feels wrong. Corrupted. Like something has been written over reality itself.

As you step closer to inspect it, your Urn of Soulcatching begins to vibrate.

It quickly becomes violent.

The glass and metal shake in your hands as if something inside is trying to break out. Sparks of pale light erupt from the rune beneath your feet. For a moment, you see it clearly—souls inside the Urn, pressing against the boundary. Not calm. Not contained. Desperate.

They are trying to leave.

You do not have time to react.

The rune activates.

Light erupts upward, swallowing the ruins, the ground, everything.

---

You blink.

The monastery is gone.

---

You are standing on a dirt road.

On both sides, dense forest rises like a wall, thick and unmoving. The air is wrong—still, heavy, almost watching. In front of you, two massive headless stone statues flank an enormous iron gate. Cold metal bars stretch upward into the fog.

The gate opens on its own.

At the same time, the souls inside your Urn violently release, streaming out in silent bursts, vanishing through the opening as if pulled toward something beyond.

Behind you, somewhere in the mist, you hear a carriage moving. Slow. Approaching.
