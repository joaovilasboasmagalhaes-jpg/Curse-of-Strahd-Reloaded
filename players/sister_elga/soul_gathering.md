# Soul Gathering

## Urn of Soulcatching

While the Urn is within 300 feet of the Undead type, its flame turns bright purple.

When a creature dies within 30 feet of you, you can use your **reaction** to attempt to capture its soul within the urn. The soul is drawn into the urn as part of a brief rite performed instinctively by the bearer.
After capturing a soul, make a **Religion (Wisdom or Intelligence) check**. If the creature’s **CR is higher than your character level**, you make this check with **disadvantage**.

---

## Soul Result (Religion Check)

| Roll | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Natural 1 | Roll on the Liturgy of Ruin |
| 1–8 | The ritual fails |
| 9–12 | You learn the soul’s alignment |
| 13–16 | You learn the soul’s name and alignment |
| 17–19 | You learn a relevant memory, name, and alignment |
| Natural 20 | Roll on the Liturgy of Grace and gain all information |

On a successful result, you gain all information up to and including the tier rolled.

The information reflects only what the soul knew in life.

---

## Liturgy of Ruin (d4)

| Roll | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The ritual leaves a lingering mark on you. You have disadvantage on your next Religion check involving the urn. |
| 2 | The connection with the dead lashes back. You take necrotic damage equal to the creature’s CR × 1d10. This damage cannot be reduced or prevented, and your maximum hit points are reduced by the same amount until you complete a long rest. |
| 3 | The strain overwhelms your body. You gain one level of exhaustion. |
| 4 | The disturbance echoes beyond the veil. Strahd becomes aware that the urn was used. |

---

## Liturgy of Grace (d4)

| Roll | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The ritual goes smoothly. You gain **Divine Favor**. If you already have Divine Favor, you may instead grant it to another creature of your choice within 30 feet. |
| 2 | The urn releases protective energy. You gain advantage on your next attack roll, ability check, or saving throw against undead. |
| 3 | The captured energy shields you. You and creatures of your choice within 30 feet gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus. |
| 4 | The boundary between life and death opens briefly. You and creatures of your choice within 30 feet gain the effects of @UUID[Spell.bless] for 24 hours. |

## Soul Affinity

Each soul within your **Urn of Soulcatching** has an **Affinity** with you, representing the strength of your connection to it.

A newly captured soul has **0 Affinity**.

### Increasing Affinity

At the end of each long rest, you can choose one soul in your urn. Its Affinity increases by 1, to a maximum of 3.

You can also increase a soul's Affinity when you meaningfully connect with it. This might involve fulfilling a request, interacting with something important to it, or otherwise doing something that strengthens your connection. The DM determines when an action qualifies.

Named or significant souls may have specific desires or circumstances that make increasing their Affinity more difficult.

### Using a Soul

You can expend a spell slot to commune with a captured soul. The effect depends on its Affinity.

| Affinity | Connection |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | You can sense only vague emotions, sensations, or impressions from the soul. |
| 1 | You can draw simple emotions, sensations, or impressions from the soul. |
| 2 | You can ask the soul questions and receive meaningful information. |
| 3 | You have a strong connection with the soul and can reliably draw upon its knowledge and experiences. |

The information or assistance a soul provides can only come from what it knew or experienced in life.
