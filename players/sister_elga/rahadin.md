# Rahadin

Here’s the summary of our plans for Rahadin:

1. **Origin of His Deathless State:**
   - Following the fall of the Priory of the Ven (~375 B.C.), Strahd used Abbot Ferd’s captured research on soul-anchoring to experiment on Rahadin.
   - The ritual was intended to grant Rahadin immortality/resilience by anchoring his vitality to captive souls.
   - **The Unplanned Side Effect:** The souls of the dusk elves Rahadin personally slaughtered became permanently chained to his spiritual aura. Instead of peacefully fueling his life force, they form a screaming, terrifying psychic chorus around him that drags him back to life out of raw terror and spite.

2. **True Death Condition:**
   - Rahadin cannot be permanently slain while his soul-chorus remains bound. If struck down, the tethered spirits wrench his body back into unlife.
   - Sister Elga’s *Urn of Soulcatching* is the exact divine tool required to peel away, collect, and purify these bound dusk elf souls. Once the chorus is unanchored, Rahadin becomes fully mortal and subject to true death.

3. **First Encounter (Arc B — The Proclamation in Barovia):**
   - When Rahadin arrives at the town square after the siege ([Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia.md](Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia.md#Rahadin’s%20Proclamation)), his *Deathly Choir* resonates with Sister Elga's *Urn of Soulcatching*.
   - The urn vibrates and its flame flares bright purple. Elga alone hears distinct dusk elf voices crying in ancient Elvish.
   - Rahadin notices the soul-drag, spots her holy symbol and urn, but dismisses her with cold arrogance (*"Carry your little jar if it brings you comfort, Sister..."*), underestimating her as a harmless novice carrying the relic of a dead church.

4. **Narrative & Encounter Design:**
   - **Endgame Payoff:** This resolution is an endgame beat—not meant to be fully tackled early on while Rahadin is far beyond the party's level.
   - **Seeding Clues:** Earlier interactions should drop hints about the unnatural soul-density of his aura and familiar Ven-like ritual runes etched into his equipment or flesh.
   - **Lightweight Implementation:** Reuse existing campaign scenes and replace other evil-enforcer beats with Rahadin (2–3 targeted swaps) to keep DM prep minimal while reinforcing his presence.
