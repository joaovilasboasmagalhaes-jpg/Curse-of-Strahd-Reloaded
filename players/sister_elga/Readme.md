# Sister Elga: Document Guide

This directory contains reference material and campaign-planning notes related to Sister Elga.

## Story Snapshot (DM-Facing)

> This snapshot combines established character facts with campaign hooks. Treat planned revelations as DM notes, not as information Elga or the other players already know.

- **Main goal:** Discover what happened to the Priory of the Ven, recover and preserve its knowledge, and rebuild the monastery while seeking justice for its destruction.
- **Most distinguishing feature:** Elga is a Priest of the Ven devoted to Kelemvor, trained to hunt undeath and guide its trapped souls to rest with her Urn of Soulcatching.
- **Story and NPC hooks:** Her missing Tome of Records, the ruined Priory, and her fear of becoming undead all offer personal stakes. The Urn's reaction to nearby undead can draw attention; a Ven symbol or her work with souls may interest religious figures, undead hunters, or the dead themselves. The planned resonance with Rahadin is an optional early breadcrumb. See [Backstory](backstory.md), [Plot Hook](plot_hook.md), and [Rahadin](rahadin.md).
