# Sister Elga: DM Plans

> [!warning] DM only. Mutable.
> Spoilers for Elga's whole arc. Written after Session 7 (Arc C, Strahd's roadside greeting). Recommended defaults are marked; change them freely. The fixed backstory truth stays at the bottom under "Monastery Event Summary."

## 1. What This Plan Fixes

- Every major reveal sat in two places: the Silent Cloister (which no arc or date points to) and the endgame (Rahadin, the Heart of Sorrow). Between them Elga had little to do.
- The existing tie-ins have NPCs recognize Elga and hand her information. She receives; she rarely decides.
- The Urn captures and communes, but nothing about it grows, so her power and her story do not advance together.
- The Cloister was written as one visit with Ferd as the climax, which made the mentor the whole arc instead of its midpoint.

The fix is a staged arc: in each act, one question, one clue, one choice, one cost, anchored to scenes the party plays anyway.

## 2. The Spine: Warden or Shepherd

Ferd held souls because he could not bear to lose them, and accidentally built the Heart of Sorrow. Strahd holds souls on purpose. The Urn is a small version of the same instrument, and it is already filling. The arc asks whether Elga becomes a **shepherd** (guides, then lets go) or a **warden** (keeps).

- **Doctrine under strain.** "Destroy undeath; die with dignity" was written for a world where death works. Session 7 already shows cracks: she avoided Doru, pledged to kill Ireena if she turns, and took in the Lansten family.
- **Flaw.** Rebuild the past at any cost, which is another way of holding on.
- **Fear.** Becoming what she hunts.
- **The blank line.** Her backstory leaves "the one line she will not cross" empty. Play answers it at the Offer (Stage 4).
- **Ruling on souls (decided).** No soul escapes Barovia while the Mists hold it. Passing is a meaningful farewell and can soothe a soul, but it cannot send one to Kelemvor or beyond the Mists. Rose and Thorn appeared to pass; Elga believed she had fulfilled her promise, but that apparent departure was not proof they escaped the cycle. Preserve the emotional truth of the moment, then let the later revelation make it more tragic and give Elga new purpose. Unbinding severs a local anchor (such as Rahadin's choir or the Heart); it still does not let a soul leave Barovia. Shepherding souls to Kelemvor becomes possible only after the Mists fall.

## 3. Design Rules

- **Gate by growth and deeds, not by date.** Each stage unlocks from something Elga does, so an early or late visit never breaks the order.
- **Question, clue, choice, cost.** Every stage gives her something to wonder about, a piece of evidence, a decision, and a price.
- **Clues come through the Urn.** She hears, feels, and perceives; NPCs confirm but never explain in full.
- **Two sources per revelation.** If she skips one scene, a second delivers the same clue.
- **No confiscation.** The Urn stays with her. Opposition pressures; it does not strip.

## 4. Where She Stands (Through Session 7)

- **Urn contents (per the logs; verify).** Walter, Tydon, Hugh Jardon, Hughie, Pard-on, and the Lansten family. Rose and Thorn were released.
- **Never accounted for.** The souls the Urn carried on arrival streamed out through the gate in Session 1.
- **Open threads.** The Lansten souls vs. the scouts still searching for them; Ismark's Record of the Lost and Missing, now in her keeping; the rune's origin; Doru; her pledge about Ireena.
- **Rahadin (Session 6).** He addressed her about her jar as a priest of the Ven. The plan assumes he implied the Priory is long gone; confirm what he actually said.
- **What the player knows.** She returned from her assignment to find the monastery in ruins, then was teleported to the outside gate of Barovia. She does not know the monastery was transported here or that the ruin in Barovia is the same place. NPCs have referred to her order or the Ven in Barovia, but she has no way to identify who or what they mean. The Session 6 log only records Rahadin addressing her as a priest of the Ven; do not assume he told her the Priory is gone. The Heart, the choir's identity, Ferd's confession, and the Sanctum seal remain unknown to her.

## 6. Stage Details

### Stage 1: The Empty Urn (Arc C, run next)

*Question: where did my souls go, and what is this place doing to the dead?*

1. **C2b, Strahd's greeting.** His gaze rests on the Urn longer than on anything else she carries. *"A lantern for lost things. I once knew a great many people who kept such lanterns."* He moves on. If pressed, he declines.
2. **The March of the Dead.** The Urn pulls toward the procession. If she tries a Passing, nothing happens; the spirits do not turn their heads. She perceives a thin bright strand running from each spirit toward the castle and cannot touch it. This is the first hint of Unbinding and the first proof that released and held differ. Do not explain.
3. **C4d, the Dancing Fire.** Her Abbot dreamed of a campfire, a dancing people, and a curse on the Great Guide, and sent her to kill them. Let the Vistani fire echo that (music, dancing, a curse on the Dark Lord). She may tell her dream in the Game of Stories; the Vistani answer in their own way. No lore here, only the question of what her Abbot was looking at.
4. **C4b, Eva (existing callout).** Add: if Elga asks where her Urn's souls went, Eva answers as fact, not fortune: *"Barovia keeps what it takes close to its heart."*
5. **Hook out.** Eva, the tower of the dead, and Strahd's remark all point her away from the road. She will want the crags. Let her want it; Stage 3 is where she gets it.

### Stage 2: The Choir Has Names (Act II)

*Question: whose voices are around Rahadin, and what happened the night the Heart was born?*

- **Kasimir (Arc E).** When Elga tells him about the voices in Elvish, he goes still, then gives her names from his house: Erevan Löwenhart and his wife Lorelei (Kasimir's aunt), and the women and children of the 353 B.C. encampment. He becomes her first ally in grief and pays off in Arc R.
- **Rahadin, rung 2 (D6 / O1).** She now hears names, and one voice answers her: "Sister." Rahadin notices her listening: *"You listen very closely, Sister."* The cost is attention; he now watches her.
- **Agni's philter.** The first time she acts against the letter of the Doctrine for a living person. Give her a private moment with the Doctrine and do not script the answer.
- **Arc H, the Wayward Souls and the Gallows Speaker.** She may Pass the five specters instead of destroying them. When Leo falls, the guards in his cloak come loose. One young soldier gives her a memory without a roll: on the night he died he heard singing from the sky in a language he did not know, then a red heart tore through the roof. Elga knows the chant: the Ven's *Lament for the Fallen*.
- **Khazan's marginalia (existing).** The hope beat: Ven monks lived on here. Hope, then dread, then confirmation is the intended emotional order across Act II and Visit 1.
- **Hook out.** She has a place to go (Argynvostholt knows) and a reason: that hymn was her order's.

### Stage 3: What the Cloister Remembers (Act III, Neyavr 11-13)

*Question: what did my order do, and who is still here?*

- **Arc K, Kala.** Kala: *"I felt like I was supposed to go somewhere, but the mist stopped me."* Elga recognizes a held soul. She cannot fix it but can teach Kala Remembrance and promise to return. At the seance she can steady Ezmerelda's circle and ask one of the questions.
- **Arc M.** Godfrey (existing) names the Cloister. Elga's Passing lets each lantern spirit speak a name and a message before its local haunting ends; it does not carry the spirit beyond the Mists. Brother Marek is her foil: a broken believer, what she becomes without answers.
- **Cloister Visit 1 (Neyavr 13).** The Urn burns long before the walls appear. The outer cloister is open: Vane (Courtyard), Theresa (Infirmary), the Tome (Library). The brethren are looped in the siege and reachable only at Affinity 1 or higher. They recognize her and plead: take the Abbot's chair and rebuild. The Tome's last pages (Ferd's hand): *"We called a heart into the sky and it screamed."* The Sanctum is sealed with Rahadin's sigil, and the Urn flinches from it as it did from him. Vane and Theresa cannot be released while the seal holds; they say Ferd is below.
- **Cost.** The Urn's flinch is felt at the other end of the same binding. At dinner Rahadin may say: *"You have been to the western crags, Sister."*
- **Hook out.** The seal and the choir are twins. To open one she must touch the other.

### Stage 4: The Offer (Neyavr 14)

*Question: what will she hold, and for whom?*

- **O5b, theology.** Strahd enjoys debating clerics, so Elga is a natural target. He turns her doctrine back on her: *"Death is a step, you say. Whose step, when the stair is locked? I only improved on your order's lock."*
- **Why Strahd wants the Urn.** It is a working Ven soul-vessel, filled with souls, and a living example of the craft he stole from the Priory. He wants to study and use it, but it is an opportunity, not a requirement for the Heart or his larger plan.
- **Why he bargains instead of taking it.** Strahd could try to seize the Urn, but that would win him only the vessel. The real prize is Elga's willing cooperation: her choosing to hold souls for him would make her repeat the Ven's original mistake and turn her into a warden like Ferd. He considers that corruption more satisfying and useful than simple theft. Do not give the Urn an unestablished anti-theft enchantment; he chooses not to take it.
- **The private word** (foyer or tour). He offers the key to the Sanctum seal: Ferd alive, the stolen volumes returned, the Priory raised again. The price: *"Hold them for me. I do not ask you to kill. Your order never could resist keeping a thing from the dark."* He does not lie, does not coerce, and is courteous if refused. If Elga declines, he proceeds without the Urn and does not punish her for refusing. He asks again in Arc U.
- **Her answer is the blank line.** Ask the player to write it on the sheet afterward.
- **Table note.** Make the offer visibly a choice, not a trap. Check with the player beforehand if unsure how they want it to land.
- **O9a, Varushka.** The Passing is the appease route. Varushka is held, so Elga can ease her but not release her; the module's locket and note complete it. A small foreshadow of Unbinding.

### Stage 5: The Heart and the Seal (Neyavr 15-16)

*Question: what does she do when the door is only open if she turns the key Strahd gave her?*

- **Arc P, the Heart tower.** She hears voices she knows: the souls her Urn carried on arrival, drawn here. The Heart is immune. She can only speak to them and promise. She also sees Ven geometry in the crystal.
- **Rahadin's Challenge.** When the choir surges (his Psychic Scream or equivalent), her reaction can seize one bound soul (Religion check; DC by Affinity tier). On success the soul enters the Urn, Rahadin staggers, and the Sanctum seal cracks. He knows what she did; from now on he hunts her.
- **Arc Q, the Abbot.** She holds the Abbot's soul steady while the Icon of Dawn's Grace works. A hold used to heal, in contrast to Ferd's and Strahd's hold used to keep.

### Stage 6: Ferd (Act IV, Cloister Visit 2)

*Question: can she forgive him, and let go of the Priory?*

- With the seal cracked, Vane and Theresa can receive their Passing rites. This is the emotional release the brethren's plea in Visit 1 set up; it does not send them beyond the Mists.
- **Ferd's confession (see [Silent Cloister](silent_cloister.md)).** The dream connects to the Vistani fire, but what Ferd believed it meant and why he sent Elga remain undecided. Do not assume he knowingly sent her to murder innocent people; his motive must make sense to him without treating her protection as justification for killing. The rune's origin is also unresolved. He teaches her the **Rite of Unbinding** while still bound.
- **Choice.** Perform the Passing for him (a meaningful farewell, not escape from Barovia), keep him as a voice in the Urn (the Warden's path; the Burden peaks), or refuse him. All valid.
- **The Sanctum's vessels** stay safe in the vault as an ark until the Mists lift.

### Stage 7: The Reckoning (Act IV)

- **Arc R.** Kasimir joins and asks Elga to free his dead. Rahadin becomes a shared enemy.
- **Arc S.** Khazan's note ("Why?") is answered. Agni's Panacea needs her. Optional: if Patrina's hilt is found, Elga holds the means to Pass Patrina and end Kasimir's hope of restoring her.
- **Arc T.** Rahadin's Vengeance is the payoff. She Unbinds the choir, freeing the dusk elves from Rahadin's anchor; their Passing rites are meaningful but cannot carry them beyond the Mists. Kasimir is present.
- **Arc U.** The Rite becomes the counter-liturgy. Used as a channeled action, it breaks the Heart's hold and frees the souls inside, including hers, from that anchor. They remain within Barovia while the Mists hold; after Strahd falls and the Mists dissipate, she can shepherd them to Kelemvor. Strahd asks again.
- **Epilogue.** She closes the Ledger (see below), reconsecrates the Cloister as the first house of the reformed Ven, and shepherds the Sanctum's souls.
- **Optional big swing (decide later).** Unbinding as the one tool that could free Tatyana's soul from the Dark Powers' claim.

## 7. The Rites (Power Track)

| Rite | Gate | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Gathering, Remembrance | Have | Capture a soul; build Affinity |
| Passing | Have | Gives a soul a farewell and eases its local haunting; does not let it escape Barovia |
| Unbinding | Ferd, Stage 6 | Severs a local hold (Rahadin, a seal, or the Heart); the soul remains within Barovia. Religion check against the binder's strength; failure risks Liturgy of Ruin |
| Shepherding | After the Mists fall | Guides the Sanctum's souls and the Urn's own to Kelemvor |

**Burden (optional).** Count unreleased souls. At 5 the Urn murmurs at night; at 10 it is warm; at 15 she dreams in the souls' voices. If you want teeth, widen the Liturgy of Ruin trigger at each step. The aim is for her to feel Ferd's problem before she meets him.

## 8. Running Threads

- **The Ledger.** Whenever the party learns a name's fate (Gertruda, Anton, Doru, the Lansten), Elga amends Ismark's Record. She returns it to Ismark closed in the epilogue.
- **The Lansten souls.** Decide soon: tell the scouts, perform Passing rites for them, or keep them in the Urn. Passing does not free them from Barovia. Each choice informs the Burden.
- **Doctrine entries.** Each time she breaks the Doctrine in practice, note it; by Stage 6 the player should recognize their own drift.

## 9. Links to Other Characters (Flag Only; Verify Against Their Plans)

- **Grim.** The Heart tower is a natural shared moment: his "heart" question beside her "my souls." Check his plan before leaning on this.
- **Agni.** Philter (Stage 2) and Panacea (Stage 7).
- **Ivan.** Two ways of handling the dead: he destroys, she shepherds. Friction and respect.
- **Caspian.** He may perceive the Mists' strands at the March of the Dead before she does.
- **Kasimir and Ezmerelda.** Kasimir is her ally in grief; Ezmerelda is her seance partner.

## 10. If the Player Goes Off-Script

- **Cloister early.** Run "First Sight": the outer cloister, looped brethren, the plea, the seal. Nothing past that opens, because the gates are deeds, not days.
- **Skips Kasimir.** The voice in the choir supplies the names, or Patrina's banshee, or Kasimir later.
- **Skips Arc H.** The hymn comes from Varushka, Leo in Arc O, or the Tome.
- **Skips Visit 1.** Godfrey, a lantern spirit, or Strahd himself (gloating) names the Cloister and the lock.
- **Refuses the Offer.** Fine; the seal cracks in Stage 5 or Arc T instead.
- **Fails the Arc P soul-seize.** The seal cracks when Rahadin falls in Arc T, and Visit 2 happens after the Mountain Fane.

## 11. Decisions & Open Questions

1. **Rune origin (unresolved).** Possibilities: an accidental byproduct of the Convergence; a countermeasure Ferd planned before the rite; a ritual Ferd made after arriving in Barovia; or an intervention by someone/something else (Vistani, Madam Eva, the Mists, the hags, or another source). Do not present any as fact yet.
2. **Can souls escape Barovia? (decided).** No, not while the Mists remain. Passing and Unbinding have real local effects but do not deliver souls to Kelemvor. The exact post-Passing fate of an unanchored soul can remain mysterious until play reveals it.
3. **Where the Urn's lost souls went (unresolved).** The Heart is the simplest option; scattering them through Barovia is also appealing. Keep both open until a later clue or choice makes one fit better.
4. **Ferd's culpability and motive (unresolved).** The dream connects to the Vistani fire. Do not assume Ferd knowingly sent Elga to kill innocents. Decide what he believed, what danger he thought he saw, and why he gave that order; his rationale may explain his choice without absolving its consequences. Protection alone is not sufficient justification.
5. **Strahd's offer (decided).** Sincere. Strahd is not known to be a liar, so he honors the terms he actually offers; do not turn the bargain into a hidden trap. State the terms clearly enough for Elga to make an informed choice.

## Monastery Event Summary (DM Notes)

### The Convergence & The Creation of the Heart of Sorrow (351 B.C.)
- **The Initial Lock-On:** In their home world, Abbot Ferd and the Priory of the Ven initiated a grand soul-cleansing divination rite designed to search the multiverse for the deepest concentration of undead suffering. The ritual locked directly onto the most cataclysmic dark event of that era: the **Wedding Massacre at Castle Ravenloft in 351 B.C.**, where Strahd forged his pact with the Dark Powers, slew Sergei, drove Tatyana off the Overlook, and butchered his guards.
- **The Misguided Attempt at Salvation:** As the Priory received overwhelming, horrific visions of the carnage unfolding in Castle Ravenloft, Abbot Ferd and his monks panicked. In a desperate act of religious duty, they channeled all their sacred soul-gathering rites through the dimensional conduit to *rescue* and purify the escaping souls of the slaughtered guards and wedding victims.
- **The Planar Inversion & The Heart’s Birth:** Because Barovia was simultaneously tearing away from reality into the Demiplane of Dread, the Priory’s divine soul-gathering matrix inverted. Instead of ushering the souls to Kelemvor's Fugue Plane, the colossal spiritual vacuum congealed the blood, despair, and trapped spirits of Strahd's first victims into a massive, crystalline soul-engine hovering at the apex of Ravenloft's high tower: **The Heart of Sorrow**.
- **The Temporal Backlash:** The dimensional feedback destroyed the monastery in the material world and dragged the entire structure through time and space, depositing it as a smoking, half-ruined priory in the shadow of Mount Ghakis / Argynvostholt in early **352 B.C.** (383 years before the current 735 B.C. campaign).

### Strahd's Hunt for the "Author" & The Rahadin Prototype
- **Strahd Discovers the Heart:** When Strahd surveyed the wreckage of his castle the morning after the massacre, he found the dried husks of his victims and the mysterious Heart of Sorrow pulsing above. He recognized that an alien, astonishingly sophisticated soul-containment matrix had formed it, but could not fully comprehend or command its potential.
- **Hunting the Source:** Intrigued, Strahd sent scouts and searched the valley to find the "author" of this miracle. Within years, he discovered the disoriented Priory of the Ven hidden in the foothills.
- **Sacking the Priory & Rahadin's Experiment (~360–375 B.C.):**
  - Strahd raided the monastery, capturing Abbot Ferd and pillaging the Ven's research on soul-anchoring vessels and urn-craft.
  - Before applying this lore to himself, Strahd used Rahadin as a living test subject. The ritual anchored Rahadin against true death, but suffered an uncalculated side effect: the screams and lingering spirits of the dusk elves Rahadin had personally butchered became permanently bound into his spiritual aura, forming his terrifying psychic death-chorus.
- **Weaponizing the Heart (590–595 B.C.):** Decades later, with the Ven's complete soul-framework deciphered, Strahd captured the vestige of the Devourer from the Amber Temple, sealed it within the Heart of Sorrow, and channeled the stolen power of the Ladies Three (the Fanes) into it, binding the life of Barovia itself to his own survival.

### Present State (735 B.C.) & Sister Elga’s Arc
- **The Monastery Ruin:** The monastery is now a 380-year-old ancient ruin in Barovia (detailed in [players/sister_elga/silent_cloister.md](players/sister_elga/silent_cloister.md)). Within it lies:
  - The charred remnants of the original *Tome of Records*.
  - The trapped, restless spirits of Elga's long-lost brethren waiting to be shepherded home by her Urn.
  - The tragic, sealed fate of Abbot Ferd, who witnessed the horrific consequences of his well-intentioned rite.
- **Cross-Campaign Breadcrumbs & World Touchpoints:**
  1. **Rahadin's Arrival ([Arc B](Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia.md#Rahadin’s%20Proclamation)):** His deathly choir triggers a violent purple resonance in Elga's Urn; Rahadin mocks her dead church and dismisses her urn as a harmless trinket.
  2. **Madam Eva ([Arc C](Act I - Into the Mists/Arc C - Into the Valley.md#C4b.%20Madam%20Eva's%20Tent)):** Recognizes Elga as the "little sister from the storm," noting that Ferd came to her tent centuries ago. True to her divine rules, she refuses to disclose what she told Ferd, pointing Elga toward the mountain crags beneath Mount Ghakis and the nearby halls of Argynvostholt.
  3. **Khazan's Spellbook & Victor ([Arc H](Act II - The Shadowed Town/Arc H - The Lost Soul.md#The%20Fate%20of%20Stella%20Wachter)):** Contains Khazan's research notes on the Ven's soul-anchoring formulas, assisting in resolving Stella Wachter's displaced soul.
  4. **Sir Godfrey Gwilym ([Arc M](Act III - The Broken Land/Arc M - The Dragon's Manor.md#What%20Godfrey%20Knows)):** Remembers the Priory offering spiritual solace and silver to the revenants before Strahd sacked the cloisters.
  5. **Krezkov Basement Shackles ([Arc K](Act III - The Broken Land/Arc K - The Fallen Abbey.md#Hidden%20Basement)):** The silver shackles and salt wards bear the scales of Kelemvor, crafted by Ven Doomguides for the early settlers of Krezk.
- **The Core Revelation:** Elga discovers that her church wasn't destroyed by random villains—her order's attempt to intervene in Strahd's founding tragedy backfired, literally *creating* the Heart of Sorrow and giving Strahd and Rahadin their greatest defenses.
- **The Divine Countermeasure (The True Shepherd):** Because both Rahadin's soul-shield and the Heart of Sorrow are built upon corrupted Ven liturgy, Sister Elga’s *Urn of Soulcatching* is the only divine tool capable of dismantling them:
  1. She harvests/cleanses the dusk elf souls surrounding Rahadin, stripping his deathless recovery and making him mortal.
  2. She unbinds the sacred soul-geometry sustaining the Heart of Sorrow.
  3. She gathers her fallen brothers, sisters, and mentor, granting them absolution.
  4. At the campaign's conclusion, with the Mists dissipated, she shepherds four centuries of trapped Barovian souls into Kelemvor's true afterlife and establishes a reformed Order of the Ven.
