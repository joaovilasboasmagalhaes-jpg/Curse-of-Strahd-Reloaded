# Sister Elga

## The Beginning.

Thunder rolled outside of the monastery, and the rain drops aggressively bashed the windows of the monastery. Around her, Sister Elga could hear the quiet prayer of her sisters and brothers. 

A flash of white lit up the stained glass panels that decorated the windows of the monastery, beautifully illuminating the pictures depicted in them.

**They weren’t nice.** Folk knew what transpired within that monastery, and they avoided it like the plague. Monks and priests dressed in garments that would likely mean Death was approaching. 

It was a dark omen to see a Priest of the Ven around. They were known as Hunters. Once fully trained, they would set out to the world, wandering throughout the many cities and villages that dotted the continent. Chasing Undeath was their calling, and to punish any who would enforce it upon soulless bodies. Then, they would learn their rituals, study them, document them, and send them back to the monastery, so that others could study and learn from it.

Elga did not remember why she was here. She simply was. Her caretakers had said something about a lone basket being left at the door of the monastery when she was just a wee girl. 

The monks and clerics of the monastery ensured that her wellbeing was taken care of for the most part of her childhood, but it didn’t take long for her to start working and aiding around the monastery.

Surprisingly, there was a whole lot to do within it. Organizing the Monastery’s library, helping with alchemical preparations, cooking food for the other brothers and sisters, and of course, prayer. Every now and then, the friars would allow her to take part in the combat drills, which would allow her to begin learning the basics rather soon in her life.

## The Ritual of Doom Guidance

**Another flash and roll of thunder hit.**   
Her breath was shaky, and her back clung to the trunk of a nearby tree. The forest was eerily quiet and she knew something may have been hunting her. 

Her rite of Doom guidance had started a few hours ago, her task was to locate any undead dwelling within the forest that neighbored the monastery. She knew from gossip with her fellow monks that the undead would have been placed there carefully by her brothers, but that did not diminish the task ahead.

She let her breath catch up, the adrenaline pumping through her body, urging her on to move. She grasped the holy symbol on her neck, the golden scales, grasped by a skeletal hand, that marked her devotion to the Great Guide, Kelemvor.

Uttering a small prayer for guidance, she felt her senses sharpening. She could hear it then. Rhythmless footsteps, odd thumps against the earth and roots that spread throughout the forest. 

It took her a few moments to make a decision on the strategy. She considered stealth for a moment, but she had never been one for sneaky endeavors. So, she chose the most natural of her instincts. Face the danger head on, with her God at her back. 

Once more she uttered a prayer, this time around, one filled with wrath and holy retribution. The dark forest filled itself with light as the defiled creature burst aflame. It thrashed around for a few seconds before its body laid limp on the forest floor. 

Elga approached it, and began a small ritual of purification. She unstrapped the Urn of Soulcatching from her belt, its glow gone soft. She quickly set it down and began chanting a prayer of redeeming, attempting to make right the wrong-fullness of undeath. The urn began shining, a twisted purple as it attempted to capture the soul of the body. 

Of all the rituals Elga was taught within the monastery this was always the one that strained her the most. Small beads of sweat began coalescing at the top of her forehead, one eventually trickling into her eye. It stung, but she let the pain be the bridge between the soul and her Urn. 

Abruptly, the Urn ceased glowing and the strain lifted. It felt like a success. It was time to head back.

## The Assignment

**A flash of bright light and the rumble of the sky is heard.** 

Elga kneels before the Abbot of the Monastery. He smiles fondly:  
“You’ve come far since the beginning of your journey here, Sister Elga.” \- he places an old, boney hand on Elga’s shoulder \- “But I cannot help it but believe that our Great Guide has further plans for you.”   
“I hope He does, Abbot Ferd. I am His hand.” \- a brief response but one that is suitable.  
“Answered like a true believer, Sister. Now that you have passed your Doomguidance Ritual, I am sure you know what sort of life lies ahead of you?”  
“Of course, your Eminence. My role is clear as daylight, and my path is paved by the many who have shared this burden before. I will gather my belongings, set out at Dawn, and come back in the Winter to train the newer blood.” Elga bows low, she would never offend the Abbot, despite not always agreeing with the way he ran the Priory.  
“You are not yet dismissed, Sister.” The Abbot’s voice takes on a rather ominous tone, and Elga is unsure if she should stand her ground or run. She goes against the best of her instincts and remains kneeling.  
“What would you have me do, your Eminence?” Elga asked, uncertain to her fate.  
“I have been plagued by ominous dreams. Something is afoot within our woods. Surely, you have felt it too?”  
“Your Eminence, I have not.”  
“Perhaps more prayer would do you good, Sister.”  
“Of course, Your Eminence. What would you have me do?”  
“The dreams are filled with devilish music. I see a campfire burning in our woods. A people, dancing and cursing the name of the Great Guide. Such heresy can only be dealt with in one way, do you understand me, Sister?”  
Elga swallows dry. Of all the tasks she dreamt about once she became a Priest of the Ven, cold blooded killing was not something she had foreseen.   
“As your Eminence commands it.”  
“Very good, Sister. You may set out in the morning. Leave me to my prayer.”  
Elga rose to her feet, sweat already threatening to leave her pores. She could feel the bile rising in her stomach as anxiety set in, but she focused on the mission ahead. She would rest, then set out to find these people. 

## The Motive

**A flash of lightning and a thunderous boom echoes throughout the region.**

There's flames. Elga stands on the entrance to the monastery. So far, her mission had been a failure, she had not managed to track any people within the forest, despite being out there for around a week. Exhausted, and running low on supplies, Elga made her way back to the Monastery to find it alight.  
Bodies arranged in weird, macabre poses, her brothers and sisters. She utters a small prayer to Kelemvor, seeking guidance, but hears nothing back. She searches the rubble, looking for anything, praying the fire doesn’t spread to the forest. It doesn’t. But it also doesn’t stop the empty feeling she now has in her stomach. Elga has failed, she’s been a priest of the Ven for less than a week, and her first mission causes the small ecosystem that she was brought up in to collapse.  
There is no way to prove it, but she assumes that whoever the Abbot had been speaking of was the cause for her monastery to be destroyed. Now, there was only one thing left to do. Search for the people who did this, and make them pay. **Rebuild, and start anew.**

# **Duties of a Priest of the Ven**

## Alchemical Proficiency

Priests of the Ven are renowned scholars of the alchemical arts \- the fabrication of ointments and potions is essential for the survival in the wild, and, as such, they normally transport with themselves a set of Alchemist Tools to perform these duties.   
However, these skills are not only used for their own good, if the civilization they are within requires intervention in terms of potions or other kinds, such as poisons and others, it is the duty of a Priest to perform, to the best of his abilities, concoctions that could aid civilians. The Priests expect payment nonetheless, but there are exceptional cases in which said payment can be forgone.

## Hunting Undeath

Perhaps the most imperative function of a Priest of the Ven, they are responsible for hunting down Undeath and any plagues that run parallel with it. It is a reason why commoners do not enjoy seeing a Priest of the Ven around, for they claim they bring along with them Dark creatures.   
This is a misconception, obviously, as it is their duty to chase, and investigate, such dark rituals, and as such, it is obvious that whenever a Priest of the Ven is around, it is likely something has gone awry somewhere.  
Alongside hunting Undeath, it is also their responsibility to store and create the knowledge regarding the way that Undeath gets created. With that, they often carry a Tome of Records that will help them keep track of the situations they have encountered.

## Tome of Records Retrieval

In the event that a Priest of the Ven perishes, it is imperative that their Tome of Records is retrieved and then stored somewhere restricted, to train future Priests of the Ven in the knowledge gathered by previous masters.

## Soul Catching and Soul Treating

Priests of the Ven understand that souls that are mauled by Undeath must undergo a special treatment, and must be harvested so not to disturb the wellbeing of mortals.   
So, it is one of the duties of the Priests of the Ven to conduct rituals that allow for the Souls of the Undeath to be restored, and sent back to the gates of Kelemvor.  
These rituals bring the clerics closer to Kelemvor, and are an essential part of the path that is undertaken by them.   
The Souls that dwell in each of the Priest’s  Urns are almost as important as the Cleric’s life, and it should be their goal to restore them to Kelemvor.

# **Kelemvor’s Doctrine & Values**

“Death is but part of life: fear it not, evade it not, and view it not as evil. To fear death delivers you into the hands of those who can bring death down upon you. Die with dignity, neither raging nor seeking to embrace undeath. Do honor to the dead, for their strivings in life brought the Material Plane to where it is now, and to forget them is to forget also where we are now \- and why.” \- The guide to an initiate.

* The Doctrine of Kelemvor is all about dealing with the process of Death. It is known that Kelemvor guides souls to the next step in the Afterlife.  
* Death is a beginning, not an end. A necessity, rather than a punishment. A necessary step in continuing a Soul’s journey.   
* Seek out people close to death, let them know that Kelemvor will receive them justly and mercifully.  
* Remember the dead, so that the living may prosper from it.  
* **Undeath is an abomination, and it must be destroyed whenever possible.** **As should any that attempt to prolong their lives unnaturally.**

## Rituals

* Lament for the Fallen \- A prayer performed at battlefields, or where many people were killed recently. Mostly done in a singing format.  
* **Passing** \- Funeral rites, a signal to Kelemvor that a soul is headed His way, and to remain alert for its essence.  
* **Remembrance** \- a daily ritual where the souls of the dead are remembered, can be a prayer or a song.  
* **Daeum** \- A ritual exclusive to members of the church. A thanks to Kelemvor for granting his followers the strength and courage to perform the necessary deeds and goals of his Church.
