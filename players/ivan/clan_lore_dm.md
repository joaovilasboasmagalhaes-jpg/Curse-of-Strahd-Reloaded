# Clan Venatricho: Mutable DM Lore

> [!warning] DM-only canon
> This document is mutable planning material. It is not player-facing canon. Details become fixed only when they are deliberately revealed in play or copied into a player document.

## 1. The Two Clans

The werewolves of Barovia descend from the Wolfir, hunters once blessed by the Huntress. Centuries ago, after Strahd's conquest, the Wolfir divided into two branches.

### Clan Venatricho

Clan Venatricho settled in the southern Svalich Woods. It rejected Strahd's authority, abandoned the consumption of humanoid flesh, and preserved a disciplined form of inherited lycanthropy. Its members live as a hidden community rather than as a roaming monster pack.

The clan is unified. It has one leader, one Inner Circle, and one shared culture. No ordinary member knows that another werewolf clan exists.

### The Northern Pack

The northern werewolves settled around Lake Baratok. They accepted Strahd's authority, worshipped Mother Night, and preserved the violent practice of consuming humanoid flesh to embrace the curse's power.

Strahd permits the northern pack to pass through the Mists and hunt beyond Barovia. They are useful predators, scouts, and occasional raiders, but not trusted equals.

The northern pack believes the southern branch is extinct or irrelevant. The southern clan believes it is the only surviving inherited werewolf community.

## 2. Why the Secret Survived

The secret is not maintained by one perfect spell. It survives through several overlapping causes.

### The Old Boundary Pact

When the Wolfir divided, the two branches agreed to avoid one another. Each side would not cross the other's hunting grounds, reveal its existence, or take prisoners alive. Over generations, this became a sacred taboo.

Neither side actively searched for the other. The northern pack assumed the southern branch had died out; the southern clan treated the old branch as a lost part of history.

### Different Ways of Hiding

The two clans also became difficult to connect to one another:

- Clan Venatricho lives in concealed First Folk caves and avoids settlements, roads, and outsiders.
- Its hunters use false trails, decoy camps, scent-masking smoke, and old paths that do not appear on modern maps.
- The northern pack is mobile and often leaves Barovia through the Mists, appearing beyond the valley as unrelated werewolf attacks.
- The two groups use different names, symbols, religious language, and hunting customs.

A Barovian who encountered either group would simply report seeing a werewolf. No one would naturally conclude that two related clans existed.

### The Fading Wards

The southern caves retain old First Folk wards tied to the Huntress. The wards do not make the clan invisible. Instead, they encourage misdirection: outsiders lose their way, overlook concealed paths, or remember the woods incorrectly after leaving.

The wards are weakening because Strahd severed the old connection between the Huntress and the land. The clan has remained hidden for centuries, but its protection is no longer reliable.

## 3. Strahd's Relationship to the Secret

Strahd does not preserve the separation because he considers the clans a threat. He has no reason to fear Clan Venatricho:

- It is small and isolated.
- It does not raid settlements.
- It does not seek political influence.
- It does not challenge his rule.
- Its wards and secrecy make it difficult to find, but not strategically important.
- The northern pack already fills the role of useful werewolf servants.

Strahd may know that an older southern branch once existed, but his information is ancient and incomplete. He likely considers it extinct, harmless, or not worth investigating. He has no reason to expose the clan's existence to its own members.

After Strahd awakens, his renewed demand for fealty is directed at the werewolves who already serve or are known to him. Kiril learns about that demand through his contact with the northern pack and through his private dealings with Baba Lysaga. Ivan never sees an emissary and does not know that this process is occurring.

Kiril uses Strahd's name and reputation to threaten Ivan, but Ivan does not know whether Strahd personally knows about Clan Venatricho.

## 4. Skennis's Knowledge and Plan

Skennis is the only living member of Clan Venatricho who knows the full history of the two branches. He also knows that the wards are failing.

His original plan was gradual and conservative:

1. Continue hiding the clan while the wards remain usable.
2. Train Ivan to become the next leader.
3. Reveal the northern clan and the true history of the schism to Ivan only after Ivan was ready to carry the burden.
4. Move vulnerable families and children to secondary shelters in stages, so a failing ward would not expose the entire community at once.
5. Preserve the clan's customs and records until the Huntress's connection could be restored.
6. Eventually seek a way to renew the wards through the old Fane rites, but only after the clan had a leader capable of protecting the ritual and controlling what knowledge was released.

Skennis did not intend to lead the clan into open war with Strahd. He believed survival depended on patience, secrecy, and preserving enough of the old tradition for a future generation to repair what had been damaged.

His greatest mistake was waiting too long to tell Ivan. He thought the truth would endanger Ivan before Ivan was ready. Instead, Kiril discovered the secret first.

## 5. The Petrovich Family

- **Old Skennis Petrovich:** Former leader of Clan Venatricho. He preserved the hidden history and trained Ivan to succeed him.
- **Martha Petrovich:** Ivan, Emil, and Kiril's deceased mother. Her deathbed promise remains Ivan's central moral obligation: protect his brothers and do not let the Mists divide them.
- **Ivan Petrovich:** Eldest son and intended successor. His inherited lycanthropy is currently suppressed by starvation, trauma, and exile.
- **Kiril Petrovich / Kiril Stoyanovich:** Middle son. He discovered the northern pack, accepted corrupted power, and forced Ivan into exile.
- **Emil Petrovich / Emil Toranescu:** Youngest son. He was Ivan's closest confidant and later resisted Kiril after Ivan's departure.

Kiril and Emil's alternate surnames are personal or social names, not evidence that they belong to different families.

## 6. The Huntress and the Fane Naming Problem

The campaign's DM canon identifies the Huntress's sacred place as the **Forest Fane at Yester Hill**. The southern clan's oral tradition uses older and less precise language, and some clan records call it the "Mountain Fane" because they associate the Huntress with the mountain ranges and the old hunting grounds.

This is a naming error preserved by centuries of oral transmission, not a second Huntress fane. Ivan can sincerely remember the name he was taught without that memory establishing the Fane's true location.

The player-facing material should not be silently rewritten. If this distinction becomes relevant in play, it can be revealed as a fragment of lost or corrupted clan history.

## 7. Kiril's Discovery

Kiril violates the old boundary taboo during an unauthorized hunt. He pursues prey into territory that the southern clan avoids and encounters a northern werewolf.

The creature reveals only fragments before Kiril and the hunters kill it:

- another werewolf clan exists;
- it serves Strahd;
- it can cross the Mists;
- it is physically stronger;
- and it embraces practices the southern clan rejected.

Kiril confronts Skennis. Skennis confirms enough to prove that Kiril's discovery is real, but refuses to reveal the entire history or the location of the northern den.

Kiril interprets this as proof that Skennis deliberately kept the clan weak.

## 8. The Betrayal and Exile

Strahd awakens after the failed rebellion from the Village of Barovia. Around this time, Kiril seeks power through Baba Lysaga and offers loyalty to Strahd's cause.

Kiril consumes humanoid flesh and accepts the witch's corruption. He becomes stronger, more resilient, and increasingly unstable.

Skennis then begins behaving strangely. Whether he is charmed, threatened, or sacrificing his authority to protect the clan remains unresolved. Publicly, he supports Kiril's succession.

Kiril privately confronts Ivan. He does not tell Ivan about the northern pack or the full truth of his bargain. He tells Ivan only that Strahd's attention has turned toward the clan and that resistance will bring its destruction.

Kiril offers Ivan a false choice: challenge him and endanger the clan, or leave and allow the clan to survive. Ivan chooses exile and writes the letter that makes his departure appear voluntary.

At this point, the southern clan remains unified. No ordinary member knows what has truly happened.

## 9. What Happens After Ivan Leaves

After Ivan is gone, Kiril claims full authority. He tells the clan that Ivan abandoned his responsibilities and that Skennis is too weak to lead.

Kiril then moves the entire clan north under the pretense of finding safer territory and stronger allies. Only after the clan has left its old home does he reveal the existence of the northern pack.

The northern werewolves do not welcome Clan Venatricho as equals. Kiril attempts to prove his worth through his corrupted strength and his submission to Strahd.

The southern clan is absorbed into the northern den:

- Skennis is imprisoned and starved.
- Emil resists Kiril and is defeated.
- Emil is delivered to Castle Ravenloft.
- Zuleika is imprisoned in Mother Night's shrine.
- Some southern werewolves submit willingly; others obey from fear or confusion.

By the time Ivan reaches Barovia, he does not know that his clan has moved north, that Skennis is alive, or that Emil has been taken to Castle Ravenloft.

## 10. Canon Questions Still Open

These remain intentionally unresolved until they become useful in play:

- Did Strahd ever learn the exact location of Clan Venatricho?
- Was Skennis charmed, threatened, or acting according to a hidden plan when he endorsed Kiril?
- How much of the southern clan's history survives in the hidden vault?
- Did any southern werewolves resist Kiril openly after the move?
- Does the northern pack believe Kiril is their true leader, or merely Strahd's appointed intermediary?
- Can the old wards be restored without fully reconsecrating the Forest Fane?
