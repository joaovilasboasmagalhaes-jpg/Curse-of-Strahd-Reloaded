# Ivan Petrovich: Campaign Integration

> [!warning] Mutable DM document
> This document contains campaign-facing consequences and implementation notes. The mutable canon for the clans, family history, secrecy, Skennis's ward plan, and Ivan's pre-campaign chronology lives in [Clan Lore](clan_lore_dm.md).

## Arc Spine

**Theme: division and return.** Martha's last words to her sons were "Do not let the Mists divide you." Ivan's exile was meant to prevent a division and caused one. His instinct is to protect people by leaving, hiding, and carrying the truth alone. He first has to choose his family over his fear; later, he can choose to restore the Huntress's bond with the land. The arc's final personal goal is not simply defeating Kiril or retrieving the spear, but deciding what Ivan will do with the inheritance those acts recover.

- **Wound:** He ran, and the people he ran for believe he is a coward.
- **False belief:** Protecting people means disappearing. He is not a leader. Infected werewolves are beyond saving and should be avoided.
- **Table evidence (Sessions 2-7):** He buries the natural dead with care (Rose and Thorn), wants to destroy what he reads as unnatural (Walter, Doru), and after sparing Doru told the party "This is the last time you make me do something like this."
- **What he learns:** The divided can be reunited. Mercy and purity are not opposites. A leader stands with the pack and tells it the truth. His family's gift is not only a private bloodline or a lost custom; it can help heal the Huntress's wounded relationship with Barovia.
- **End state:** He accepts responsibility by choice. He may become a leader of his people, but his defining act is helping restore a living bond between the Huntress, her Wolfir, and the land.

Ivan learns the lore in pieces. The Reveal Ledger at the end of this document tracks what he knows at each point; check it before moving a reveal earlier.

## Stages

1. **Hunted** (Arc C): a direction and the first sign that home is tied to the valley's werewolves.
2. **The First Moon** (Act II): his people are close, and his lie has a cost.
3. **Pilgrimage** (Act III opening): he stands where his father said the Huntress was wronged, and Krezk shows him the curse is not one thing.
4. **Kin** (Arcs L to P): he finds his family and chooses mercy where he once chose distance.
5. **Alpha** (Arc Q): he faces Kiril and speaks to the pack.
6. **Heir of the Huntress** (Arc T and Epilogue): he offers his Wolfir bond in the reconsecration of the Forest Fane. This deepens and empowers the ritual but is not required for the party to reconsecrate the Fanes.

## Stage 1: Hunted (Arc C)

Ivan starts Suppressed and unable to shift, with no idea where his clan is. Rahadin's sniff in Arc B told him the castle noticed him. This stage gives him a direction, not answers.

- **C2b, Strahd's greeting.** Strahd does not recognize Ivan as a member of Clan Venatricho and must not use the superseded "your line survives" greeting. He greets Ivan from the party's introduction and what his spies observed. Strahd knows Kiril pledged loyalty and has received a brief report of his southern origin, but has had little contact with him. If Ivan identifies Kiril as his brother or raises the clan, Strahd can acknowledge only that limited report; he does not know Ivan or Skennis are members or relatives. He does not automatically detect Wolfir or lycanthropy. Use the Ivan-specific disrespect lines in Arc C only if Strahd has learned Ivan is local; otherwise use the standard outsider lines.
- **C4b, Eva's epithet.** Give Ivan an epithet that is true but opaque, such as "the wolf who shut the door behind him."
- **C4d, Van Richten's manuscript.** It describes only infected werewolves. Ivan can say what it leaves out without saying how he knows.
- **C4f, the Tarokka.** The Five of Swords reads "a den of wolves in the hills overlooking a mountain lake. The treasure belongs to Mother Night." Ivan hears Mother Night, not the Huntress, and can reasonably assume an infected pack that is not his own. Let him assume it.
- **C4b/f, the Seeker's signs.** Eva's easy fondness for Muriel and her reading of the wolf-den card hint that the old faith's stories are connected to living people. Do not explain the Huntress's place in Eva's history here.
- **C7a, the watching wolves.** Ivan alone can read the two larger wolves on the crag: paired lookouts, upwind, on high ground. That is Venatricho outpost craft, not feral behavior.
- **C11, the Werewolves' Hunt.** The two dead werewolves bear fresh bite-scars, so they were infected recently, and they hunt as trained hunters. Optional: one wears a rough vertical line of blood on the brow, a corrupted version of Ivan's own Blood Rite. Someone is teaching infected werewolves his clan's customs.

Ivan already buried Rose and Thorn and later chose to spare Doru. Build on those played moments, not over them: he has demonstrated that he can honor the dead and make a hard choice to protect someone he distrusts. Let the pack notice this slowly, through reactions to his rites and his decisions, rather than telling him what his growth means.

## Stage 2: The First Moon (Act II, Neyavr 2 to 9)

- **Arc E, Lake Baratok.** Ivan catches pine-resin smoke and old Venatricho trail-signs: the false trails and decoy camps he was taught to lay. His clan is here, in the country the Tarokka tied to Mother Night. He cannot find the den without a stakeout and a plan, but he now knows it exists.
- **Optional Arc E change, Zsolt and Mathilda (E7).** If you want an earlier personal encounter, make the two werewolves members of Clan Venatricho. Mathilda, the quiet one, was one of the hard instructors who trained Ivan; Zsolt has accepted Kiril's new direction, while Mathilda has not. Her first words could echo his lie: "You wrote that you weren't ready." This reveals that the clan is in the west before Arc L, so use it only if you want to move that discovery earlier. Otherwise keep E7 as written and let it provide rumors only; Ivan's clan location remains unknown until Arc L.
- **Arc E, rumors.** Kasimir's and Szoldar's "new, harsher alpha" now lands on a name Ivan already suspects.
- **Arc H, the first full moon (Neyavr 8).** A forced change without Emil beside him. Whether it is his first away from family depends on when the exile happened relative to the Octyavr 22 moon, which is yours to set. The change breaks through suppression: the blood still answers. It falls on the night of the Ethereal Plane ritual, so decide how to stage it (see Open Decisions).
- **Arc I, Krezk and Berez.** Ivan smells the curse on Baron Dmitri, who is hiding it. Baba Lysaga catches Ivan's scent and treats it as a joke ("another of that boy's litter"); she confirms only that a wolf came begging for Strahd's favor and she gave him a gift. If Ivan is offered the Abbot's cure for lycanthropy, he refuses: "never deny the night" is one of his clan's taboos.

The Act II question is not yet "will Ivan lead the clan?" He still has no evidence that his people are nearby. It is whether he will act on what the signs suggest, and whether he can treat unfamiliar lycanthropes as individuals rather than as a category he was taught to fear.

## Stage 3: Pilgrimage (Act III opening, Arcs J and K)

- **Arc J, the Huntress's monument and Yester Hill.** Ivan recognizes the neglected wolf monument as a remnant of his faith. At the hill, Kavan's ghost confirms that the Huntress's Fane is the Forest Fane and tells where the spear lies (King Dostron's tomb). This makes the Ravenloft heist Ivan's mission as well as Emil's rescue. Kavan also establishes that Strahd's corruption of the land is ongoing, not only a historical wound. If a PC holds the *wolf's tooth*, Kavan recognizes it as the Huntress's.
- **Arc J, the Whispering Wall.** Ivan's clan believes natural-dead children of the Huntress return to the Wall, and that Martha is there. Strahd invites the party in to face visions of their deepest desires. The module makes the Wall the Devourer's corpse, so whether Ivan's vision of his mother is real or bait is an open decision. The test is whether he can walk away from his dead, not combat.
- **Arc K, Krezk.** The silver shackles in the cottage basement and Ilya's story show Ivan a boy of thirteen who was lied to, then killed his sister on his first moon. Ivan knows the training Ilya never had.

## Stage 4: Kin (Arcs L to P)

### Arc L: The Den of Wolves

By now Ivan knows the den exists, that his clan is in it, and that Kiril leads. The den confirms and complicates that: Skennis is alive and a prisoner, Emil is in Ravenloft, and a second clan exists. It is also the first major revelation of Caspian's backstory: this is the pack whose raiding party destroyed his home village of Ashcairn. See [Caspian's Campaign Integration](../caspian/dm_campaign_integration.md) for how the two reveals should share the scene without competing for focus.

- Bianca recognizes Ivan as Kiril's brother and becomes emotionally conflicted rather than simply treating him as another intruder.
- Skennis recognizes Ivan by scent and voice. His reaction should be immediate, but his first priority is getting Ivan to understand that the exile was not what it appeared to be.
- Skennis can reveal that Kiril discovered a forbidden part of their history, manipulated the clan, and moved them north after Ivan left.
- Skennis should not explain every unresolved detail. His memory, starvation, and fear leave room for uncertainty about whether he was charmed, threatened, or attempting to protect Ivan when he supported Kiril.
- Zuleika can explain Emil's perspective: Emil believed Ivan abandoned the family, but resisted Kiril after Ivan's departure.
- The *Holy Symbol of Ravenkind* remains the immediate reason to cooperate with Zuleika. Rescuing Emil becomes Ivan's personal reason to accept the oath.
- Ilya is the Doru test again, with Ivan's own kind. Ivan is now the one best placed to argue for a cursed child and the only one who can teach him control. Let Ivan decide whether to begin; it starts what Emil continues in the Epilogue.
- Ezmerelda's Black Terror story at the L3a campfire is told in front of Ivan. See Open Decisions.

### Arc O: Dinner with the Devil

If Emil's later disposition places him in Castle Ravenloft before Dinner with the Devil, Strahd may refer to holding a werewolf prisoner. Do not assume Emil is there: he is not a Ravenloft prisoner at Session 8, and his later fate is undecided. Strahd does not know Emil is Ivan's brother unless that is established through a later report or reveal. Adapt the source line about the pack to Ivan's canon before using it.

### Arc P: Ravenloft Heist

- Ivan encounters Emil in Castle Ravenloft's dungeons.
- Their scene should focus first on the emotional damage caused by the false farewell letter, then on Emil's imprisonment and Kiril's betrayal.
- Emil does not need to know every detail of Kiril's bargain. He can confirm what he witnessed and identify which parts remain uncertain.
- The *Spear of the Huntress*, also called *Bloodthorn* in the old histories, is the ancestral relic connected to the Wolfir and Kavan's corruption of the Huntress's gift. Kavan already told Ivan where it lies (Arc J). Once the Huntress blesses it, it grants *hunter's mark* without concentration, which pairs with Ivan's Prey; if Ivan presents it to her in Arc T, play that as his moment.

## Stage 5: Alpha (Arc Q)

### Arc Q: A Shining Beacon

Kiril's confrontation should be personal rather than expositional. He can accuse Ivan of abandoning the clan and mock the old discipline of Clan Venatricho. He presents his corrupted strength as proof that he was right.

If Ravka Ashclaw survived Arc L, she makes her last stand here alongside Kiril, giving Caspian his own reckoning in parallel (see [Caspian's Campaign Integration](../caspian/dm_campaign_integration.md)). Keep Kiril's scene focused on Ivan; Ravka's fight is Caspian's, not a competing claim on Kiril's narrative attention.

His transformation into the mutant lycan represents the final defilement of inherited Wolfir power through hunger, coercion, and Strahd's influence.

After Kiril's death, Ivan may perform the Law of the Clean Kill and place stones over his brother's eyes. This gives Kiril a final human moment without excusing what he did.

The encounter triggers as written (the Symbol, or failed scent-masking) or because Mathilda reported Ivan in Arc E.

Split the module's pack-choice speech: Emil addresses the northern pack members who followed him, and Ivan addresses the southern clan members who knew him. What Ivan says to them is his first act as the leader he did not want to be.

Kiril's flesh is humanoid, and clan law forbids feeding on it, so Ivan can only take the half-value hunting ritual to convert his Hunt Points. Let his brother's corruption and his own mechanic rhyme.

After Kiril falls, the first decisions fall to Ivan: Skennis's last days, Bianca, the surviving southern clan, and whether the rest of the pack can be redeemed.

## Stage 6: Heir of the Huntress (Arc T and Epilogue)

### Arc R: Trials of the Mountain

Soldav is the first community Ivan has met that shares his faith. Burebis and the Mountain Folk confirm what Kavan told him: the Huntress's Fane is the Forest Fane at Yester Hill, not a "Mountain Fane." Ivan is corrected by people who mean it kindly. If a PC holds the *wolf's tooth*, Burebis recognizes it.

### Arc T: The Three Fanes

The party's existing Arc T route remains complete without Ivan: the necessary relationships are healed, the enchanted gems are recovered and buried, and the Fanes can be reconsecrated. Ivan is not a key the party must bring or a substitute for another character's quest.

If Ivan is present, give him an optional but consequential role at the Forest Fane. The living Wolfir bloodline is a connection the original ritual does not require, but the Huntress can use it as an additional channel. Ivan chooses to run a Hunt around the stones, naming or marking a willing Prey and refusing to let the Hunt become a slaughter. The spear answers his call; this is the point where its blessing and his custom become one.

On success, the Huntress's spirit can manifest more clearly through the Fane and briefly contest Strahd's claim. A modest payoff could suppress one Forest-Fane benefit Strahd would otherwise wield in the final confrontation, or give the party one clear opening against it. If Ivan is absent, declines, or the party has not recovered the spear, the standard gem-and-quest reconsecration still succeeds; the Huntress is weaker or less present, but the campaign proceeds.

The restored Forest Fane can reveal the deeper truth of Ivan's inheritance:

- the Wolfir gift is not the same as the infected curse;
- the southern clan preserved a damaged but legitimate tradition;
- Kiril's power was not a restoration, but a corruption;
- Ivan's suppressed transformation reflects trauma and the broken connection to the Huntress, not personal failure.

After the party restores the Fane, the Huntress can speak through Ivan in a full sentence, once, where she otherwise manages three words. This is recognition and a choice offered to Ivan, not a prophecy that appoints him leader. Any permanent mechanical reward should be decided separately from this narrative revelation.

## Epilogue Possibilities

If Strahd is defeated and the Fanes are restored, Ivan and Emil may help rebuild a peaceful Wolfir tradition. Their responsibilities could include:

- recovering surviving members of Clan Venatricho;
- deciding whether the northern werewolves can be redeemed or must remain separate;
- teaching inherited lycanthropes to control their transformations;
- helping Ilya Krezkov understand his condition;
- and deciding whether the old secrecy is still necessary.

## Open Decisions

- **Full moon against the Arc H ritual (Neyavr 8).** Options: he changes at moonrise and enters the Ethereal Plane as a wolf whose scent works on spirits, giving him a role against Leo Dilisnya; or the change is a short scene earlier that evening. Avoid leaving him behind. Decide how much control he keeps (his notes say he recognizes friends).
- **Zsolt and Mathilda as clan members.** Optional and not yet canon. If used, it moves "the clan has gone west" from Arc L to Arc E. Decide whether inherited clan members' bites spread the curse.
- **The Black Terror.** Ezmerelda's attacker raided beyond the Mists two years ago, but Kiril only changed three months ago and the southern clan cannot cross the Mists. Either the Black Terror was a northern hunter Kiril replaced, or Arc L and Arc Q stop having Ezmerelda recognize Kiril.
- **Emil and Zuleika.** Arcs I and K say Zuleika left Krezk 13 to 14 years ago, and she is Dmitri's elder sister (he has a 13-year-old son). Merging her with 19-year-old Emil means editing those arcs as well as Arc L, or making her his mentor rather than his mate.
- **The *wolf's tooth*.** Does any PC hold it? If Ivan does, Arcs J, R, and T pay off.
- **The Whispering Wall.** Ivan's clan believes their dead go there; the module makes it the Devourer's corpse. Decide whether that belief is a corrupted memory, a trap, or true.
- **Optional Forest-Fane payoff.** Choose one modest effect if Ivan participates: suppress one Forest-Fane ability Strahd would use, or grant the party one opening against his connection. Do not make his absence prevent reconsecration.
