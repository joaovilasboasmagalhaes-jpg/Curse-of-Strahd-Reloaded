# Answering

## What people in my clan do I know? What are their names? What do they do to survive?

Your clan is not a large one. It is a secluded community, usually between 50 and 150 members. Everyone knows everyone, and every person has a place within the clan.

The clan is led by a hierarchy. At the top is the leader, who has the final authority over decisions. The leader is chosen by the **Inner Circle**, a group of influential members trusted by the clan.

The Inner Circle itself is not inherited. There is no official process for joining it. Members are chosen because the clan believes they are powerful, wise, experienced, and have the clan’s interests in mind.

You know your father, **Old Skennis**, was the leader for decades. He was respected and feared, and his word carried great weight. He spent his life preparing you, not only because you were his oldest son, but because he believed you could one day carry the responsibility of leadership.

Your younger brother Kiril was always different from you. He was a natural hunter and, in many ways, more gifted than you physically. He was faster, more aggressive, and had better instincts in the wild. However, your father favored your discipline and patience over Kiril’s aggression, believing that leadership required more than strength.

The clan survives through hunting and careful isolation. Cubs are taught from a young age, and when they reach a certain point, they begin joining hunting parties. At first, these hunts are supervised by older members who teach them the ways of the forest. Eventually, they are allowed to hunt on their own.

Some hunting parties are given permission to travel further from the clan’s territory when necessary, usually to find food or resources.

You grew up in a cave system deep in the forest. The cave itself was mostly a home, with the clan living together inside. Outside the cave was a fenced camp area and several smaller outposts along the perimeter, used to watch for danger.

You know there was a deeper section of the cave where the Inner Circle worked and gathered, but you were never allowed inside.

## What do I know about the place I grew up?

You grew up in the Svalich Woods, south of the Village of Barovia.

Your clan has existed there for generations, hidden away from outsiders.

You were taught about the dangers of infected lycanthropes. They are different from your kind. They lose themselves to the beast and become controlled by instinct, making them dangerous even to those they once knew.

Because of this, your clan avoids them. You do not seek them out. You do not speak with them. You do not invite their attention.

Most outsiders do not understand the difference between different kinds of lycanthropy. To them, a werewolf is a werewolf.

---

## What do I know about Strahd?

Strahd is known as **the Ancient**.

He is the absolute ruler of Barovia. People fear him because he controls the land and everything within it.

Your clan has avoided his attention for generations. You were always taught that this was not because your clan was stronger than him, but because Strahd simply had no reason to care.

If he ever decided that your clan was a problem, there would be little they could do to stop him.

---

## What did my brother tell the clan about me? Did he tell me something to my face and later lie about it?

You do not know what Kiril told the clan.

The only thing you know is what he told you.

He threatened the clan and forced you to leave. He told you that if you ever returned or revealed the truth, he would follow through with his deal.

You asked him for one thing: the ability to leave behind a letter explaining your departure.

He agreed.

You believe the clan knows the reason you gave in that letter.

---

## Why can’t I shift into my lycan form?

Your body is not currently in the state it needs to be.

You have been surviving alone for days, barely eating enough to keep yourself alive. Your instincts are dulled, and your connection to your own nature feels distant.

Until you properly feed and recover your strength, you cannot fully access your abilities.

---

## Is my lycanthropy known or common? Should I avoid showing what I can do?

Lycanthropy is known in Barovia, but most people only know the stories about werewolves.

They do not understand the difference between inherited and infected lycanthropy.

Your kind is rare, especially with the level of control you have over your transformations.

You do not know how outsiders would react if they saw what you truly are. Some may fear you. Some may assume you are no different from the monsters they have heard stories about.

Because of this, you were taught not to reveal your nature carelessly.

And more importantly, you do not want to attract Strahd’s attention.
