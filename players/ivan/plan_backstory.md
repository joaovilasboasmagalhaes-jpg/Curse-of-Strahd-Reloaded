# Ivan Petrovich - DM Master Notes & Resolution

## 1. The Tale of Two Packs & The Secret Schism

* **Generations Ago:** Following Strahd's rise, the Barovian werewolves fractured into two distinct branches:
  * **The Northern Pack (Lake Baratok):** Remained in the north, accepted Strahd as their feudal master, worshipped Mother Night, and gained monstrous physical power by consuming human flesh. They enjoyed Strahd's special dispensation to hunt beyond the Mists.
  * **The Southern Pack / Clan Venatricho (Southern Svalich Woods):** Fled into total isolation south of the Village of Barovia. They rejected Strahd, rejected cannibalism, and preserved the ancient Wolfir covenant with the **Huntress** (Rozana of Summer).
* **The Kept Secret:** The existence of the Northern Pack was kept hidden from ordinary southern pack members to protect them from the temptation of savage power. Only the reigning Alpha (**Old Skennis**) knew, with historical records preserved in a hidden vault for the designated heir.

## 2. What Each Character Knows & Believes

### Ivan Petrovich (Player Character)
* **What he knows:** Grew up in Clan Venatricho under Skennis's strict discipline, believing their pack was the only inherited lycanthrope clan in Barovia. Taught to avoid infected werewolves and hate the undead.
* **What he believes:** Kiril was consumed by jealousy over Ivan being groomed for leadership. Kiril threatened to bring Strahd's wrath upon the clan unless Ivan went into exile. Ivan left behind a fake letter of shame to protect his family.
* **What he DOES NOT know:** The existence of the Northern Pack, the secret schism, Kiril's cannibalistic empowerment via Baba Lysaga, and the fact that Kiril marched the entire clan north to Lake Baratok.

### Old Skennis Petrovich (Father)
* **What he knows:** The entire secret history of the two packs, the fading of the Wolfir bloodline due to Strahd desecrating the Forest Fane, and the truth about Kiril's dangerous obsession.
* **His mistake:** Hid the truth from his sons until Kiril discovered it independently, creating the resentment that broke the family.
* **Current state:** Starving and blind in the Lake Baratok den ([Arc L - The Den of Wolves](../../Act%20III%20-%20The%20Broken%20Land/Arc%20L%20-%20The%20Den%20of%20Wolves.md#L4c-wolf-den)), punished by Kiril for withholding the secret.

### Kiril Petrovich / "Kiril Stoyanovich" (Middle Brother)
* **His discovery:** Captured a Northern werewolf during a hunt, learned about the Northern Pack's strength, and forced Skennis to reveal the truth.
* **His motive:** Believed the Southern clan had grown weak, soft, and defenseless. When Strahd woke up 3 months ago demanding fealty, Kiril concluded that only reunification, flesh-eating, and submission to Strahd could ensure their survival.
* **His pact:** Allied with Baba Lysaga and Strahd's brides, drank dark alchemical brews, devoured human flesh to mutate into an apex mutant lycan, blackmailed Ivan out of the picture, and forcibly merged both packs at Lake Baratok.

### Emil Petrovich / "Emil Toranescu" (Youngest Brother)
* **His stance:** Opposed Kiril's tyranny and submission to Strahd. 
* **Current state:** Imprisoned in Castle Ravenloft's dungeons by Strahd as a hostage/trophy delivered by Kiril.

## 3. Immediate Plot Resolution (Answers to Unanswered Questions)

1. **What happened to Skennis?**
   * Kiril deposed Skennis, slashed his face, and dragged him to the northern den at Lake Baratok, keeping him starved in the dirt as a broken captive.
2. **Why did Strahd take an interest in Kiril?**
   * Strahd needed a loyal, ruthless attack dog to control the werewolves upon his reawakening. Kiril willingly offered fealty and severed the werewolves' independence.
3. **Did Strahd approach Kiril, or did Kiril seek him out?**
   * Kiril sought out Strahd's power via Baba Lysaga in Berez. Lysaga facilitated the pact and the mutant transformation on Strahd's behalf.
4. **Why did Kiril exile Ivan instead of killing him?**
   * Kiril wanted to utterly break Ivan's honor and legitimacy. Forcing Ivan to write a letter admitting cowardice eliminated Ivan as a rival without sparking a civil war among traditionalist hunters.
5. **How did the Northern and Southern packs merge?**
   * Backed by Strahd's dire wolves and his own mutated strength, Kiril marched the Southern clan north, overthrew the existing Northern leadership (delivering Emil to Ravenloft and collaring Zuleika), and forged both factions into a single reign of terror under his command.
