# Game of Stories: Ivan's Non-Canon Retelling

> **Non-canon as backstory.** Ivan told this version in Session 9. The telling happened in play, but the events as told do not replace the [canonical Kiril memory](core_moments_memories.md#kiril).

The winter I turned eighteen, a brutal fever swept through my hometown. Even our harsh populos struggled against the mountain chill. My father was bedridden for the first time in my memory, leaving a burden on my shoulders in more ways than one. I hunted for food for days straight without sleep, refusing help because I believed I had to carry the weight alone.

On the fourth night, deep in the woods, a shadow crossed a large frozen lake. It was moving with the winter snow, radiating heat, drenched in blood splatters. And then it slipped into multiple small creatures. Exhausted from fatigue, I missed their approach. A massive grey shadow with fur lunged out of the ice itself, tearing into my left shoulder and pinning me into a frozen creek bed before I could even draw my blade.

I tried calling for help, but my words froze even before leaving my mouth. As the beast’s jaws snapped inches from my throat, a streak of silver fur slammed into its side with the force of an avalanche.

It was a wolf.

It danced around these creatures, snapping its jaws as it was creating shockwaves just the force of it’s, moving like lightning on the ice, drawing their attention away from me. Enough for me to drive my dagger into the beast's heart.
It just instantly vanished with the wind.

I looked at this wolf, my chest tight with exhaustion and confusion. It approached me, and just slapped my stunnedcold face with it’s tail, almost mockingly.

It wiped a smear of blood from it’s paw and let out that sharp, reckless cackle —almost like a hyina.

I limped while trying to follow it, trying to figure it out. And I never saw that wolf ever again.
