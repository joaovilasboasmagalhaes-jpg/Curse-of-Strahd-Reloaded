## 1. Core Identity & Mechanics

- **Name:** Ivan Petrovich
- Age: 29
- **Class/Subclass:** Blood Hunter (Alternate Order of the Pale Moon)
- **Role within Clan:** Eldest Son, Exiled Heir Apparent, Haunted One
- **The Concept:** A disciplined warrior who uses Blood Hunter that possesses an inherited lycanthropy currently suppressed by trauma, grief, and starvation. He aims to overcome his insecurities and make Strahd pay for what he did to his family, so he can finally return home.

## 2. Personality Traits

- **Personality Traits**
    - **Grimly Disciplined:** Ivan does not panic. His father’s brutal training taught him that fear is a choice, and a calm mind is a warrior’s greatest weapon.
    - **Viscerally Protective:** Though he tries to maintain an aloof, stoic exterior, his protective instincts are deeply hardwired.
    - **Religiously Pure:** Ivan views his bloodline as a sacred gift from the Huntress. He is not a feral monster; he is a holy predator. He possesses a deep, almost aristocratic pride in his true nature, which directly fuels his disgust for the "unclean" undead.
- **Key Phrases & Vocabulary:**
    - Refers to Strahd as **"The Parasite."**
    - Refers to his companions or those he protects as **"The Pack"** or **"My Kin."**
    - *“In my mountains, we have saying: The wolf that barks too loud is first to starve.”*
    - *“You smell of fear. Drop it. It does not help you here.”*
    - *“ Venatrix, grant me sight. Let my teeth find the throat.”*
- **Physical Mannerisms**
    - Though his shifting abilities are currently suppressed, his predatory instincts are active. When entering a new room or meeting someone new, he will subtly flare his nostrils, inhaling deeply to gauge their scent (fear, blood, sweat, or the chemical rot of the undead).
- **Personal Customs & Rituals**
    - **The Law of the Clean Kill**: Ivan will never leave an enemy or a beast to suffer. o him, this is not cruelty; it is the ultimate sign of respect to a fallen foe , ensuring they pass cleanly into the natural cycle.
    - **The Blood Rite (Before Battle)**: Right before a major conflict, as he prepares to activate his Blood Hunter crimson rites, Ivan performs a quick ancestral custom. He slices his palm or uses an enemy’s blood to draw a single, sharp vertical line down his forehead or across his eyes.
    - **Dealing with the Dead**: If a natural creature or a humanoid ally dies, Ivan will perform a brief ritual—placing dirt, leaves, or stones over their eyes to "shield them from the mists" so their spirit can find its way to the Whispering Wall. However, if he defeats an undead abomination, he will go out of his way to burn the remains or scatter them, ensuring the parasite's rot cannot corrupt the soil.

## 3. The Nature of Your Blood: Inherited Lycanthropy

Unlike the savage, cursed monsters feared across Barovia, Ivan's lycanthropy is a divine boon.

- **The Origin:** Granted generations ago via a pact with the Huntress (The Fane of the Mountain), a powerful entity who blessed Ivan's bloodline.
- **The Shift:** Transitions are nearly painless. Transformed states are age-appropriate (e.g., young adolescents shift into wolf pups).
- **Control:** Inherited lycanthropes retain their full personality, intellect, and mind at will.
- **The Exception (The Full Moon):** Forced into were-form. Control is temporarily lost to animalistic instincts, but Ivan remains non-violent toward loved ones and can recognize friends and family.
- **Infected vs. Inherited:** Ivan was taught to strictly avoid, ignore, and never speak to infected lycanthropes, who are slaves to the beast and inherently dangerous.
- **Current Status (Suppressed):** Due to days of starvation, isolation, and psychological trauma in the mists, Ivan's instincts are dulled. He cannot currently shift. He must recover his physical strength and connection to nature to reclaim his power.

## 4. The Venatricho Clan & The Svalich Woods

The Venatricho clan is a secluded, tightly knit community hidden from the eyes of Barovia.

- **Name**: Derived from *Venatrix* (The Huntress) and *Venatio* (The Wild Hunt), forming ***Venatricho***.
- **Size & Culture:** Consisting of between 50 and 150 members, it functions as a communal society where everyone has a specific role.
- **The Homestead:** The clan resides in a deep cave system south of the Village of Barovia. The layout features an outdoor fenced camp and several smaller outposts along the perimeter used to watch for danger. A mysterious "Inner Section" of the cave is reserved strictly for the work and gatherings of the Inner Circle.
- **Governance:** The clan is led by a single Alpha/Leader whose authority over decisions is final. However, this leader is chosen, trusted, and advised by the Inner Circle—a non-inherited meritocracy composed of the most powerful, wise, and experienced members who keep the clan's interests in mind.
- **Survival:** The community survives through hunting and careful isolation. Cubs are trained from a young age; older members supervise the youth in hunting parties until they are deemed ready for independent or long-distance hunting to secure food or resources.

## 6. The Betrayal & The Weight of Exile

- **The Deathbed Promise:** On the night Ivan's mother passed away, he and Emil were the only ones by her side. She made them promise: *"Ivan, protect your brothers. Your strength is a shield, not a weapon. Emil, guide your brothers... Do not let the mists divide you."* This final charge forged a sacred, maternal obligation for Ivan to protect his youngest brother.
- **The Pact & The Ultimatum:** Fueled by envy, Kiril made a dark alliance with Strahd von Zarovich. He confronted Ivan, threatening to unleash Strahd's wrath to slaughter the entire pack unless Ivan willingly surrendered his claim to leadership and left.
- **The Exile:** Fearing for the lives of his loved ones, Ivan chose to exile himself, pretending he was ashamed and unready to lead. He left behind a letter spinning this lie to spare the pack the bitter truth of the conflict.
- **The Heartbreak:** By leaving, Ivan intentionally let Emil believe he is a coward who abandoned him, their fading father, and their mother's dying wish. Ivan's driving motivation now is to find a way to look Emil in the eyes and show him that this exile was how he protected him. Furthermore, Ivan strongly suspects Kiril is being actively manipulated or influenced by Strahd.

## 7. Perspectives on the World

- **On Strahd (”The Parasite”, "The Ancient"):** To Ivan, Strahd is the absolute ruler of Barovia. The clan's historical strategy was total avoidance. He was taught that this avoidance was possible not because the clan was strong enough to oppose him, but because Strahd simply had no reason to care about them. Now, that delicate balance has shattered, as the vampire lord has actively touched Ivan's family.
- **On Outsiders:** Ivan understands that to the common Barovian, a werewolf is a werewolf. Most people do not know or comprehend the difference between inherited and infected lycanthropy. Revealing his true nature carelessly will invite severe fear, violence, or worse—Strahd’s direct and unwanted attention.
