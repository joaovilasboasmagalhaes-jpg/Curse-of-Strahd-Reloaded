# Kiril Petrovich

Relation: ANTAGONIST, BROTHER, FRIEND, MIDDLE-CHILD
Age: 26
Description: Physically superior, faster, more aggressive, and a natural hunter. As the middle child, he was caught between your heavy leadership training and your parents' protective love for the youngest, Emil. Kiril took your mother's death terribly, feeling completely unseen by your father. Without Marta to temper his anger, his deep-seated resentment toward your father's favoritism grew into a volatile, desperate hunger for power.
