# Skennis Petrovich

Relation: FATHER, FRIEND, LEADER
Age: 64
Description: The respected, feared leader of the clan for decades. Stoic, fiercely calculating, and deeply burdened by the weight of keeping the pack hidden from Strahd. He rarely smiled and never praised you directly. He drove Ivan ruthlessly because he saw him as the pack’s only viable shield against Kiril's volatile nature.
Image: https://i.pinimg.com/474x/44/a3/3c/44a33c472500b7f994ad758190fba26c.jpg

Beyond being a brutal martial instructor, Skennis was Ivan's spiritual guide into the ancient, dark history of the valley. In secret, late-night lectures away from the eyes of the pack, he passed down the forbidden truths of their bloodline.

He was the one who whispered to Ivan that Strahd is an apex parasite—a thief who did not create Barovia, but merely stole its power. Skennis taught Ivan that their inherited lycanthropy is not a curse, but the Wolfir Boon, a sacred fragment of a divine spark gifted generations ago by the Huntress (The Fane of the Mountain), an ancient Archfey of the summer hunt and natural death.

Skennis revealed the ultimate tragedy of their kind: that Strahd desecrated the Huntress's sacred stone circle atop Yester Hill with undead abominations to siphon her power over the land. He instilled in Ivan a visceral, biological hatred for the undead, showing him that their bloodline is actively fading because the vampire lord is choking their divine source. In the end, Ivan's father did not just prepare him to lead a pack—he prepared him to inherit a quiet war against a tyrant.
