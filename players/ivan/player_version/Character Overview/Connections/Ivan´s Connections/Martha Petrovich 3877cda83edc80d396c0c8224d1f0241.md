# Martha Petrovich

Relation: FRIEND, MOTHER
Age: 58
Description: The emotional anchor of the den. While Skennis was the stone wall protecting the clan, Marta was the fire keeping it warm. She was patient, deeply spiritual, and possessed an unbreakable mental fortitude. She tended your wounds after training and taught you that true control of the beast comes from a calm mind, not anger. Her passing a few years ago dissolved the glue holding the family together.
