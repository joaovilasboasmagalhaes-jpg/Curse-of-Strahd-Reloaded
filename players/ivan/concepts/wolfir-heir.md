# Concept: The Wolfir Heir

> [!info] Concept file
> Status: `exploring` (being developed), `chosen` (the DM decided; ready to integrate), `integrated` (applied to the campaign files), or `discarded` (kept for the record). Nothing here is campaign canon until it is integrated.

## Idea

Ivan begins as an exiled son following signs that his family has been drawn into the werewolf conflict. He gradually uncovers the Wolfir history his clan preserved imperfectly, reunites with his family, and decides whether to accept responsibility for what remains. The Forest Fane offers a later culmination: Ivan can contribute his inherited bond to the Huntress in an optional rite that strengthens the party's effort to undo Strahd's control, but the campaign's Fane quest remains completable without him.

Player profile, design notes, and existing concepts are not present in Ivan's folder. This direction reflects the DM's current goals, not an assumption about the player's preferences.

## Arc beats

1. **Hunted, Arc C:** Strahd notices Ivan; Eva and the werewolf clues point toward a den without revealing his family's fate.
2. **Signs of home, Acts II-III opening:** Lake Baratok traces, the full moon, Krezk's werewolf family, Yester Hill, and Kavan's history connect Ivan's experience to the valley's divided lycanthrope traditions.
3. **Kin, Arcs L-P:** Ivan finds Skennis and learns the clan was moved after his exile; he can rescue Emil and recover the Spear of the Huntress.
4. **Alpha, Arc Q:** Ivan confronts Kiril and chooses what to tell the southern clan. He may accept leadership as a responsibility, not a birthright he must obey.
5. **Heir of the Huntress, Arc T/Epilogue:** Ivan may voluntarily run a Hunt at the Forest Fane, letting the Huntress use his Wolfir bond as an additional channel against Strahd's control. The party can still reconsecrate the Fanes if Ivan is absent or declines.

## What it gives the player

Ivan's concrete want is to find his family, discover what happened after his exile, and stop Kiril from destroying the clan. The deeper question is whether protection means leaving others behind or choosing to stand with them and share the burden.

The player can decide how to respond to Kiril, whether to forgive or condemn him, what to say to Emil and the clan, and whether to take part in the Forest Fane's optional Hunt. Potential growth is from reluctant heir to a person who chooses what leadership means, rather than simply inheriting it. The Fane scene can give the Huntress and Wolfir lore a personal payoff without making Ivan the key that unlocks the campaign's main ritual.

**Provisional emphasis options:**

- **Family-first:** Keep Kiril, Skennis, and Emil as the main resolution; treat the Fane as a brief epilogue-scale recognition. Strongest intimate focus; risks leaving Ivan's religious identity and the Huntress lore mostly decorative.
- **Fane culmination (current DM direction):** Let the family story resolve in Arc Q, then carry its meaning into Ivan's optional contribution at Arc T. Strong campaign connection while preserving party agency; risks giving Ivan too much weight in the group Fane ritual if the optional contribution is not kept modest.
- **Mercy-and-cure:** Center Ivan's choices about Doru, Ilya, infected werewolves, and the surviving pack, with leadership and the Fane as consequences. Distinct moral focus; risks overlapping with Agni's cure story and Caspian's werewolf targets unless the questions and outcomes stay clearly different.

The Fane emphasis is recommended provisionally because it follows the DM's expressed goal. It is not a recommendation based on unrecorded player preferences.

## How it differs from the other players

- **Caspian:** Both arcs meet at the werewolf den, but Caspian seeks justice for Ashcairn and the captured children, especially against Ravka. Ivan's conflict is with his family, his exile, and what leadership means. Keep Ravka's reckoning from replacing Ivan's Kiril scene.
- **Agni:** Both arcs involve a loved one affected by vampirism. Agni's focus is a cure for Milosh and his own hunger; Ivan's is family separation, the Wolfir legacy, and the Huntress's Fane.
- **Grim:** Both are parents seeking a child. Grim's focus is her bargain, the heart-pouch, and Milo's agency; Ivan's is the clan he left, his brothers, and a communal legacy.
- **Sister Elga:** Both have religious traditions and the dead. Elga shepherds souls and re-examines doctrine; Ivan's customs concern the hunt, natural death, and choosing whether to inherit a damaged faith.
