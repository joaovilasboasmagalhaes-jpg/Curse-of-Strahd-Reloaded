The party heads to the church carrying the corpse of the late burgomaster. They see the bodies in the street. At the town square, Sister Elga’s urn lights up revealing a near undead. She explains this to Ismark, who states it might be a straggler left behind near the Svalich Woods and they continue.

They arrive at the church finding Parriwimple sitting on the steps outside the building. They have a conversation about Bildrath’s condition, Kolyan and Doru. Parriwimple invites the players to pass by the shop to give them something.

Inside the church they find Father Donavich asleep at the altar. He explains he’d been praying all night due to the vampire spawn’s aggressive behavior. From there they learn about Doru being a vampire spawn and trapped in the Undercroft. They convince Donavich to come down and talk with him. They also have a discussion where Ivan reveals he has no intention of permitting Doru to live. Sister Elga remains on the 1st floor of the Church with Ismark and Ireena thinking she wouldn't be able to permit Doru to leave while maintaining her core religious beliefs so she prefers to avoid the situation.

The party talk with Doru and learn what he has to share about vampires, his trip and van Richten. Agni recognizes the name, and Ivan and Sister Elga know him to be a renowned author and monster hunter. The party performs the test with Agni first. Here, Doru reveals his blood to be different and less appealing. Grim, in an attempt to bandage Agni’s hand touches his bare skin and feels the touch of death he carries. She recoils for a second, then recomposes and offers her own blood for the test. This time, Doru feels the intensity of his hunger, but resists the temptation, and since the party was encouraging from the beginning, Doru passes without a chance to fail. He and Donavich share a hug in tears and the priest promises to make his accommodations more comfortable, mentioning he can try animal blood as a replacement for humans.

Before leaving, Ivan threatens to kill Doru should he ever hear word of any incident in this town and sets all responsibility of future events on Father Donavich. He turns to the party with a declaration: “This is the last time you make me do something like this“. After Ivan leaves, Grim asks Doru if he knows anything of people without hearts (in relation to her son) and he only mentions vaguely the heart of Strahd at Castle Ravenloft. 

Donavich then finds some sheets and furniture for the undercroft and proceeds with the burial. He also asks Sister Elga to examine his holy sigil to which she replies it to be safe and uncorrupted. Donavich says a prayer and Ismark and Ireena say their farewell. The party notices the swarms of bats, and Donavich recommends the party to try to head to the abbey of St Markovia in Kresk.

The party heads to Bildrath’s Mercantile, while Ismark and Ireena leave to the manor. Parriwimple gifts them each a waterskin and Grim a daisy. They haggle with an agate moss and a religious symbol. The rest was shopping undefined.

Back at the manor again, Ismark shares a copy of the Record of Lost and Missing with Sister Elga. They also see the scouts from their entrance to the town and know they are searching for Gertruda and the Langsten family. The party prepares to leave, then fo fetch Ireena from the knoll.

Ireena gifts them her silver bracelet and reveals she knows nothing about it. They don't ask about the other items. Going back, they say their farewells from Ismark and leave Barovia.

After crossing the bridge they meet the scouts shortly and leave them to find Van Richten’s cache. In the process they find the zombified Langsten family who simply stare at them and shield their young when they come close. Sister Elga performs a ritual to save their souls, and their original form is restored. Their souls float into the urn and Elga learns about them.

Coming out of the woods they reach a crossroads. Then Ivan gets the sense they are being watched. Strahd’s carriage arrives coming from the Vistani camp. Ireena is shocked to see Escher as the driver. Strahd comes out and greets them:

“Good day. I am Count Strahd von Zarovich. It is a pleasure to finally make your acquaintance. “
