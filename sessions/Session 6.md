The party and Ismark fight the remaining undead. Ismark almost falls, but Sister Elga heals him. They kill one of the zombies and the wight and turn the other zombie. 

Rahadin appears and the dead villagers rise. He gives his proclamation and congratulates Ismark. He then turns to the party, sniffing the air when looking at Ivan and Agni. Finally he talks to Elga about her jar as a priest of the Ven. Grim asks him about the pouch with his son’s heart but he knows nothing of this.

Ismark and the party head to the mansion to find Kolyan dead and Ireena unconscious. The scene unravels, with them helping to clean the body and talking about Ireena’s possible transformation. Sister Elga is prepared to kill her if she turns into a vampire. They find the torn page from Van Richten’s book and become less alert to Ireena turning, since the conditions haven’t been met, but they don’t dismiss the possibility.

Grim talks with Caspian about what they just faced and asks him if he’s set on facing Strahd.

They sleep and wake up to Ireena and Ismark arguing. Sister Elga wants to investigate but the other stop her out of respect. They spot the swarms of bats spying through the windows and head to Ismark to warn him. Ismark then speaks with them and finds each of their goals when he asks them to take Ireena to Vallaki.  

The players then convince Ireena to leave with them to Vallaki and sleep the rest of the night. Ivan stays watching a bit more until the bats disperce. 

In the morning before dawn they eat breakfast and Ismark mentions Madam Eva as an advisor to learn more about Strahd. They spot a rat who is one of Strahd’s spies and kill it, though Ivan just wanted to eat it. 

The party prepares to carry the body to the church.
