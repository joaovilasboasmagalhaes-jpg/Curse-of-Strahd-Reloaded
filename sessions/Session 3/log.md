The party starts the session heading down to the lower level of the dungeon in Death House. Party: Agni, Caspian, Grim, Ivan, Sister Elga.

They step into the reliquary hearing the words stronger than ever. They analyze the items and pick them up looking for something to get triggered, but nothing happens. They explore the prison and immediately kill the skeleton shackled to the wall. Ivan tried to burn it, but didn’t succeed. They also took a 25GP gold ring from him. Sister Elga collects its soul, it learns its name was Tydon, its alignement was chaotic neutral and the memory of sacrificing the baby and being captured and left there. She also rolled a 20 on the soul capturing and rolled on the Liturgy of Grace table, getting a 2, which granted her advantage on the next interaction with an undead.

Ivan saw the monstrosity again and scared it away, then Grimm examined the entire prison looking for a secret. After not finding anything else, the party lifted the portcullis gate and went inside the ritual chamber. The chanting stopped and they saw the flesh mound as well as the altar. 2 of them stepped into the dais (Ivan and Sister Elga). Stepping out, Sister Elga heard a voice in her head “One Must Die” but resisted it. She then warned Ivan of this. The party discussed wether to do the ritual and fead the monster, or to kill it. 

People in favor of doing the ritual: Grim.

Grim saw the ritual as the way out. The party had already explored the entire place and killing the monster immediately could lead to nowhere, basically “soft-locking” the party. So trying the ritual seemed like a good idea. Later when they decided to kill the creature, she tried to kill it on the altar, hoping it would take an effect towards them leaving the house. She ultimately gave up on this attempt when the situation got dire and it wasn’t realistic anymore. She is not a bad person, but if she has to sacrifice a “random” baby to escape and continue her mission of retrieving her son, she will do it.

People opposing the ritual: Sister Elga, Ivan, Agni.

Sister Elga saw it as part of the unholy rituals that took place in the house. It had to be cleansed, its soul had to be saved. 

Ivan saw it as an abnormality, something unnatural that does not belong in this world. He has a sense of duty to destroy it and any other creature like it.

Agni saw himself in the creature, something innocent turned into a monster against his will.

People without an opinion on the matter: Caspian

Finally, Caspian didn’t have an opinion, he would do whatever the party chose to do. He was only interested in getting out and didn’t care how they achieved it.

Sister Elga contacted the spirit of the nursemaid to guide her or ask her who Walter was and got a feeling that he was the baby in the flesh mound. The party fought the monster, Grimm thought that killing it on the altar could have a special effect (it didn’t) but gave up when it wasn’t realistic anymore and the situation was dire. Midway through the fight, the chamber begins to flood and the house begins to crumble. The party manages to kill the creature and leave the house. Sister Elga collected its soul only learning its alignment - Neutral.

The house crumbles to the ground creating an ever deepening hole in the ground as the party runs outside. The mists have receded. They follow a flickering light through the road to a broken tower (Will’o’wisp encounter) and find it empty and abandoned. They make a long rest there, with Caspian and Grimm doing half the watches (they only sleep 4 hours). Throughout the long rest, a swarm of bats passes by them and later at the end of the rest, Ivan sees a wolf patrolling the hole made by the house. The party dismissed this, but unknowingly to them, these are Strahd Spies’ . Caspian told Grimm more details about the werewolves that attacked his village and expressed his hatred for Strahd.
