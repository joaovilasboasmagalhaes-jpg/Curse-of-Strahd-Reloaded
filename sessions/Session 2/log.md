party: agni, caspian, grim, ivan, sister elga.

the party is in the death house, in the attic. they just finished talking with the spirits of the children.

sister elga had taken the souls of the children Rose and Thorn into her urn. she promised them to reunite them with their family. ivan gathered their remains from the attic room where they died, to bury them in their rightful place.

the party took a short rest while ivan and caspian stayed watching. ivan heard skittering on the walls going down through the levels of the house. he broke a piece of wall and investigated inside, spotting the tail of a rat running away from the hole. caspian noticed a shadow moving across the window as all the windows of the house darkened. the other players saw the windows darkening too. 

After a short rest, they went down the secret staircase that lead to the dungeon. the found all the crypts and hypothesized about the empty one. since there was a crypt for walter and an empty one, they hypothesized about the stillborn baby not being walter. after finding the cripts for rose and thorn, sister elga was hesitant about opening them. ivan convinced her by saying that this is the rightful place for them to rest. ivan places their remains in the coffins and the souls tug at the urn to leave. sister elga allows them to leave and they show up one last time before disappearing and being at rest.

the party explores the dungeon starting on the cult initates quarters and then the members quarters. They ponder on looting the chests, but decide to do it. sister elga and agni investigate the well. They manage to avoid the corridor, but in the dining room, Ivan triggers the grick. He runs away and the party lets it do so.

They head to the darklord shrine, where Caspian takes the orb and they trigger the shadows and ghouls to come alive. Sister Elga turns half of the creatures with Turn Undead and they easily wipe them out one by one. Sister Elga also captures some of their souls with her urn and gains memories from 3 of them:

- Hugh Jardon, evil, remembers tripping one night on the corridor trap (this enables the party to locate the right corridor and they avoid it, thought they don’t know where it is in the corridor)
- Hughie, evil, remembers the master of the cult disappearing and not returning and praying for their return in their shrine until they died
- Pard-on, evil, remembers having to fix the hidden wall in the shrine after having broken it (this allows sister elga to locate the hidden wall that leads to the “Wolf Den” room

The party then tries to open the mimic door, with Ivan being the lead. They get ambushed by the mimic and take some damage, but kill it as well. They find the dinner letter on the table outside the cult master’s chamber and figure out together that barovia is in a different dimension in space and time. The tavern where some of the players met was also supernatural as it isn’t recorded in any of their maps. They hypothesize that the vistani might be aligned with strahd.

They enter the master bedroom and see the chest full of valuables. They take very big precautions opening it, Grim using her shadow to take an item from behind a barricade. When they confirmed that the chest wasn’t trapped they took the items. They also found the Cult Victims journal and the Adventurer Journal and were able to link the two together, confirming their space-time-dimension theory. 

With nothing else to explore, the party ready themselves and go down the staircase towards the chanting sound. As they come down they distinctly hear the chant “He is the Ancient, He is the Land”.
