---

## Section 1 — Safety & Boundaries

### Content Boundaries

The table is open to exploring all themes as long as they are handled seriously and remain relevant to the story. 

### Sensitive Themes (Off-screen or limited detail)

Certain topics should not be described in detail, such as **sexual violence**. These will be handled indirectly or faded to black when relevant.

### Player Conflict (PvP)

PvP is only allowed if all involved players agree in-character beforehand.

---

## Section 2 — Tone of the Campaign

### Horror & Terror

The campaign includes both horror and terror elements.

### Overall Tone

The game is primarily serious, with dedicated moments for downtime and lighter interaction.

---

## Section 3 — Difficulty & Survival

### Combat Difficulty

Most encounters will be challenging. Easy fights will be few, and high-risk encounters (including potential TPKs) are expected.

### Enemy Intelligence

Enemies generally behave like trained soldiers: they will retreat when needed and use basic tactical thinking.

### Resources & Scarcity

Healing and supplies are moderately scarce. They are not extremely rare, but also not freely available in every location.

### Magic Items

Magic items appear slightly more frequently than a strict low-magic setting, but are still meaningful and not common.

### Resurrection Magic

Resurrection is rare and only appears in situations where it makes sense narratively.

---

## Section 4 — Character Rules

### Ability Scores

Stats are rolled using 4d6, drop the lowest die.

### Hit Points

Hit points are rolled on level up. Any roll of 1 is rerolled once.

### Starting Level

All characters begin at level 1.

---

## Section 5 — Combat Rules

### Flanking

Flanking grants a +2 bonus to attack rolls.

### Healing Potions

Drinking a healing potion takes an action for full healing. As a bonus action, you may drink it and roll for healing instead.

### Death Saves

Death saving throws are always rolled in secret.

### Turn Timing

Each turn has a soft limit of 2 minutes, with warnings if needed.

### Critical Hits

Double Dice.

### Conditions

Standard D&D conditions are used (incapacitated, stunned, and paralyzed).

### Talking in Combat

Not enforced, but keep it brief and strategic where possible.

---

## Section 6 — Gameplay Style

### Focus

The campaign is balanced between combat and roleplay.

### Structure

The game leans slightly toward a guided story, with some player freedom for exploration and decisions.
