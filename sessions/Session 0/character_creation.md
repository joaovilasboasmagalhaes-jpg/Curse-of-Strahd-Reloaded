# Race & Lineage

Not all who arrive in Barovia are treated the same.

Those who resemble humans move through the land with less immediate attention. Those who do not—those with strange forms, inhuman features, or unnatural presence—tend to be remembered. Watched. Avoided.

Fear here is quiet, but constant.

Gothic lineages such as **Dhampir, Hexblood, or Reborn** are permitted, so long as they make sense for who you are.

# Background

Some histories are still carried when the Mists take you.

**Permitted additional backgrounds:** Haunted One, Investigator.

The usual marks of your past may be replaced. If another truth better defines what you are now, you may exchange your standard background feature for one from the permitted list.

# Classes

The Mists do not care what disciplines you follow.

You may choose from any 2014 5e class, as well as *LaserLlama* alternate classes, if approved.

Two paths have already been observed within Barovia and are known to respond well to its influence:

- Bard: **College of Spirits**
- Warlock: **The Undead**

---

# Equipment

You arrive with what your class provides. Nothing more is guaranteed.

Additional items may be carried for personal meaning or identity, but they hold no promise of advantage.

You also begin with **one common magic item**. Even these small enchantments rarely remain ordinary once exposed to the Mists.

Homebrew items are allowed, but must be anchored to an existing common magic item of similar nature.

You may also take one item from the Horror Trinkets table.

# Dark Gifts

Some who come to Barovia do not leave unchanged.

The Mists are not merely a barrier. They watch. They linger. And sometimes, they *offer.*

You may choose to begin your journey already marked by one of these “gifts” — blessings granted by forces beyond mortal understanding. The people of Barovia do not call them blessings for long.

These **Dark Gifts** are drawn from forbidden manifestations catalogued by scholars of the Mists (see *Van Richten’s Guide to Ravenloft*). Each one grants strange power… and something else in return.

Those who accept such an offering may find:

- Strength that does not feel entirely their own
- Senses that notice things they wish they had not seen
- Changes to body, mind, or soul that cannot be undone
- Consequences that do not always reveal themselves immediately

This choice is optional. But in Barovia, few things are ever truly optional once the Mists take interest.

If chosen, select one Dark Gift from the list provided by the DM.
