Caspian explains to the party how much he hates Strahd. 

The party got an idea of the terrain before the mists occluded the valley from sight. Ivan also gave a short mention of the settlements in Barovia.

The party notices the chasm that had formed in place of the house has now disappeared and the forest seems to have gotten closer to the tower they slept in.

The party heads to the village of Barovia. Here they see the village preparing for siege. Ismark introduces himself and lets them inside leading them to the tavern. 

Here, he explain the situation the village is in at the time. Bildrath is also there and explains his side of the story. The party disagrees with Bildrath’s atitude and antagonize him, especially Ivan, who at one point threatens him. 

Ivan tries to de-escalate the situation and Mary comes in pleading for help regarding her runaway daughter who left this morning to Vallaki. Ismark comforts her and prepares a search party to find her. Bildrath insults him and dismisses the situation claiming “the girl is dead already” and the party antagonizes him once more. Ismark then ushers the party outside before the situation escalates further. 

Outside, Sister Elga and Grimm offer to help the sick and injured around the town square while Caspian, Ivan and Agni head to the burgomaster’s mansion as instructed by Ismark. 

Sister Elga assist in the making of remedies and oils using their proficiency with herbalism kit. They make a good job and gain the village’s favor in future Charisma checks. Grimm helps in a more practical and personal approach talking with people and finding information about recent events. They find out about the stranger that arrived at the village days before the rebellion. They know this stranger had a pet monkey. 

At the same time, Caspian, Ivan and Agni find an angry mob in front of the burgomaster’s mansion. The mob wants to sacrifice Ireena to Strahd on account of her red hair. The party stands in between them and tries to convince the mob to come to reason. However, they merely appeal to logic and not emotion and are therefore unsuccessful leading to a brawl. The mob gets a few hits in the fight, and Agni pushes a villager to the ground and intimidates them with his physical prowess. The mob gets disheartened and ceases their efforts. 

Kolyan and Ireena invite the party inside and talk with them to get to know their current situation, explain recent events and ask their opinion on a few aspects. The rest of the party arrives. Kolyan gives the party a scroll to aid them in their journey. Ireena shows them to their room. The party barely goes in before heading out once again. 

Hearing about the village’s priest and his son’s folly, the party heads to the Church. There, they speak with the priest to convince him to hold onto hope and assist the village in the coming siege. Sister Elga manages to move him a little thanks to her selfishness and appeal to faith and emotion. Grimm, however, dissuades him once more by hypothesising that his god might be fake and Strahd might be toying with him. 

The party prepares to leave as Agni uses his Blood Sense ability to sense all living creatures in the vicinity. This only confirms the presence of all creatures that were already acknowledged. At the same time, Sister Elga notices the glow of her urn indicating the presence of undead nearby. As this happens, the siege begins and the party heads to their position, the eastern barricade. Here, they spot bildrath and a bigger man waiting for them.
