# Strahd

Before Strahd can see him, Ivan uses his magic item to change his appearance, mainly his face. When presenting himself he uses a false name: Boris.

Strahd speaks to the players. They seem cautious. No-one lashes out. Sister Elga seems to be more confrontational than the others. Even Caspian was cautious.

Strahd says a few personal lines to the players who spoke to him first. Sister Elga is direct in questioning and Strahd exclaims her master was also bold. He asks Agni if he’s been feeling urges. He asks Caspian why he has formed a bad opinion of him and Caspian says he doesn’t really know him, so Strahd comes back by saying this is an oportunity for them to form a good relationship.

He also speaks fondly to Ireena and explains that he had to punish the village of Barovia for their treachury. The players asked about the innocent and he said if they resisted their lord, they were not innocent. The players asked about the ones who ran form the village and got killed by the creatures in the woods and Strahd explains he left them to their own fate, not stopping or siccing the creatures.

Strahd explains he came from a visit to madam Eva and learned about them (he only learned they existed from his fate, not any specific details about the party. those details he learned form his spies. the party got suspicious of madam eva after this).

They ask how he knows so much about them and he says “It is a poor lord who does not know what happens in his lands”.

Strahd’s questions:

- Strahd asks why he shouldn’t take ireena now. They argue if that’s the kind of man he is (i think caspian and sister elga said that). He explains he is the kind of man to get what he wants. He deemed theirs a poor answer.
- Why he shouldn’t punish them for death house. He used it to attract adventurers and as a trial for them. The party (agni) says they beat the trial better than all others. good answer
- why he should allow outsiders to leave, when the previous one was treachorous against him (he is speaking of van richten but the party didn’t make the connection, however some of the players took note of this). Sister elga said because it’s more fun to let them roam. very good answer.

Ivan introduced himself as Boris and changed his face. The party lied for him and said Ivan was a barovian townsman. Strahd confirmed with them this was their truth before accepting it. He said he would investigate further (Strahd knew someone called ivan was with the party but not what he looked like, Strahd most likely assumes they lied, but might entertain this lie for a bit).

He then leaves southwest of the crossroads.

The party is surprised about the information Strahd knew about them (they had seed the spies in previous sessions but most likely still haven’t made the association for all of them, only some that were obvious). They mostly blamed madam Eva for the info Strahd had, though this was an assumption.

Ivan was the Hanged One with his real face. I chose ivan because he openly lied to Strahd, but otherwise it would’ve been Sister Elga since she was the most confrontational. Grim was also very direct in her questioning, so she would be considered 3rd.

# Madam Eva

Upon arriving they try to find the Vistani who brought them in but don’t see their caravan or the people. Stanimir welcomes them and has a friendly chat. He invites them to madam Eva’s tent saying she’s been awaiting them, and then to the feast. Madam Eva asks the party what their shared objective is but they can’t answer immediately. Instead they each get their signs read by her one at a time and alone. The order was: Caspian, Sister Elga, Agni, Ivan, Grim. The players had a hard time memorizing it so I told them I would send them the signs in writing after the session.

Regarding the questions each one made:

- Caspian - the player had trouble following the sign so we agreed to leave the question for later after he had a chance to read it in his own time

- Sister Elga - asked who the black figure was who invaded her cloister. I didn’t have an answer prepared for that so I improvised with these two:
    > *“I saw its shape, but not its face;
    > the glass revealed no root or place.”
    > 
    > “Barovia keeps what it takes
    > close to its heart.”*
    Which didn’t make a lot of sense, we might correct this with the player between sessions
    
- Agni - I think he asked about the cure and I said this:
    > *“Three locks bind one door; their forms are clear;
    > I show them, but cannot turn them here.
    > His crystallized heart-blood, sealed in glass, is the first key;
    > claim Vasili’s vial, not his life, and set the bloodline free.
    > Beneath the mountain, amber guards its ember deep;
    > the second key lies sealed within, still fast asleep.
    > When the valley’s lord is slain, the blood-chain breaks apart;
    > his passing frees the root that holds those dear to your heart.
    > I show where all three keys lie;
    > the hand that turns them must be thine.”*    
    Notice that I changed the mention of Osric to Vasili in this text from the original. This was a mistake in the original reading, since Agni’s only way of knowing Osric’s name was through the one question and he spent it on this other question so we would have to use his fake name in this answer
    
- Ivan - he asked if Strahd was responsible for what happened to him. I said i already pointed him to the one to blame and then read this:
    > *“She lent him strength; he swore the oath;
    > one lord now holds the branches both.”*

- Grim - she asked where the drowned city was and I said this:
    > *“The drowned roofs lie southeast, beneath the grey;
    > where old river-waters took fields away.
    > Seek the river-crossing; turn south from its stones;
    > the hut above floodwater crowns the drowned homes.”*
    Grim got the idea that Strahd was not responsible for the kidnapping of her son and payed attention to the mention of hags. She then expressed how desperate she was to get him and that she would even consider asking him for help since he is the most powerful person in the land. She also considered giving him Ireena if she had to do it. Grim then realized she was having very dark thoughts and Madam Eva reminded her of the dark deal she made, and that it might have unforeseen consequences. Grim is opening a new door for DM plans with these new attitudes.
    

After Grim left, the party discussed what their shared goal was outside the tent and alone. Each of them shared their own reasons for being in Barovia and a bit of their backstories:

- Agni - he told the story of a vampire infecting him and his son and he followed him here through a ritual and now he wants a cure. Through the conversation he suspects it wasn’t Strahd himself who he saw
- Caspian - he revealed his village was attacked by werewolves and the attackers spoke of the devil Strahd so he wants revenge. The player forgot to mention the kidnapped children.
- Grim - she talked about the shadow who killed her husband and took her son and about the deal (i didn’t notice if she separated the two, but probably not). She also revealed the bag with the heart (which incidentally is stained from the flesh, which intrigued Elga since her sign talked about a bag and of stains)
- Ivan - he told the party that Strahd threatened his entire family if Ivan didn’t leave his clan. He also believes Strahd has an ability to manipulate or influence people’s minds (Ivan believes his brother cast him out because of this power of Strahd, but this part he didn't explain to the party). Ivan never told them he and his clan are wolfir.
- Sister Elga - she explained how she is a priest of the Ven and what they defend. She talked about the monastery and about stepping on a rune. They also had an extensive conversation about her order and the exceptions to getting rid of undeath, and situations like Doru or Agni.

Finally they came back into Madam Eva’s tent and she received the raven with a familiar smile and talked about her old pet raven. Then she told them she would prepare for their shared reading and would meet them in the river Ivlis crossroads at midnight. Before the party left, madam Eva warned them of a servant of Strahd approaching.

They met Eliza and Arturi. Arturi asked them about Barovia and if they met Dr Rudolph Van Richten there. The party said no. They then all went to sit by the fire and the session ended.

After the session ended, I gave inspiration to Caspian and Ivan for their roleplay. Ivan was scared of Strahd and played it well, showing genuine fear and concern for his family's safety. Caspian was able to hold his composure and act with determination despite the hatred and anger he felt towards Strahd.
