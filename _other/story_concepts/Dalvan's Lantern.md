# Concept: Dalvan's Lantern

> [!info] Concept file
> Status: `exploring` (being developed), `chosen` (the DM decided; ready to integrate), `integrated` (applied to campaign files), or `discarded` (kept for the record). Nothing here is campaign canon until it is integrated.

## Idea

Dalvan Olensky was a Barovian native who died years before Strahd's recent rebellion. He desperately sought a way out of the valley, consulted Madam Eva, and heard her warning that another attempt to leave would kill him. He tried anyway, for reasons still to be developed, and died in the Mists. Afterward, Dalvan found a new purpose: at the Seeker's direction, he became a lantern-bearing guide who protects travelers from being lost to the Mists. He cannot make the Mists safe or compel anyone to follow; he can warn, guide, and offer a choice.

## Story beats

1. **Setup:** Years before the current campaign, Dalvan makes one or more attempts to leave Barovia. He survives an early attempt and seeks Madam Eva because he believes she may know a way through, not because he is idly asking for a fortune.
2. **Escalation:** Eva reads the Horseman and warns that another attempt will kill him. Dalvan continues looking for alternatives. His reason for leaving, the stakes behind it, and the time between the reading and his final attempt remain open.
3. **Choice:** Dalvan knowingly attempts to leave again. He dies after repeated exposure to the Mists. The prophecy informs his choice but does not cause it or remove his agency.
4. **Payoff and resolution:** After death, Dalvan accepts a purpose as a guide to travelers in danger of the Mists. The skeletal rider appears at the River Ivlis Crossroads after the party's shared reading, on their existing route, and later guides them to Katarina's locket in Arc O. When the Mists dissipate, Dalvan is finally released.

**Player-facing introduction:** If the party stays for the Game of Stories at Tser Pool, a Vistana tells a ghost story about a lantern-bearing rider who appears near the Mists. Some travelers say he leads the lost back toward safety; others call him a death-omen or a lure. The teller presents it as a tale heard on the road, not verified history, and does not name Dalvan. The skeletal rider's later appearance at the crossroads may remind the party of the story, but the connection is theirs to make. Keep this brief and within the existing campfire scene; do not delay the midnight reading or add a detour.

## Campaign connections

- **Established:** The current party is at Tser Pool on Neyavr 1, 735 B.C., before Madam Eva's shared reading. Their first logged encounter with Dalvan has not occurred. Arc C already places the skeletal rider at the River Ivlis Crossroads after the reading. Arc O uses the rider to guide the party to Katarina's locket, and the epilogue says Dalvan's spirit finds peace when the Mists dissipate.
- **Proposed:** Dalvan died years before the current rebellion, so he is outside the period and scope of Ismark's Record of the Lost and Missing. The record remains unchanged. His origin may be the Village of Barovia, Vallaki, or another Barovian settlement; decide this after choosing his reason to leave.
- **Proposed:** His role as a protector is a development of the Seeker's existing relationship to the rider and the later guidance in Arc O. Define whether he serves at Madam Eva's request, by his own choice, or through both.
- **Scene purpose:** His first appearance is not a tutorial about the Mists. It is an omen and possible offer of guidance after the party receives its own reading. The scene should stay on the party's route and preserve their choice to follow, question, or ignore him.
- **Proposed campfire seed:** The rider's story can be told by a Vistana during the existing Game of Stories at Tser Pool. A Vistani source may lend the tale lived authority because Vistani travel through the Mists, but the party's recent experience with an abandoning Vistani guide may also make them doubt it. Treat this as a rumor, not proof, and leave the storyteller's source open.

## Player choices and spotlight

The party may notice that the rider's lantern and bearing seem to offer guidance, but need not follow him or accept his help. If they engage, they can decide what to ask and whether his warning about the Mists merits trust. His story can invite reflection on the difference between receiving a warning and surrendering one's agency, without telling the characters how to interpret their own readings.

## Open questions

- Why was Dalvan so desperate to leave? What concrete stake made him risk his life even after Eva's warning?
- Where was he born and raised: the Village of Barovia, Vallaki, or elsewhere?
- What did his first escape attempt look like, and how did he survive it?
- How long did he search for other options between Eva's reading and his fatal attempt?
- What did Eva actually say? How direct was her warning, and what did she hope he would choose?
- How did Dalvan become the Seeker's servant, and was accepting that role his choice?
- What does protecting travelers from the Mists mean in practice? Who can call him, what danger can he perceive, and what can he do besides warn or guide?
- Who tells the campfire story: Stanimir, another established Vistana, or an unnamed traveler? What is their source, and do they believe the tale?
- What details make the story memorable without revealing Dalvan's identity or giving the party instructions about crossing the Mists?
- What specific clue in his first appearance tells the party he may be offering help, without requiring a detour or disclosing his full history?
- How should his service end when the Mists dissipate, in keeping with the epilogue?

## Decision

The DM is workshopping the premise. No option has been chosen or rejected. The motive for Dalvan's escape remains intentionally unresolved; develop it before integration.
