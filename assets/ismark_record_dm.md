# Record of the Lost and Missing — DM Reference

This guide explains the entries in [[ismark_record]] and identifies what each keeper could know, when that information became available, and where the campaign establishes it. The player-facing entries currently extend through the morning after Night 7, but the register remains open: neither keeper can know when the last person will die, disappear, or be found. Later revelations are noted separately and are not facts for the in-world record unless explicitly stated.

## Timeline and Authorship

- Night 1 is the first dusk-to-dawn siege night. Night 7 is the night the characters arrive and defend the village; Strahd withdraws his forces after Kolyan's death. The current entries continue through the following morning, not to a closure date for the register.
- The record begins on Night 1 with events that occurred before the siege. The first refugee departure and Doru's march both occurred roughly three months earlier, but they were separate events.
- Doru's march preceded Rahadin's proclamation. Rahadin's ninety-day warning preceded the siege by ninety days. Arc B places Van Richten's arrival sixteen weeks before the present and the march roughly three months before the present, implying an arrival about a month before the march. History of Barovia instead places the march about two weeks and two days after Van Richten's arrival. Keep the arrival at roughly sixteen weeks before the present as Arc B's stated date, but treat its interval to the march as an unresolved source discrepancy.
- Kolyan is the principal keeper through his death on Night 7. Ismark supplies and writes entries for events he handles, including searches, and continues the ledger after Kolyan's death. For each entry below, “learned” is when the keeper received or observed the information; “written” is when the entry could be added. Where the text gives no exact writing time, a bounded window is supplied.
- The player-facing record should contain only what its keeper knew by the time the entry was written. DM-only truth is labeled and should not be copied into the register.

## Entry Notes

The entries below follow their order in the player-facing register. The order is not always the order of the underlying events.

### 1. Doru (young adult)

**Register data:** Missing; presumed dead. Last seen departing in daylight for Castle Ravenloft about three months before Night 1. Led about two dozen villagers against the castle; no return, recovered bodies, or word.

**Author and knowledge:** Kolyan. He witnessed the public departure. The company's failure to return and the absence of news are established by the time the register begins. The campaign does not specify when Kolyan first concluded Doru was presumed dead.

**Timeline:** Departure: approximately three months before Night 1. Entry: Night 1, in the initial register.

**Campaign references:** [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#B5c. The Blood of the Vine Tavern|Arc B, the rebellion's history]]; [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/History of Barovia#Strahd’s Return|History of Barovia, Strahd's return]].

**Future campaign relevance:** High. Doru returns as a vampire spawn in the church scene later in this arc, then can recur with Donavich in Arc O, Arc P, and the epilogue. Remember his name and relationship to Gertruda and Father Donavich.

**DM-only:** Doru survived, was turned into a vampire spawn, and was sent back to Barovia. Kolyan does not know this when the register is written. See [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/History of Barovia#Strahd’s Return|History of Barovia]].

### 2. Alanik Ray (adult)

**Register data:** Missing; presumed dead. Departed with Doru's company for Castle Ravenloft. He had lodged at the burgomaster's mansion for several weeks and joined at Doru's urging.

**Author and knowledge:** Kolyan. As burgomaster and host, he knew the scholar as Alanik Ray and knew he was lodging at the mansion. Ireena can account for his decision to join the march. The campaign does not specify the precise conversation by which Kolyan learned that detail.

**Timeline:** Arrival as a lodger: approximately sixteen weeks before the campaign present, per Doru's account. Departure: with Doru's company, roughly three months before Night 1. Entry: Night 1. The interval between arrival and departure is inconsistent across sources; see `T_ALANIK_ARRIVAL`.

**Campaign references:** [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#Lunch with the Burgomaster|Arc B, lunch with the burgomaster]]; [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#Descent to the Undercroft|Arc B, Doru's account]]; [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/History of Barovia#Strahd’s Return|History of Barovia]].

**Future campaign relevance:** Very high. Alanik Ray is Van Richten's disguise. The players can encounter Van Richten in Arc C and learn his identity and role in Doru's rebellion; he recurs throughout the campaign. Remember both names and that they refer to one person.

**DM-only:** Alanik Ray is Dr. Rudolph van Richten's disguise. Van Richten escaped the castle; Kolyan has no basis to know this and records the scholar's disappearance under the name known to him.

### 3. Escher (young adult)

**Register data:** Missing; presumed dead. Escher was the village bard and helped Doru assemble the marchers. He is named individually in the player-facing record.

**Author and knowledge:** Kolyan knew Escher as the village bard and knew he departed with Doru's company. The campaign identifies Escher's role in assembling the marchers; the ledger names him separately because his identity matters later.

**Timeline:** Joined the company at `T_MARCH`, approximately three months before Night 1. If Kolyan knew of his departure, the entry belongs with the other march losses, entered Night 1.

**Campaign references:** [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#Descent to the Undercroft|Arc B, Doru's account]]; [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/History of Barovia#Strahd’s Return|History of Barovia]].

**Future campaign relevance:** High. Escher is a recurring member of Strahd's court, appearing in Arc C and later arcs including O, P, and T. His past as a Barovian bard and his relationship with Doru matter to those scenes and the epilogue.

**DM-only:** Strahd spared Escher and turned him into a vampire spawn. This is not information Kolyan has when the register is written.

### 4. Other members of Doru's company (mostly young adults)

**Register data:** Other members of Doru's company, mostly young villagers. Missing; presumed dead. Departed with Doru, Alanik, and Escher; none returned and no bodies were found.

**Author and knowledge:** The departure was public; the campaign gives an approximate group size but no exact roster or counting convention.

**Timeline:** Departed approximately three months before Night 1. Entry: Night 1.

**Campaign references:** [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/History of Barovia#Strahd’s Return|History of Barovia]]; [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/Lore of Barovia#Barovians|Lore of Barovia, Barovians]].

**Continuity note:** The wording “about two dozen villagers” may describe the whole band, including Doru and Escher, or the number gathered besides them. The campaign does not establish an exact roster or counting convention. Do not read the entry as two dozen additional people on top of every named participant.

### 5. Emeric (older adult)

**Register data:** Departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed. Older villager with graying hair. Left west along the Old Svalich Road with the first group after Rahadin's proclamation.

**Author and knowledge:** Kolyan. His departure was witnessed by the burgomaster. Emeric's later appearance and role at the refugee camp are established in Arc H, but are not knowledge Kolyan has at entry time.

**Timeline:** Departure: after Rahadin's warning, about three months before Night 1. Entry: Night 1.

**Campaign references:** [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/History of Barovia#Strahd’s Return|History of Barovia, refugee departure]]; [[Act II - The Shadowed Town/Arc H - The Lost Soul#H3a. Inside the Camp|Arc H, Emeric at the camp]].

**Future campaign relevance:** Moderate. Emeric reappears as a refugee-camp contact in Arc H and helps introduce the Barovian refugees' situation. Remember him through the Vallaki refugee storyline; no major later personal arc is established.

### 6. Magda (young adult)

**Register data:** Departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed. A young villager who left with Emeric's party.

**Author and knowledge:** Kolyan. Her departure with the group is known at the time. Her later appearance at the refugee camp is a later campaign confirmation, not a new loss.

**Timeline:** Departure: with Emeric, after Rahadin's warning and before Night 1. Entry: Night 1.

**Campaign references:** [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/History of Barovia#Strahd’s Return|History of Barovia, refugee departure]]; [[Act II - The Shadowed Town/Arc H - The Lost Soul#H3a. Inside the Camp|Arc H, Magda at the camp]].

**Future campaign relevance:** Moderate. Magda reappears with Emeric in the Arc H refugee camp and helps establish the refugees' situation. Remember her through that storyline; no major later personal arc is established.

### 7. Franz (adult)

**Register data:** Departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed. Widower; wife Alana died of illness before the departure. Traveled with his children and sister Nyanka.

**Author and knowledge:** Kolyan. Franz's departure and family relationship are appropriate local knowledge for the burgomaster, though the campaign does not stage a specific report to him. Franz later provides the detailed account in Arc H.

**Timeline:** Departure: with the first refugee group, before Night 1. Entry: Night 1. Alana's death predates the departure; its precise date is unspecified.

**Campaign references:** [[Act II - The Shadowed Town/Arc H - The Lost Soul#H3b. Franz’s Tent|Arc H, Franz's account]]; [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/History of Barovia#Strahd’s Return|History of Barovia, refugee departure]].

**Future campaign relevance:** High for Arc H. Franz's bargain with Morgantha drives the rescue of Fyodor and Myrtle and the confrontation with the hags. Remember his grief, his children, and Nyanka's role in caring for them.

**Scope note:** Alana is not a separate loss entry: the campaign places her illness and death before the departure, outside the siege and Doru's march.

### 8. Fyodor (child)

**Register data:** Departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed. Franz's seven-year-old son at departure; traveled with Franz, Myrtle, and Nyanka.

**Author and knowledge:** Kolyan. The family relationship and age are confirmed by Franz later. The campaign does not establish a specific report of these details to Kolyan.

**Timeline:** Departure: with Franz before Night 1. Entry: Night 1.

**Campaign references:** [[Act II - The Shadowed Town/Arc H - The Lost Soul#H3b. Franz’s Tent|Arc H, Franz's account]]; [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/History of Barovia#Strahd’s Return|History of Barovia, refugee departure]].

### 9. Myrtle (child)

**Timeline:** Departure: with Franz before Night 1. Entry: Night 1.

**Campaign references:** [[Act II - The Shadowed Town/Arc H - The Lost Soul#H3b. Franz’s Tent|Arc H, Franz's account]]; [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/History of Barovia#Strahd’s Return|History of Barovia, refugee departure]].

### 10. Nyanka (young adult)

**Register data:** Departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed. Franz's sister; traveled with him and his children.

**Author and knowledge:** Kolyan. Her relationship and departure are confirmed in Arc H. The campaign does not state exactly how Kolyan recorded the family roster.

**Timeline:** Departure: with Franz before Night 1. Entry: Night 1.

**Future campaign relevance:** Moderate for Arc H. Nyanka is Franz's sister and may care for Fyodor and Myrtle if they are rescued. Remember her as their aunt and an adult in the refugee camp.

### 11. Other members of the first refugee party (mixed ages; proposed Kolyan entry)

**Register data:** Up to eighteen other villagers. Departed for Vallaki with Emeric's party; whereabouts unconfirmed.

**Author and knowledge:** History of Barovia says no more than two dozen people fled; Arc H describes the first two-dozen refugees. Six are listed individually in the player-facing summary. Using 24 as the working maximum leaves up to 18 additional people; the actual total is approximate.

**Timeline:** Departure at `T_EXODUS`; entry on Night 1. The two people later killed by wolves are included in this group, not counted in addition to it. Their fate is not known to Kolyan or Ismark and should not change their group entry.

**Campaign references:** [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/History of Barovia#Strahd’s Return|History of Barovia, refugee departure]]; [[Act II - The Shadowed Town/Arc H - The Lost Soul#H3b. Franz’s Tent|Arc H, Franz's account]].

**Count caveat:** “Two dozen at most” permits a smaller party. Eighteen is the maximum remainder if the working total is 24, not a confirmed exact count.

**Current asset mapping:** The player-facing register includes the wolf victims within this collective departure entry rather than as a separate dead entry. DM-only: two members of the group were killed by wolves; Kolyan and Ismark know the roster but do not know which members died or that these two were among them.

### 12. Anton Konstantinovich (adult)

**Register data:** Missing. Alenka's brother. Last seen at home before retiring on Night 4; the door was undisturbed, with no blood, signs of struggle, or body. Cause unknown.

**Author and knowledge:** Kolyan. Alenka is the source. The mansion confrontation brings her and Kolyan together; her account places the disappearance “two nights ago.”

**Timeline:** Disappeared during Night 4; reported on the day after Night 6, before Night 7 begins. Kolyan can enter the record after hearing Alenka's report that day.

**Campaign references:** [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#B5d. The Burgomaster’s Mansion|Arc B, Alenka's grief]].

**Future campaign relevance:** High. Anton reappears as Volenta's masked servant in Arc O; his identity is a reveal during the castle dinner. Remember his connection to Alenka and Dezdrelda.

**Continuity note:** The campaign confirms that Anton and Dezdrelda vanished and that their bodies were not found. It does not establish that the door was undisturbed or that there was no blood or sign of struggle. Those details are additions in the register, not facts independently confirmed by the campaign.

**DM-only:** Anton is held in Castle Ravenloft by Volenta and later appears as a masked servant. Kolyan does not know this. See [[Act III - The Broken Land/Arc O - Dinner with the Devil#Volenta’s Toy|Arc O, Volenta's Toy]].

### 13. Dezdrelda Konstaninova (adult)

**Register data:** Missing. Anton's wife. Last seen at home with him before retiring on Night 4; disappeared with him without signs of forced entry or violence.

**Author and knowledge:** Kolyan. Same source and report as Anton: Alenka.

**Timeline:** Disappeared during Night 4; reported on the day after Night 6. The two entries can be written together after Alenka's report.

**Campaign references:** [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#B5d. The Burgomaster’s Mansion|Arc B, Alenka's grief]].

**Future campaign relevance:** Moderate. Arc O establishes that Volenta held Dezdrelda with Anton and later killed her. Remember her as Anton's wife and Alenka's sister-in-law; she has no established later active role.

The same continuity note applies to the door, blood, and signs-of-struggle details in Dezdrelda's entry; the campaign does not specify them.

**DM-only:** Her captivity with Anton is not known to the keeper. Arc O identifies Anton's later fate but does not establish Dezdrelda's full fate in the referenced scene.

### 14. Marta (adult)

**Register data:** Dead. Bildrath's sister; chose to remain and defend her home with her husband and son. Killed during the Night 4 attack.

**Author and knowledge:** Ismark. Bildrath tells him of Marta's death and her choice to stay when they speak at the tavern on the day the players arrive, before Night 7.

**Timeline:** Marta dies during Night 4. Bildrath reports her death to Ismark before Night 7.

**Campaign references:** [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#Bildrath's Fury|Arc B, Bildrath's account]]; [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/Lore of Barovia#Barovians|Lore of Barovia, siege casualties]].

**Future campaign relevance:** Low beyond Arc B. Marta's death motivates Bildrath's grief and Parriwimple's orphanhood.

### 15. Dragomir (adult)

**Register data:** Dead. Marta's husband and Parriwimple's father. Killed during the Night 4 attack. Parriwimple survived and is in Bildrath's care.

**Author and knowledge:** Ismark learns of Dragomir's death from Bildrath in the same tavern account before Night 7.

**Future campaign relevance:** Low beyond Arc B. Dragomir's death contributes to Bildrath's grief and Parriwimple's orphanhood.

### 16. Kolyan Indirovich (older adult)

**Register data:** Dead. Last seen alive at the mansion on Night 7. Found in the first-floor parlor with a fatal clean cut to the chest. Ireena was unconscious nearby with two puncture wounds and recalls a tall, gaunt figure with a deep voice and red eyes. Perpetrator unconfirmed.

**Author and knowledge:** Ismark. He personally found the bodies after Rahadin offered condolences in the square. Ireena supplied the visitor and attack details after regaining consciousness. Rahadin's premature condolence is evidence of foreknowledge, not proof of identity.

**Timeline:** Death and discovery: Night 7, after the defense. Ireena's account becomes available after she wakes. Entry: after Ismark finds Kolyan and learns the relevant details, during his Night 7 continuation.

**Campaign references:** [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#Rahadin’s Proclamation|Arc B, Rahadin's condolence]]; [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#Strahd’s Aftermath|Arc B, the mansion aftermath]]; [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/Lore of Barovia#Barovians|Lore of Barovia, siege outcome]].

**Future campaign relevance:** Moderate. Kolyan's death drives Ismark and Ireena's choices in Act I; his burial, letter of introduction, and legacy shape their later relationships. Remember him through the Barovia and Vallaki arcs.

**DM-only:** Strahd killed Kolyan during his visit to the mansion. Ismark's record should retain the uncertainty available to him at the time.

### 17. Other siege dead (mixed ages)

**Register data:** Barovian villagers and defenders killed at barricades, in homes, and in the streets during the siege; dozens by rough count, exact total unresolved.

**Author and knowledge:** Ismark. He fought at the western barricade and saw the bodies after it fell. The campaign supports a rough estimate of dozens, not a final count.

**Timeline:** Deaths: across Nights 1–7. Entry: after the Night 7 fighting and Kolyan's death, when Ismark takes stock of the losses. Additional named losses may be added as they are confirmed.

**Campaign references:** [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#Ismark’s Last Stand|Arc B, the western barricade]]; [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/Lore of Barovia#Barovians|Lore of Barovia, siege casualties]].

### 18. Gertruda (young adult; Ismark entry)

**Register data:** Missing. Mary's twenty-one-year-old daughter and Doru's betrothed. Last seen at home on Night 6; gone by dawn after Night 6, as was the family horse Nori. Mary believes she went to Vallaki for aid.

**Author and knowledge:** Ismark. Mary reports Gertruda's absence to him on the morning after Night 6. He sends a search party that day. Kolyan is not told before his death. On the morning after Night 7, Ismark debriefs the scouts, learns they found no trace of Gertruda, and records her as missing.

**Timeline:** Last seen Night 6; absence discovered and reported at dawn after Night 6; search sent that day; entry written after the scouts return unsuccessful on the morning after Night 7.

**Campaign references:** [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#Mary's News|Arc B, Mary's news and the search party]]; [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#B5j. **Future campaign relevance:** Very high. Gertruda is a prisoner in Castle Ravenloft, central to Arc O's Doru storyline and a rescue objective in Arc P. Her survival and reunion with Doru also matter in the epilogue. Remember her relationship to Mary, Doru, and Ireena.

**DM-only:** Strahd intercepted Gertruda and took her to Castle Ravenloft. Ismark does not know this when he writes the entry.

### 19. Lansten family (mixed ages; Ismark entry)

**Register data:** Five villagers: two parents and three children (one girl and two boys). Missing during the siege.

**Author and knowledge:** Ismark knows the family as Barovia residents. After debriefing the scouts about Gertruda, he redirects them to search for the Lansten family. Arc B establishes the debrief; Arc C shows Kereza and Korga searching for the Lanstens later that morning.

**Timeline:** The family goes missing during a zombie attack on Night 7; exact location unknown. Ismark enters them as missing on the morning after Night 7, when he redirects the scouts to search for them. This is before the players meet the scouts on the road.

**Campaign references:** [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia#B5j. Lugdana's Knoll|Arc B, Ismark debriefing and redirecting the scouts]]; [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc C - Into the Valley#C1a. The Barovian Scouts|Arc C, Kereza and Korga searching for the Lansten family]].

**Future campaign relevance:** Brief but useful. The five family members are encountered as zombies near Van Richten's cache in Arc C. Remember that the group has five members and that their later fate is confirmed there.

**Continuity bridge:** Arc B says the scouts return after an unsuccessful search for Gertruda; Arc C later shows Kereza and Korga searching for the Lanstens. The intervening redirection by Ismark is the continuity established for this record.

**DM-only:** The five family members are later encountered as zombies near Van Richten's cache. See [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc C - Into the Valley#C1b. Van Richten’s Cache|Arc C, Van Richten's cache]].

## Campaign Facts Not Yet Represented

These are candidates for a register update, not facts to insert without resolving the keeper's knowledge and the record's closing time.

### Elric Martikov (young adult)

Elric joined Doru's march and died at Castle Ravenloft. He was a Martikov and a resident of the winery, not an established resident of the village of Barovia. The campaign names him, but does not establish that Kolyan knew of his participation in the march or his death. He is therefore not a confirmed omission from a village register; keep the information DM-only unless the scope is expanded and a source reaches the keeper.

References: [[Act III - The Broken Land/Arc J - The Stolen Gem|Arc J, Elric and the Martikov family]]; [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/Lore of Barovia#Keepers of the Feather|Lore of Barovia, Keepers of the Feather]].

**Future campaign relevance:** Moderate for Martikov and Keeper storylines. Elric's death informs Muriel's grief and the Martikov family conflict in Arc J. Remember the name if those arcs are active, but do not add him as a confirmed village resident in this register.

### Refugees killed after leaving (age unknown)

The two wolf victims in Arc C are members of `T_EXODUS`. The keepers do not learn that two members of the party died, so the collective entry remains “departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed”; do not give them a separate dead entry. Their deaths could have occurred on the outward journey or after they turned back toward Barovia; the keepers do not know which. Lore of Barovia says some refugees headed back and were never seen again. Do not alter recorded statuses on that basis.

References: [[Act I - Into the Mists/Arc C - Into the Valley#The Graves|Arc C, the graves]]; [[Chapter 2 - The Land of Barovia/Lore of Barovia#Barovian Refugees|Lore of Barovia, refugee losses]].

## Recommended Player-Asset Reconciliation

1. Keep Alanik recorded under that name; his identity as Van Richten is DM-only.
2. Use a working total of up to 24 refugees for `T_EXODUS`: six people listed individually and up to 18 additional villagers. The two wolf victims are included in this group and do not receive separate entries.
3. Keep the Lansten family as an Ismark entry written after he debriefs the Gertruda scouts and redirects them to that search on the morning after Night 7.
4. Keep Gertruda as an Ismark entry written after the scouts return without finding her; Kolyan did not learn of her disappearance.
5. Record Marta and Dragomir as killed during Night 4, as established for this version of the timeline.
6. Keep the register open for later deaths, disappearances, and reports. Do not change refugee entries based on later disappearances that the keepers never learn about.
7. Do not add Elric as a confirmed resident of the village of Barovia.
