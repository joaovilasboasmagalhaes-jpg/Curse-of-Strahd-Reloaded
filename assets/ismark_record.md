Record of the lost and missing — Village of Barovia
Kept by Kolyan Indirovich

Dating convention: the first night of the siege is Night 1. Each night runs from dusk to the following dawn.

---

**Doru**, son of Father Donavich; young man of the village, and Mary's daughter's betrothed
status: missing; presumed dead
last seen: roughly three months before Night 1, departing in daylight from the eastern edge of the village for Castle Ravenloft. Departure witnessed by the burgomaster and many villagers.
how lost: Led a company of about two dozen villagers against the castle. None returned; no bodies were recovered and no word has been received.

**Alanik Ray**, adult visiting scholar and lodger at the burgomaster's mansion
status: missing; presumed dead
last seen: the same day as Doru, departing for Castle Ravenloft with Doru's company.
how lost: He had lodged at the mansion for several weeks to study Barovian history and ecology, and joined the march at Doru's urging. No one from the company returned.

**Escher**, young adult village bard and Doru's oldest friend
status: missing; presumed dead
last seen: departing with Doru's company for Castle Ravenloft, roughly three months before Night 1.
how lost: Helped Doru assemble the marchers and joined the company. None returned; no bodies were recovered and no word has been received.

**Other members of Doru's company**, mostly young adults
status: missing; presumed dead
last seen: departing with Doru and Alanik for Castle Ravenloft, roughly three months before Night 1.
how lost: None returned and no bodies were found. The company numbered about two dozen; the precise count is uncertain.

---

**Emeric**, an older adult villager with graying hair
status: departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: after Rahadin's proclamation, roughly three months before Night 1, departing west along the Old Svalich Road with the first group bound for Vallaki. Departure witnessed by the burgomaster.
how lost: No word received since departure. It has not been established whether the two travelers later reported killed by wolves belonged to this party.

**Magda**, a young adult villager
status: departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: with Emeric's party, on the same road and day.
how lost: No word received since departure.

**Franz**, an adult widower; wife Alana died of illness before his departure
status: departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: with Emeric's party, traveling with his two children and his sister Nyanka.
how lost: No word received since departure. Alana's death preceded the departure and was unrelated to the siege.

**Fyodor**, Franz's son and a child at the time of departure
status: departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: with his father, sister Myrtle, and aunt Nyanka in Emeric's party.
how lost: No word received since departure.

**Myrtle**, Franz's daughter and a child at the time of departure
status: departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: with her father, brother Fyodor, and aunt Nyanka in Emeric's party.
how lost: No word received since departure.

**Nyanka**, Franz's young adult sister
status: departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: with Franz and his children in Emeric's party.
how lost: No word received since departure.

**Other members of Emeric's party**, up to eighteen villagers of mixed ages
status: departed for Vallaki; whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: departing west along the Old Svalich Road with Emeric's party, after Rahadin's proclamation and roughly three months before Night 1.
how lost: No word received since departure. The party numbered no more than two dozen; six members are listed above. The exact total is approximate.

---

**Anton Konstantinovich**, Alenka Konstantinova's brother
status: missing
last seen: at home before retiring on Night 4, according to Alenka.
how lost: Alenka reported him missing on the morning after Night 4. The door was undisturbed; no blood, signs of struggle, or body were found. Cause and manner of disappearance unknown.

**Dezdrelda Konstaninova**, Anton's wife
status: missing
last seen: at home with Anton before retiring on Night 4, according to Alenka.
how lost: Reported missing with her husband on the morning after Night 4. The door was undisturbed; no blood, signs of struggle, or body were found. Cause and manner of disappearance unknown.

**Marta**, adult sister of Bildrath Cantemir
status: dead
last seen: at home with her husband and son on Night 4.
how lost: According to Bildrath, she chose to stay and defend her home. Killed during the Night 4 attacks.

**Dragomir**, adult husband of Marta; father of Parriwimple
status: dead
last seen: at home with Marta and their son on Night 4.
how lost: Killed during the Night 4 attacks. Parriwimple survived and is in Bildrath's care.

---

**Kolyan Indirovich**, older adult burgomaster of Barovia; father of Ismark Kolyanovich
status: dead
last seen alive: Night 7, at the mansion. Ireena had returned for crossbow bolts and heard him invite a visitor inside before her memory of the night failed.
how lost: Found dead in the first-floor parlor after Rahadin offered condolences to Ismark in the town square, before Kolyan's death had been reported to him. Kolyan bore one long, clean cut across the chest. Ireena was found unconscious in the same room, alive, with two puncture wounds at her throat. Her account describes a tall, gaunt figure with a deep voice and red eyes. Cause of death: fatal chest wound. Perpetrator unidentified; Strahd is suspected by Ismark, unconfirmed.

**Other villagers and defenders killed in the siege**, mixed ages, dozens by rough count
status: dead
last seen: at the barricades, in their homes, and in the streets over the course of the siege.
how lost: Killed in the attacks. Many bodies were visible when the western barricade fell. The exact total remains a rough count.

The undead withdrew after Kolyan's death. The siege ended on Night 7.

---

**Gertruda**, Mary's young adult daughter and Doru's betrothed
status: missing
last seen: at home with her mother on Night 6, before they retired, according to Mary. Mary found her gone at dawn after Night 6; the family horse, Nori, was also missing from its stall.
how lost: Mary believed Gertruda had set out alone for Vallaki to seek aid from its burgomaster. Ismark sent scouts to search the western road on the morning after Night 6. They returned without finding her on the morning after Night 7; Ismark recorded her as missing then.

**Lansten family**, mixed ages: two adults and three children (one girl and two boys)
status: missing
last seen: during a zombie attack on Night 7; exact location not recorded.
how lost: Their disappearance was reported to Ismark during the scouts' debriefing on the morning after Night 7. He directed the scouts to search for them. Their fate is unknown.
