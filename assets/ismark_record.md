Record of the lost and missing — village of Barovia
kept by I. Kolyanovich

dates below counted from the night our own people first laid blame on me for this — call it Night 1. all else reckoned before or after that mark.

---

Doru, son of Father Donavich
status: missing
last seen: roughly three months before Night 1. departed the village on the Old Svalich Road, headed for Castle Ravenloft, in daylight. seen off by much of the village at the eastern edge of town, self included.
how lost: led ~24 volunteers against the castle. none returned. no bodies recovered, no word since. presumed dead.

Alanik Ray, foreign scholar, lodger at the mansion
status: missing
last seen: same day as Doru, departed with the same party, same road.
how lost: guest of the family several weeks prior study of local history. accompanied Doru's party at Doru's urging, by Ireena's account. no word since. presumed dead.

unnamed volunteers (~24), villagers, mostly young, names not fully recorded
status: missing
last seen: same day as Doru, same road, same party.
how lost: see Doru, above. full list to be reconstructed from memory or others' account — ask Bildrath Cantemir, who knew many of them.

Emeric, villager, older, no trade recorded
status: departed for Vallaki, whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: shortly after Rahadin's proclamation, roughly three months before Night 1, departing the village on the Old Svalich Road westward, among the first ~24 to leave. seen off at the western edge of town, self included.
note: no word since. some among this party may be counted among the two dead found and buried along that road — see below.

Magda, villager, younger, no trade recorded
status: departed for Vallaki, whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: same day as Emeric, same party, same road.
note: no word since.

Franz, villager, widower — wife Alana died of illness before this, unrelated to the castle or the siege
status: departed for Vallaki, whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: same day as Emeric, same party, same road. took his two children with him.
note: no word since.

Fyodor, son to Franz, seven years at time of departure
status: departed for Vallaki, whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: same day as Emeric, traveling with his father Franz and his aunt Nyanka.
note: no word since.

Myrtle, daughter to Franz, five years at time of departure
status: departed for Vallaki, whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: same day as Emeric, traveling with her father Franz and her aunt Nyanka.
note: no word since.

Nyanka, sister to Franz
status: departed for Vallaki, whereabouts unconfirmed
last seen: same day as Emeric, same party, traveling with Franz and his children.
note: no word since.

two unnamed, of the party that departed for Vallaki
status: dead
last seen: somewhere on the Old Svalich Road, west of the village, sometime in the weeks after their departure.
how lost: killed by wolves along the road. graves dug and marked by the rest of their party, who continued on without them. names not brought back to me by whoever carried the news — will ask any trader or hunter who passes through.

Marta, sister to Bildrath Cantemir
status: dead
last seen: during the six-night siege, night not recorded.
note: stayed to defend home w/ husband + son per own choice.

Dragomir, husband to Marta
status: dead
last seen: during the six-night siege, night not recorded.
note: see Marta, above. son Parriwimple survives, in Bildrath's care.

Gertruda, daughter to Mary, betrothed to Doru
status: missing
last seen: night of Night 4 into Night 5, at her mother's house in the village, by her mother Mary — last spoke with her before retiring. gone by morning of Night 5. the family horse, Nori, gone from its stall the same morning.
note: believed to have set out for Vallaki alone, unescorted, on foot or by Nori, seeking aid from its Baron. no one saw her leave. search party of local hunters and trappers sent out Night 5 to look for her along the western road; no word returned as of this writing.

Anton Konstantinovich, brother to Alenka Konstantinova
status: missing
last seen: night of Night 3 into Night 4, at his home in the village, presumably asleep. no one saw him go.
note: vanished in the night alongside his wife. no blood, no sign of struggle, no body. door found undisturbed. Alenka can say more of their house that night, if asked gently.

Dezdrelda Konstaninova, wife to Anton Konstantinovich
status: missing
last seen: same night as Anton, same house.
note: see Anton, above.

unrecorded dead of the siege, villagers and defenders, names not yet gathered
status: dead
last seen: across the six nights of the siege, at the barricades or in their homes, throughout the village.
note: dozens by rough count, exact number and names not fixed. must go door to door once the burying is done. will amend this record as each name is confirmed.

Kolyan Indirovich, burgomaster, my father
status: dead
last seen alive: Night 5, at the mansion, by Ireena — she returned home for crossbow bolts and heard him inviting some visitor in at the front door before her memory of that night goes dark.
how lost: found by myself and others in the first-floor parlor of our home, Night 5, a single long, clean cut across the chest, already dead by the time we reached him. Ireena found in the same room, unconscious, two puncture wounds at the throat, alive. she recalls a tall, gaunt shape, a deep voice, red eyes — no name, but I have my suspicion, and so does the village. Rahadin passed through the town square that same night and, on departing, offered me his condolences before I had told anyone my father was dead. make of that what you will.
