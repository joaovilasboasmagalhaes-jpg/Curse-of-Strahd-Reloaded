Bhodi, Meind, Mira and Bob the old boy were with Grim this  time around.

Bhodi had always been their leader, and again he'd found a complicated job, but this time the reward was quite the catch.

A small, youngling dragon had made a den of the underground Lake near Suniva, and the local government simply didn't have the means to handle it. Its name is Threighr, and the chromatic black youngling had grown to love attacking the main trade route of the town, stashing away all magical items it could in the lake.

The deal was simple, the dragon dies and we keep whatever we can take from the lake.


The plan? Not quite so simple. The underground Lake is mostly inaccessible, as there are many floods within the cave system, caused by Threighr to protect it.

First, Bob used one of his old connections to find a magical amulet that could store people inside, with everyone getting into it besides Grim and Bhodi.

Second, the pair had to "buy" the service of some known local thieves whose heads were wanted, dead or alive. With a twist of Grim's silver tongue, and Bhodi's magical rune armor as leverage, they convinced them to give them passage on a caravan, which had "camouflage magical runes" woven to escape Threighr's notice.

Third, Grim and Bhodi had to actually get into the amulet and manage to get inside before Threighr killed them, but also after the confusion had begun.

By a stroke of luck (or as Grim knew, the stroke of her lyre), it somehow actually went to plan, and they all managed to sneak inside the den.

The assault was simple, in theory: hit it hard when it falls asleep. The fuck up is that it didn't go to sleep, and the air inside the amulet was quickly running out.

Thus, a long, hard fight against the youngling and its acid breath took place. Had Meind not been there, Bhodi's sword arm could have melted away, and Bob's robes were all but a ruin from all the fire he was flinging around.

Mira took the final blow, her arrow finding its mark neatly through the scales Grim and Bhodi had torn apart.

The payoff was the single best the group had had yet this far, this is the job that gave Grim the bulk of her money to buy a nice house at the edge of town with Will, and help him set up his new forge there.

Not only did they get the items from the Lake, but also the bounty on the thieves at the road.

Getting out was much simpler than getting in. Now that Threighr's not around his lair to patrol it, Mira showed her little secret since nobody else is around. Although she passes as a human, in reality she was born a Water Genasi, so after the amulet's air was cleared again, she swam out with everyone in tow, the other loot safely stashed in the party's bag of holding.
