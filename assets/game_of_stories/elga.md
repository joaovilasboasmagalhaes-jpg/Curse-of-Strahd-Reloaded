# A torn page from Elga’s Journal

*The page of the journal has already yellowed, and the ink from the quill seems to be losing its strength. Elga still remembers when she wrote this, when she was just 14. She smiles as she reviews the page.*

Today was such a great day! I swear that the monastery should get visitors more often, they always have such interesting stories to tell, and we never get to go outside... The priests say that we will have enough time to explore the world once we become proper Doom Guides.

Today, Nechrobane Volahk came, as a guest, to give us a lecture on undeath and how to destroy them, I got to see a zombie for the first time!! It was such a weird experience... I never expected them to be slimy. I think he wanted us to be scared, but all of us were just curious on where he had fetched it. He laughed and told us that we shouldn’t worry about it. Then he went on and on on how zombies don’t naturally occur in environments and if we ever came across them that we should seek the source, it might be a necromancer, or an evil artifact, or something else, but that we should collect it or destroy it. Pretty standard stuff, but it’s cool to meet someone who has actually been doing it, instead of just reading it in old books.
